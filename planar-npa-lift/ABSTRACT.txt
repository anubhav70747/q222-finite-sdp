The quantum region of the CHSH value and the sum of two marginal averages is not recovered by any finite level of the Navascués–Pironio–Acín hierarchy. We give an exact finite semidefinite lift of this same region. The proof reduces every supporting inequality to equal measurement overlaps. At fixed product of the overlap parameters, the determinant is affine in their sum. In the nonclassical range the coefficient is nonnegative, so equal overlaps minimize it; the remaining bounds are local. The resulting univariate quartic matrix depends affinely on the supporting inequality and gives an explicit lift with blocks of orders five and four. The region has semidefinite extension degree two, the smallest possible for a nonpolyhedral set. Thus the family that requires unbounded NPA depth has a finite representation of minimal semidefinite block order.
