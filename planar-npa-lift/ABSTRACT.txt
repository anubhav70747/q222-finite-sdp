The Navascués–Pironio–Acín hierarchy approaches quantum correlations through increasingly large semidefinite programs. We construct a quantum region that admits one fixed semidefinite lift although every finite hierarchy level fails to describe it. The region records the CHSH value and the sum of two marginal averages, so its supporting lines include the symmetric doubly tilted CHSH family. Equal measurement overlaps suffice for every supporting inequality: a determinant comparison handles the nonclassical directions, and the remaining bounds are local. The resulting univariate quartic matrix depends affinely on the inequality coefficients and yields an explicit lift with blocks of orders five and four. Independently varying the two measurement angles produces a shifted Motzkin polynomial that obstructs every fixed NPA level near the local threshold. The region has semidefinite extension degree two, the smallest possible for a nonpolyhedral set. Physical relabellings give thirty-two exact projections that can be added to any NPA relaxation.
