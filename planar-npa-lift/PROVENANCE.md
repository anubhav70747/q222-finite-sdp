# Source provenance

The new article and note develop the complete common-tilt planar lift
from the six-page analytical extension prepared on 27 October 2026.
All its support signs, boundary cases, explicit dual matrices,
nonpolyhedrality proof and signed asymmetric branches are retained in
Part I. Section F5 adds the direct physical proof for all 32 projections.

The original finite-lift proof, matrices and delivery archives are
[preserved separately](https://github.com/anubhav70747/q222-finite-sdp/tree/main/extracted-lifts/common-tilt-planar).

Part II preserves Sections S1–S10 of the existing September 2026 PRL
manuscript's supplement exactly, together with its locally uniform
remainder convention. The earlier discussion of open finite-lift
questions is not copied as a statement about this newly resolved
projection. The original source files remain unchanged.

Scientific inputs are cited in the manuscripts: Hanselka–Sinn,
Corollary 2.8 (including singular PSD matrix polynomials);
Scheiderer, Theorem 0.1 (closed convex semialgebraic subsets of the plane);
and Gigena et al. (2025), for the symmetric tilted quantum bound.
The source studies of parallel repetition and BoundingContextuality
informed exposition only and are not redistributed here.
