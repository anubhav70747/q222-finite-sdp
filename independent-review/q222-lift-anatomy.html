How the Q222 finite-lift proof is built, and how far the lift can be extracted

The repo proves that the quantum correlation set of the simplest Bell scenario (two parties, two binary measurements each, eight coordinates) is the projection of one fixed spectrahedron. This page breaks the proof into its stages, states the idea that organizes it, records what was checked and what was found, and follows the explicit lift as far as it currently goes.

Verdict

existence proof Sound wherever it was tested. Every core lemma held under adversarial review, and the key determinant identity was re-derived symbolically. The 31,000-line appendix audit found no mathematical gap, only editorial defects.
sketched steps Every sketched step holds when checked. The thin-layer reuse of C1 certificates on a negative-β source holds, R10's contact chart is fully covered, and the HI pivots have a positive-definite residue.
explicit lift No exact pencil for all eight coordinates exists yet. The job reduces exactly to one 9-dimensional cone. Every family tried so far is provably too small. The best full-set pencils (orders 264 and 392) are strictly larger than Q222.

The organizing insight

A finite lift needs positivity that ignores how fast things degenerate. Q222 only ever needs two certificates with that property, AM–GM on monomials and Gram matrices with a strictly positive real part. Its geometry turns each Bell contact into an AM–GM identity.

Why rates matter. Scheiderer's criterion (2021, Cor. 3.19) says a closed convex semialgebraic set has a finite lift exactly when every valid slack, evaluated at every point, is a sum of squares in K⊗K for every real closed field K ⊇ ℝ. The paper uses two separately labelled fields: K_c for the Bell inequality and K_t for the experiment. Their infinitesimals are the inequality and the measurement angles collapsing at independent, unrelated rates. A certificate may take roots and inverses inside one field only, never of a mixed source–test quantity.

Two rate-blind engines. (1) Strict real residue: a Gram matrix with bounded entries whose standard-part matrix is positive definite is a tensor sum of squares, whatever its infinitesimal parts do (Lemma TF2, via a + x⊗y = ½[(a/M+x)⊗(M+y) + (a/M−x)⊗(M−y)]). (2) Monomial AM–GM: a monomial X = U_c⁻¹ ⊗ U is a pure tensor, so its cube and fourth roots split cleanly across the two fields.

Why Q222 fits. Each nonlocal slack has exactly one negative square, the CHSH core −2d·D_u·D_v·Z², and three positive transverse directions. So the 4×4 state matrix is positive semidefinite exactly when its determinant is nonnegative. That determinant is a Laurent polynomial in (u², v²) supported on the 3×3 exponent square {−1,0,1}², which has a single interior point. The eight boundary coefficients are products of event weights and are never negative, so only the centre can be. At a stationary contact the weighted boundary terms balance around the origin, split into 12 small circuits, and each circuit is an exact AM–GM sum of squares.

Where the work is. AM–GM certifies the determinant, not the full quadratic form. Recovering the form needs a Schur complement, whose inverse is a mixed quantity and therefore forbidden. The appendices replace it chart by chart. They keep event squares whole, write contact factors in the circuit roots with division-free "conductor" identities, and rescale until the leftover Gram has a strictly positive residue, at which point engine (1) applies. That accounts for most of the 343-page companion.

The finite lift, end to end

The existence proof gives no matrices. Scheiderer's criterion yields a lift through compactness, so the finite space of testing functions it implicitly uses is never named. The repo instead builds an exact route that reduces the whole problem to one 9-dimensional cone. Every step of that route is proved and machine-checked except one: an exact pencil for the cone itself.

Step 1 · Canonical reduction (exact)

Write the parent in U = u² with W = S + U·P₀, X = T + U·Q₀, Y = u(S−P₀), Z = u(T−Q₀). Then F_c = D_u·Φ_c, and for fixed source c and Bob angle v, positivity for all U ≥ 0 holds exactly when some 2×2 matrix J gives

G_J = [ P+K J ] ⪰ 0 H_J = [ P+E K−E−J ] ⪰ 0 [ Jᵀ N+K ] [ K−E−Jᵀ N+E ]

The forward direction is the split identity; the converse uses the matrix Fejér–Riesz theorem. canonical_completion.tex:98–163

Step 2 · Full-polar assembly (exact)

With the eight output relabellings R_g, g ∈ {±1}³, and the invertible 9×9 map M (det M = −32), an order-n canonical pencil with a strictly feasible point gives

Q222 = { q : ∃ Y_g ⪰ 0, ℬ*Y_g = 0, 𝒜*Y_g = M_gᵀ(1, q), g ∈ {±1}³ }

q enters affinely, only in 72 scalar blocks. Other orientations cannot repair a missing canonical certificate, so the cone must be exact. full_polar_assembly.tex:137–225

Exact pieces available now

  • Tilted-CHSH projection, exact with no auxiliaries. With t = a₀ and s = E₀₀+E₀₁+E₁₀−E₁₁:
    [ √2+t s/2 ] [ s/2 √2−t ] ⊕ (1−t) ⊕ (1+t) ⪰ 0
    Checked here at 417k grid points and 300k random quantum behaviours.
  • Size bound: any lift of Q222 has total PSD order at least 9. The first undecided template, N = 9, already has 2,025 unknowns.
  • Local pencils of orders 62, 669 and 731 for specified source boxes, plus an exact order-49 pencil for the angular Laurent cone. None of these is Q222.
  • New since the snapshot, not audited here. A fixed certificate of order 283 for the stationary R1 family on |u| ≤ 9/256, |v| ≤ 1/16, joined to order 293 with a Bob complement. An order-635 construction valid at all measurements for the native C2 source envelope. The repo itself states that global canonical coverage remains open.

What would finish the extraction

  • Quantifier elimination. It terminates in principle, since the theorem guarantees some size works, but at N = 9 it is far beyond practical reach.
  • Next concrete step. Build C_ram + πC_ram (order 98, 784 after assembly), which restores the Alice↔Bob symmetry the failed families lack, and run a separation search against C_can. Aim it at multiscale sources and the C1/C2 contact charts, using λ_min of the canonical state matrix as the oracle.
  • Testing space from the proof. In parallel, a Newton–Puiseux analysis of the contact selectors on those charts could predict a finite exponent set. That set would fix a joint testing space in u, v and ψ, which is exactly the object the existence proof never names.

What was checked, and how

  • Determinant identity: exact division and nonnegativity of all eight outer coefficients, symbolically for all weights (sympy), and with exact rationals in Ruby.
  • Core lemmas: the Scheiderer criterion (against the arXiv text), the strict Gram lemma, balanced AM–GM with the circuit list, and the assembly steps were each checked line by line. All held.
  • Appendix audit: R10, R8, R6 (with the missing matrices rebuilt), and the C1 and C2 loose ends, with key identities recomputed exactly. All are closed in substance.
  • Sketched steps: the thin-layer import, R10 coverage and the HI pivots were each re-derived. All hold.
  • Reproducible checks: the exact scripts behind these results are in independent-review/checks/ on the independent-review branch.
  • Repo verifiers: every cheap checker passes on Python 3.9 with sympy 1.14, three of them with a small compatibility shim. These include the size bound, the outer data, regular C2 local (111,823 assertions), local-order669, global-weighted, inverse-Alice, c1d-local, fixed-features, the C160 event Grams, the canonical-completion checkers, and the ramified obstruction. These confirm the author's algebra, not any reverse inclusion.
  • Not covered: a line-by-line re-derivation of all ~31k appendix lines, and the noncommuting-residue theorem (Thm 6.2) in detail.
