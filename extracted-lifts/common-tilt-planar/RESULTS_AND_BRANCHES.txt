Organizing insights
--------------------
I. A deterministic bound can make three characteristic coefficients
   positive. Then one determinant controls the entire state optimization.
II. At fixed angle product, the determinant's only varying term is a
    two-term weighted arithmetic-geometric mean. This removes one angle.
III. Univariate matrix positivity gives bounded-degree squares. Whether
     the resulting lift is common-affine depends on how the reduced
     matrix depends on the visible Bell coefficients.
IV. An explicit positive Gram anchor turns support certificates into
    an attained behavior membership lift by conic duality.
V. Contact-complement positivity is a different mechanism: it propagates
   a fixed certificate space to nearby sources. Local propagation needs
   a further argument before it supplies a whole-domain cover.
