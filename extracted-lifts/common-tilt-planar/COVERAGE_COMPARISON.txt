Covering the relabelled hard directions — 27 October 2026

New proved consequence
-----------------------
Let chi=(chi00,chi01,chi10,chi11) have entries +/-1 and product -1.
There are8 such CHSH orientations. For each chosen pair x,y in{0,1}, set

  M_chi,xy = <Ax> + chi_xy <By>,
  S_chi = sum_ij chi_ij <Ai Bj>.

Each of the32 maps pi_chi,xy=(M_chi,xy,S_chi) maps the quantum behavior
set exactly onto the planar W from proof/main.tex. This follows by a
physical input/output relabeling; the condition product(chi)=-1 is precisely
what permits the four correlator signs to be matched. Flipping all outputs
changes M to -M and leaves S unchanged, so its duplicate constraints can
be removed. An independent enumeration of all128 physical actions gives
exactly these32 maps, all with distinct unoriented coefficient planes.

For any fixed standard NPA behavior relaxation Q_L, define

  R_L = Q_L intersect [intersection over chi,x,y of pi_chi,xy^-1(W)].

This is one finite SDP. The added constraints are fully evaluated in
ALL32_EXACT_PROJECTION_CONSTRAINTS.json:64 PSD blocks of orders5 and4,
total order288,512 auxiliary scalars and96 affine equalities. These are
additional to the chosen NPA level, whose existing moment block is retained.

For every h,a and every listed projection, optimization over R_L is EXACT:

  sup_{p in R_L} [h S_chi(p)+a M_chi,xy(p)]
    = sup_{p quantum} [h S_chi(p)+a M_chi,xy(p)].

Proof. Q is contained in R_L because the32 projected constraints are
quantum-valid. Conversely R_L is contained in each inverse image of W,
so its support in that plane is at most the quantum support of W.
The quantum inclusion gives the opposite inequality. This proves equality.
The previously established NPA nontermination theorem gives, for every
fixed L, some near-critical symmetric tilt where Q_L's support is strictly
larger. Hence R_L is a strict strengthening of every fixed Q_L. This is a
symbolic theorem, not a numerical comparison or a claim that R_L equals Q.

The same construction can be intersected with existing finite outer
pencils and all globally sound C1/C2/closed-face constraints. Every such
intersection remains quantum-valid and retains exact support on these32
planes. Enforcing several exact projections does not ensure their state
and measurement realizations can all be chosen jointly.

Comparison with existing work
------------------------------
Single-marginal tilted projection:
  Exact two-coordinate quantum image (a0,CHSH), with a small explicit
  second-order-cone representation. Already in extracted-lifts/README.md.
  Its finite-NPA-exact regime is a useful baseline.

New common-tilt projection:
  Exact full two-coordinate image (a0+b0,CHSH), all support directions.
  It contains the symmetric near-critical NPA-hard directions and now
  supplies the32-copy construction above. Minimum largest block order2
  is proved; evaluated membership blocks are5 and4.

Balanced C1 slice:
  Exact cone of Bell sources with A*alpha>=2m^2 and total PSD order5.
  Its contact-complement Grams extend to a relative open five-parameter
  source neighborhood in a fixed18-function space. This is source coverage,
  not a five-dimensional behavior projection. Its generic coefficients
  include three or four nonzero marginal terms, whereas a new common-tilt
  plane has exactly two equally weighted marginals. Neither domain size
  nor number of parameters establishes containment between the results.

Equal-angle C2 cone:
  Two independent contact parameters; exact source cone with two2x2 PSD
  blocks and three scalar inequalities (total order7). Its decomposition
  into one contact source and two event rays gives a different small
  certificate. It already sharpens NPA1+AB on its stated sources.

Unequal-angle C2 curve:
  Exact conic hull of one contact curve, with two3x3 moment blocks
  (total order6). The full three-parameter contact surface has an additional
  quartic relation; its convex hull is not described by imposing the
  generator equalities on mixtures.

General doubly tilted support values:
  The signed fixed-input16,12 construction covers every tilt pair.
  Its ratio rho=(4-alpha^2)/(4-beta^2) remains nonlinear in the free tilts.
  Removing that dependence in a common affine lift would yield an explicit
  three-coordinate image (a0,b0,CHSH), a substantive next extension.

Full evaluated Q222 pencil:
  Universal C1 and C2 admission into one fixed globally sound library is
  still the missing constructive input. Existing reflection, coefficient
  density and closed-face/event pieces assemble the other branches once
  that input is available. The reviewed qualitative existence theorem is
  a separate retained result.

Best next proof targets
-------------------------
1. Use the32 projections immediately as exact additions to a chosen NPA
   level, together with the existing order5 C1 and order7/order6 C2 cones.
   The current JSON is an evaluated add-on; a combined NPA solver instance
   has not been assembled or benchmarked in this comparison.
2. Seek a common affine lift of W3=(a0,b0,CHSH), preserving both independent
   tilts as visible coefficients. A source-dependent optimal ray alone is
   insufficient. This is the nearest larger observed region.
3. For direct C2 expansion, extend the unequal-angle contact curve to the
   convex hull of its three-parameter surface while retaining the quartic
   branch and all endpoint conditions. The existing order6 moment proof
   suggests the right language, but multivariate positivity is a new step.
4. For global completion, use a proved uniform strict-source construction
   or a genuine finite coverage argument for H1/H2. Neither repeated local
   neighborhoods nor finitely many exact projections prove that coverage.
