# An exact cone of unequal-angle C2 inequalities

This compact continuous family has an exact source-cone lift of total PSD
order six. Its dual gives two explicit 3-by-3 PSD blocks on the eight visible
behavior coordinates, with six unrestricted auxiliary variables. The contact
angle ratio varies over an interval. This represents the cone generated by
this curve exactly; it does not represent the full unequal-angle hull or
the full quantum set.

## The native family

Use the canonical Bell functional

$$
H=1-\frac{E_{00}-E_{01}+E_{10}+E_{11}}2,\qquad
f_c=dH+4\sum_i c_i p_i,
$$

with source order $(d,\lambda,n,m,C,A,D,\alpha,\beta)$ and event order

$$
(++|01),(+-|00),(+-|11),(-+|10),(-+|00),(-+|11),(--|01),(+-|10).
$$

Fix $r=1/4$, $s=1/8$, and let $3/4\le a\le2$. Set

$$
\begin{gathered}
g=a-\frac58,\quad L=\frac{11a+1}{32},\quad
Q=\frac{32a^2-9a+1}{32},\\
\Delta=\frac{32a^3+112a^2+23a+1}{1024},\qquad
B=\frac{32a^3-208a^2-29a+1}{1024}.
\end{gathered}
$$

The family is $d=1$, $\lambda=\beta=0$, and

$$
\begin{aligned}
n&=-\frac{aB}{2(a+1)\Delta},&
m&=\frac{(a+s)(1-r)(as+r)L}{2(a+1)g\Delta},\\
C&=\frac{r^2(a+s)Q}{2(1-r)\Delta},&
A&=\frac{a(a+r)(a+s)L}{2(a+1)\Delta},\\
D&=\frac{(a+r)(1-s)(ar+s)L}{2(a+1)g\Delta},&
\alpha&=\frac{s^2(a+r)Q}{2(1-s)\Delta}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$

These are native C2 contacts with
$b_{\rm source}=a$, $t_{\rm source}=3/(4a)$, and
$c^2=1/(2a)$. The testing angles and homogeneous contact state are

$$
u_c=\frac1{\sqrt{2a}},\qquad v_c=\sqrt{\frac a2},\qquad
\psi_c=(1,-u_c/4,u_c/4,1)^T.
$$

The state is not unit normalized. Its two-by-two coefficient matrix has
orthogonal rows of equal length, so it is maximally entangled. The ratio
$v_c/u_c=a$ varies from $3/4$ to $2$, while $u_cv_c=1/2$.

All denominator factors in (1) are positive on the closed interval. Under
$a=3/4+(5/4)t$, the degree-three Bernstein coefficients of $B$ are

$$
-\frac{497}{4096},\quad-\frac{1463}{6144},\quad
-\frac{1737}{4096},\quad-\frac{633}{1024}.
$$

Hence $B<0$ throughout the interval. All active event weights are positive.
The other native chart gaps are $a/4$, $g$, $g/a$, $7/8$, and
$7a/8$, also positive.

The complete-parent stationary-zero argument in
[Chapter 4, SG3–SG7](../../latex/chapters/04_geometry.tex) proves global
quantum validity of these sources. Its three-state principal block is
positive from the signed core. The determinant is a Laurent polynomial
with nonnegative noncentral coefficients. The contact kernel and two
angular derivatives make its value and logarithmic gradient vanish, so
convexity in the logarithmic testing coordinates makes the determinant
nonnegative everywhere. Schur complementation gives positivity for every
state. Signed tests, zero angles and projective endpoints follow from the
same complete-parent identity and continuity. The script checks the full
contact and all six first derivatives with the source parameter held fixed
during differentiation. This is not a restriction of the validity claim
to the contact state.

## The source pencil

Multiply (1) by the positive factor

$$
k(a)=2(a+1)g(1-r)(1-s)\Delta
=\frac{21(a+1)(8a-5)(32a^3+112a^2+23a+1)}{131072}.
$$

Let $v_5(a)=(1,a,a^2,a^3,a^4,a^5)^T$. In active source order
$(d,n,m,C,A,D,\alpha)$, the cleared curve is
$\widetilde c(a)=\mathsf C v_5(a)$, where

$$
\mathsf C=\frac1{262144}
\begin{pmatrix}
-210&-4704&-20286&15120&41664&10752\\
0&105&-3213&-16968&38304&-5376\\
126&2457&12285&5544&0&0\\
-35&56&1435&-9856&3136&14336\\
0&-105&-2247&-13356&-9408&59136\\
147&2499&10878&12936&0&0\\
-15&84&39&-2076&1056&3072
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{2}
$$

Put $\ell=3/4$, $R=2$. The closed cone generated by (1) is exactly

$$
\begin{gathered}
\lambda=\beta=0,\qquad
(d,n,m,C,A,D,\alpha)^T=\mathsf C y,\\
M_\ell(y)=[y_{i+j+1}-\ell y_{i+j}]_{i,j=0}^2\succeq0,\qquad
M_R(y)=[R y_{i+j}-y_{i+j+1}]_{i,j=0}^2\succeq0.
\end{gathered}
\tag{3}
$$

The six moments are unrestricted variables. Their PSD constraints enforce
their moment interpretation; no extra source-sign blocks are required.

### Both inclusions, including singular moments

A nonnegative measure on $[\ell,R]$ gives (3) by integration. Conversely,
write $H_0=[y_{i+j}]$, $H_1=[y_{i+j+1}]$. The two blocks imply

$$
H_0=\frac{M_\ell+M_R}{R-\ell}\succeq0,\qquad
\ell H_0\preceq H_1\preceq R H_0.
$$

On $V=\mathbb R^3/\ker H_0$, use the inner product
$\langle e_i,e_j\rangle=y_{i+j}$. The bounds annihilate $\ker H_0$
in $H_1$, so they define a self-adjoint operator $\mathcal L$ by
$\langle\mathcal L e_i,e_j\rangle=y_{i+j+1}$, with
$\ell I\preceq\mathcal L\preceq R I$.
The Hankel identities give $\mathcal L e_0=e_1$ and
$\mathcal L e_1=e_2$. Consequently

$$
\langle e_0,\mathcal L^j e_0\rangle=y_j\quad(0\le j\le5);
\qquad y_5=\langle e_2,\mathcal L e_2\rangle.
$$

The spectral measure of $e_0$ therefore represents all six moments, with
at most $\dim V\le3$ atoms in the interval. If $V=0$, all moments
vanish. Applying $\mathsf C$ gives the desired mixture of source rays.
This proves both inclusions without excluding singular PSD blocks.

The source image is closed. The positive increasing factors of $k$ give

$$
k(a)\ge k(3/4)=\frac{55713}{2097152}>0.
$$

For any representing measure, its total mass is at most
$2097152d/55713$. Convergent source vectors thus have bounded moment
preimages on this compact interval; a subsequence converges within (3).
Every endpoint and finite conic limit is included.

## The explicit dual pencil on behaviors

The visible coordinates are
$q=(a_0,a_1,b_0,b_1,E_{00},E_{01},E_{10},E_{11})$.
Use

$$
\begin{aligned}
4p_n&=1+a_0-b_0-E_{00},&4p_m&=1+a_1-b_1-E_{11},\\
4p_C&=1-a_1+b_0-E_{10},&4p_A&=1-a_0+b_0-E_{00},\\
4p_D&=1-a_1+b_1-E_{11},&4p_\alpha&=1-a_0-b_1+E_{01}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Define the six affine coefficients

$$
(\gamma_0,\ldots,\gamma_5)^T
=\mathsf C^T(H,4p_n,4p_m,4p_C,4p_A,4p_D,4p_\alpha)^T,
\qquad p(a)=\sum_{j=0}^5 \gamma_j a^j.
\tag{4}
$$

The polynomial is exactly $f_{\widetilde c(a)}(q)$.
Thus a behavior obeys the whole curve family
precisely when $p\ge0$ on the interval.

The following prescription is an explicit affine pencil with six real
auxiliaries $\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_6$. Write $v_2(a)=(1,a,a^2)^T$ and set

$$
G=\begin{pmatrix}g_{00}&\xi_1&\xi_2\\
\xi_1&\xi_3&\xi_4\\\xi_2&\xi_4&\xi_5\end{pmatrix},
\quad
g_{00}=\frac{p(R)}{R-\ell}-2R\xi_1
-R^2(2\xi_2+\xi_3)-2R^3\xi_4-R^4\xi_5.
$$

The following division is exact by the definition of $g_{00}$:

$$
h(a)=\frac{p(a)-(a-\ell)v_2(a)^TGv_2(a)}{R-a}
=\sum_{j=0}^4 h_j a^j.
$$

Its coefficients are affine in $q,\xi$, with fixed rational denominators.
Finally impose

$$
G\succeq0,\qquad
J=\begin{pmatrix}
h_0&h_1/2&\xi_6\\
h_1/2&h_2-2\xi_6&h_3/2\\
\xi_6&h_3/2&h_4
\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.
\tag{5}
$$

The full polynomial and source trace identities are

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(a)&=(a-\ell)v_2^TGv_2+(R-a)v_2^TJv_2,\\
f_{\mathsf C y}(q)&=\operatorname{tr}(GM_\ell(y))+
                         \operatorname{tr}(JM_R(y)).
\end{aligned}
$$

Conversely, every polynomial nonnegative on the interval has such a PSD
Gram representation. This follows by dualizing the moment cone proved
above, without a closure caveat. Lebesgue moments give strictly positive
blocks: for any nonzero quadratic $w$, both integrals of
$(a-\ell)w(a)^2$ and $(R-a)w(a)^2$ are positive. Pairing at this fixed
strict moment point bounds the traces of convergent dual Gram preimages,
so the adjoint image is closed and dual representation is attained.
Every pair of representing Grams is included in (5): the endpoint value
fixes $g_{00}$, and $\xi_6$ is the remaining Gram freedom for $h$.

The matrices in (5) contain only the eight visible behavior coordinates
and six fixed auxiliaries. They contain no contact parameter. They impose
exactly this family of quantum-valid inequalities and can be intersected
with other sound behavior constraints. Equality with the full quantum set
is not claimed.

## Exact data and reconstruction

`source_pencil.json` and `behavior_pencil.json` contain rational sparse
upper-triangle records. Each record is
`[k,i,j,numerator,denominator]`: indices are zero-based, `k=0` denotes the
constant coefficient, and `k>=1` denotes `variables[k-1]`. Mirror every
off-diagonal record once. The source file also contains the complete
nine-coordinate embedding, including the two zero weights.

Run with Python 3 and SymPy:

```text
python build_pencils.py
```

The script reconstructs both pencils and checks (1)–(5), the Bernstein
coefficients, the full contact and its first derivatives, the sparse-data
round trips, and the exact source/behavior trace pairing. It writes
`exact_checks.json`. It uses no numerical optimizer or parameter sweep.
