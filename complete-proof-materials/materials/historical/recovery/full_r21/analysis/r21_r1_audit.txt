1. THE ACTUAL R1 STATEMENT

The source is the original stationary common-II source with d=1,
lambda=beta=0, all original strict gaps, n>=0, and

    t_s, B_s, a_s B_s, t_s B_s > 0 infinitesimal.

Source coefficients are in K_c; all testing angles and states are in K_t.
The tensor factors are separately labelled, with no disjointness assumption.
Using c_s=1+a_s B_s r and R_s=1+h a_s B_s, the compact variables are

    j=a_s c_s/R_s, B=B_s R_s/c_s, T=t_s c_s/R_s,
    p=a_s(1-r)/R_s, H=(1-h)/(t_s c_s),
    x=jB, y=TB, q_z=jT.

The strict gaps give 0<p<min(1,j), 0<H<1. The four quantities B,T,x,y
are infinitesimal. The newer theorem allows q_z to be arbitrary positive.
It supplies a literal inverse source map; it does not replace the source
by a limiting stationary model.

The conclusion is the whole original quarter-parent at all independent
projective testing angles and all states. The older FN file has a smaller
header domain, but R1N specifically checks the extension of every bound it
uses. Treating that older header as an unresolved contradiction would miss
the purpose and content of the later theorem.

2. WHY THE SOURCE-DOMAIN ENLARGEMENT IS SUBSTANTIVE

R1N4-R1N9 use the exact RSR polynomial dictionary. After division by j+1
or K=j+1-p, all source corrections are controlled by B, T, Bj, p/j and
bounded p,H. A positive lower bound on j-p, 1-p, H, or 1-H is not used.
In particular no term requires jT to be bounded.

The n numerator has the exact form

    N = p(1+epsilon_1)-Bj(j+1)(1+epsilon_2),

with infinitesimal source errors. Thus n>=0 bounds
eta=Bj(j+1)/p. If eta is infinitesimal then

    n ~ p/[2(j-p)(j+1)] > 0.

This is the reason that the most delicate source case has a nonzero usable
current. Literal n=0 remains in the other cases; it is never divided out.

Other crucial uniform quantities are

    h0=mu+1/2 ~ a positive bounded unit, mu=m(1-p)=O(p),
    A/T ~ TCH ~ TD(1-H) ~ k/2,  1/2<k<1,
    f_c ~ Tx^2, q_c ~ Tx, r_c ~ TjB^2(1-p).

All comparisons in this paragraph occur within the ordered source field.
They are not comparisons of a source infinitesimal with a testing one.

3. THE FULL PARENT IS RETAINED

FN4 is the actual polynomial

 P = m(vW-X+uvY-uZ)^2+n DuDv X^2+C Dv(uW-Y)^2+A DuDv Y^2
     +(D+1/2)(uW+uvX-Y-vZ)^2+(alpha+1/2)Du(vY-Z)^2
     +(1/2)Dv(X+uZ)^2-(1/2)DuDv Z^2,

where Du=1+u^2 and Dv=1+v^2.

FN5-FN9 introduce two unrestricted state-normal rows and expand the entire
parent, not merely its restriction to their common zero set. In particular
the complete normal block includes the cross term

    2(alpha+1/2) u v N0 M0.

The scalar graph alone would not prove the claim. The later parts of FN
explicitly allocate both linear normal arrays and this cross term.

The correct completion is

 h0 N0^2+n(N0+j b)^2
 = (n+h0)(N0+c_b b)^2+M_e b^2,

 c_b=nj/(n+h0), M_e=n h0 j^2/(n+h0), b=v-uz/j.

This identity is elementary expansion in the tensor ring with a pure-source
positive denominator. Its reserve is M_e, not the unreduced mass nj^2.
The additional correlated packet n v^2(N0+p b)^2 stays whole. At n=0,
c_b=M_e=0 and the effective current row is omitted. For n>0,

    c_b^2/M_e=n/[h0(n+h0)] <= 1/h0 <= 2.

Consequently the normal shift cannot introduce an uncontrolled coefficient
on the retained current row. This is the mathematically relevant repair
to any earlier scalar-only or double-spending argument.

4. THE ARBITRARY STATE RATIO REALLY REMAINS A ROW

The normalized quantities U=u/sqrt(x), V=v/sqrt(y), Z*=z/sqrt(jT)
need not be bounded. The contact circuit is

 Psi=(U-V)^2+Z*^2(1-UV)^2+2UV(1-Z*)^2.

This identity is valid by direct expansion. The square root of UV factors
as the testing sqrt(uv) times the source (xy)^(-1/4). Every root is an
element of K_c tensor K_t. The proof does not take a mixed inverse.

The budget coefficients depend on source variables and infinitesimal u,v.
The arbitrary positive z is kept inside the rows Q*,B*,R*,Psi and the full
normal rows. The source cases handle potentially large 1/(1-p), 1/M_e and
1/(j^2B) only where those inverses are actually bounded or are cancelled by
the chosen exact circuit identity. In the j-infinitesimal case, 1-p is a
unit, so the direct r_c budget is used; the potentially large T/j in an
alternative estimate is not needed there.

The full opposite second-normal row has squared multiplier

    a_R=T(jT)v^2(alpha-BTCH u^2)^2.

The FN24 and R1N11 costs cancel q_z exactly. They keep the whole square,
including the higher testing terms and their cross term. The case where
an effective-current denominator is used has p~1 and eta infinitesimal,
so n>0 and (1+1/n)/j^2 is bounded. The other source cases use different
explicit budgets. This is a legitimate case distinction within K_c.

The remaining hard mixed term after normalization has infinitesimal bounded

5. THE OUTER IDENTITIES CAN BE CHECKED WITHOUT A SOLVER

Set R=uW-Y, Ct=v(uX-Z), Bt=X+uZ, g=CD/(C+D), h=D/(C+D).
Harmonic completion gives exactly

    C Dv R^2+D(R+Ct)^2
    =(C+D)(R+h Ct)^2+g Ct^2+C v^2 R^2.

For v noninfinitesimal, the useful identity is

    u Bt-Ct/v=Du Z.

It implies

 2Dv Bt^2-2DuDv Z^2
 = (2Dv/Du)Bt^2+(4uDv/(Du v))Ct Bt
   -(2Dv/(Du v^2))Ct^2.

After setting K0=2sqrt(g)Ct and B0=sqrt(2Dv/Du)Bt, this is exactly the
two-row Q in R1N13. All normalized testing factors are bounded; 1/g is
infinitesimal by the source harmonic estimate Tg~k/2. Thus its residue is I.
No condition on the other angle or state is required.

For v infinitesimal, expand the four-row expression R1N14. Using
K1=2sqrt(g)Ct+Y/sqrt(g), B1=Bt-uvY, V0=vY, and
Y1=sqrt(Du/g)Y, its remaining quadratic expression simplifies exactly to

 4g Ct^2+4A DuDv Y^2+2Dv Bt^2
 +2Du v^2Y^2-4Du vYZ-2Du v^2Z^2.

Expanding the original signed core after harmonic completion gives the same
expression. Its residue is diag(1,2,2,k0^2-c_u0^2). This is positive for
unbounded u, and for every noninfinitesimal u when k0=1.

The remaining outer case has bounded positive-unit u and k0<1. Then p and
j are positive bounded units. The exact six-row identity R1N18 preserves
every state coordinate. The zero shift used to adjust its residue Gram is

    2b(PY0-V(uX-Z))=0,

because P=Ct/sqrt(T), Y0=sqrt(T)Y, V=vY. It is a polynomial row relation,
not a mixed change of field variables.

Its Q3 block in (X,Z,V) is

 m_c[(Du/u)V-X-uZ]^2+n_c Du X^2
 +(Du/2)V^2-Du VZ+(1/2)(X+uZ)^2-2bV(uX-Z).

For interior openings, m0=jp/[2(1-p)(j+1)],
n0=p/[2(j-p)(j+1)], b=k/2. For example the first two principal minors
follow directly from the coefficient matrix:

   a11=m0+n0 Du+1/2,
   det(Q3[1:2,1:2])=n0 Du u^2(m0+1/2)
     =p Du u^2(j+1-p)/[4(j-p)(1-p)(j+1)^2]>0.

The printed third minor is positive. The two-by-two (P,Y0) block has
determinant k^2u^2/4>0. Slightly smaller fixed real caps preserve positivity.

At infinite openings, the proof checks positivity on the kernel of the
large event packet(s), then chooses sufficiently large real caps. This uses
the elementary fact: if E>=0 and F is positive definite on ker(E), then
F+cE is positive definite for sufficiently large real c. A block Schur
complement proves that fact. The cap excesses remain whole nonnegative
source-event squares. No unbounded source coefficient is treated as a
small perturbation.

7. WHY STRICT RESIDUE IMPLIES AN EXACT TENSOR SOS

Let B_c,B_t be the bounded rings and B=B_c tensor_R B_t. Every a in B with
positive real residue is a finite tensor SOS. Write a=epsilon+sum x_i tensor
y_i, where each summand has an infinitesimal factor. For an infinitesimal x
and bounded y, choose real M>|y| and a positive real reserve epsilon_i. Then

 epsilon_i+x tensor y
 =1/2[(epsilon_i/M+x) tensor (M+y)
      +(epsilon_i/M-x) tensor (M-y)].

The four factors are positive in their own fields, so separate field square
roots give tensor squares. This proves the scalar lemma directly.

A real congruence normalizes a strict residue Gram to I. The decomposition
in BG lines 29-35 expresses it as a sum of fixed real rank-one matrices with
scalar coefficients having positive residue. Applying the scalar lemma to
those coefficients gives an exact SOS on arbitrary tensor rows, including
unbounded rows. This is why the argument is not merely valued-field
positivity, not a formal power-series square root, and not an inverse of a
mixed tensor.

The resulting square roots can have finite separation rank larger than two.
That is harmless for ordinary finite-SDP existence and gives no SOC claim.

8. WHAT THE DIRECTED WEAK-C2 JOIN DOES AND DOES NOT ADD

HJ specifies an original physical C2 stationary source with two infinitesimal
contact angles, strong C, weak alpha, and infinitesimal inverse total source
scale. Its reflection is packetwise exact. The actual source estimates give
B,z,y infinitesimal and the finite alternatives in HJ16.

The ten rows are exhausted by splitting t first, then h, l, j, r, g and the
remaining bounded/unbounded a alternatives. Two apparent missing cases are
excluded by actual source equations: all six weights bounded contradict the
total-scale hypothesis; simultaneous weak C contradicts the directed strong-C
hypothesis. Those exclusions are genuine source restrictions, not admissions
based on a status label.

The join invokes specified full-parent bodies for R2-R10 and WA/WE/SL/WC.
Their scopes are described explicitly, but this audit has not independently
rederived every one of those bodies. HJ is not by itself a proof of entry of
all valid global affine sources into this chart. Its last paragraph correctly
leaves source entry, other omission patterns, and other scale regimes to
separate theorems. Those are the places where a global audit must continue.
