identity in all 215 components. Historical PASS or COMPLETE labels are
retained historical records, not new mathematical premises.

together with the recovered event and closed-face certificates and finite

qualitative existence criterion. This recovery supplies no evaluated pencil,

INTEGRITY AND REPRODUCTION

From the extracted delivery root, run:

    python3 verify_recovery.py

This checks every manifest-bound file and CRC integrity of the nested ZIPs.
It uses only the Python standard library. It does not execute symbolic
checkers, solve an optimization problem, or prove any mathematical claim.
