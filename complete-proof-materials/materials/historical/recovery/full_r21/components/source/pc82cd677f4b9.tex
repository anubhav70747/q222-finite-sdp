\section{R6 with unbounded $a$: the complete thin layer is admitted}

\begin{theorem}
Retain the entire original physical R6 source intersection, $d=1$,
$\lambda=\beta=0$, all strict gaps, $n\ge0$, total scale, and prior
exclusions. Thus $t,h,l=1-h,j,r$ are positive bounded units and
$B,x=aB,y=tB,g$ positive infinitesimals. Assume $a$ unbounded and
\[
 n=0\quad\hbox{or}\quad 0<\rho:=n/(1-r)^2\in\operatorname{Inf}.
 \tag{RT1}
\]
Then the entire original four-state parent is finite tensor SOS at
every independent projective testing pair, with both signs, literal
axes and infinities, and real or complex states. No source/testing
rate comparison is used. The complementary $\rho$ noninfinitesimal
source is not admitted by this theorem.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Exact source entry, including every $g$ opening.}
Put $s=1/a$, $w=a(1-r)$, $b=a^2B$, so $x=sb$, $B=s^2b$ and
$e=1-r=sw$. The inherited exact physical identity R10.2 is
\[
 2r\Delta_*n/s^2=wQ_*-bK_*,\qquad
 Q_*\sim1,\quad K_*\sim h+t,\quad\Delta_*\sim1.       \tag{RT2}
\]
Since $g=1-w+hx$ is positive infinitesimal, $w\sim1$.
Physicality gives $b$ bounded, $n=O(s^2)$ and
$b_0(h_0+t_0)\le1$. On RT1, equality holds in residue:
\[
 b\sim1/(h+t),\qquad
 m g\sim\tfrac12,\quad C\sim{h+t\over2l},\quad
 A\sim{t\over2h},\quad D\sim{1\over2(t-l)}.          \tag{RT3}
\]
All four $m,C,A,D$ have positive real lower bounds; $m$ is unbounded.
Every assertion is joint in the independent positive $s,g$ openings.
In particular no bound on $g/s$ or $s/g$ is assumed.

\paragraph{Literal $n=0$ is a physical overlap, with an exact scope match.}
The unchanged OP4 contact map is
\[
 k_1=\sqrt{x},\quad\eta_1=t,\quad\zeta_1=s,\quad

 \tag{RT4}

The native gaps are exactly $M_1=g$ and $J_1=j$.
When $n=0$, OP4 identifies the full physical C2 parent with the native

\[
 e_{1,\mathrm{scale}}^2=k_1^2\eta_1/\zeta_1=tb
              \sim {t\over h+t}>0.                 \tag{RT5}
\]
Thus the exact theorem \texttt{c1/bounded\_e\_all\_tests.tex}
(BE1--BE6 and its contact join) applies. Its statement explicitly
allows arbitrary positive $M,J$ openings and literal beta zero.
This proves all tests and states on the whole RT1 literal-zero
boundary, rather than taking a positive-$n$ limit.

\paragraph{The positive thin layer: a bounded formal C1 base.}

construction for this formal source, not a physical theorem at

\paragraph{Scope and restoration.}
