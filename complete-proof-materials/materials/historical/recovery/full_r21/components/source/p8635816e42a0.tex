\section{Large zeta: the enlarged state chart and the exact remaining corner}
\label{sec:r17-large-zeta-damped-state}

\begin{proposition}
Retain the entire physical C14.3a source with $a=1/\zeta$, $L$ and
$\eta$ positive infinitesimals, $x=aL$, all original strict source
gaps, $\beta\ge0$ and every inherited intersection. No condition
on $L/\eta$ is imposed. At every independent local testing pair,
the complete original parent is finite tensor SOS on
\[
 Z\ne0,\qquad X/Z\text{ bounded},\qquad
                         u^2W/Z\text{ bounded}.        \tag{LS1}
\]
The state coordinate $Y/Z$ is arbitrary. Literal $Z=0$ is also
admitted. This enlarges the EZ bounded-$W/Z,X/Z$ state chart.
For complex states the corresponding bounded modulus ratios suffice.
\end{proposition}

Use the complete identity (LD9), its source scale $s=L+\eta$,
and the strict seven-root frame in (LD10)--(LD11). All its coefficient
bounds except (LD14) were proved without a restriction on $L/\eta$.
The only unprocessed expression is therefore $\epsilon uvQ\xi$.
On $Z\ne0$ set
\[
 \phi=\xi/Z=X/Z+hu.
\]
The same exact identity as DS2 gives
\begin{equation}
 \epsilon uvQ\xi
  =\epsilon U(W/Z)E\xi
     -\epsilon u\phi E^2/\eta-\epsilon uvP\xi,
 \qquad U=u^2.                                      \tag{LS2}
\end{equation}
It follows by substituting $Q=E/\eta-P$ and $vZ=\eta uW-E$.
Its only testing inverse is $Z^{-1}$; no mixed expression is inverted.

The source completion gives bounded linear membership for $P$ and
\[
 Q=\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{\eta s}}N_Q,
\]
where $\omega$ and $\omega^{-1}$ are bounded positive pure-source
units. Use $E=\eta(P+Q)$, $\epsilon=O(s)$ and
$\xi=N_x/\sqrt B$. The coefficients of the first term in (LS2)
on $PN_x$ and $N_QN_x$ have respective orders
\[
 O(\eta s)U(W/Z),\qquad O(\sqrt{\eta s})U(W/Z).
\]
They are infinitesimal on (LS1). The coefficients of the second term
on $P^2$, $PN_Q$ and $N_Q^2$ have orders
\[
 O(\eta s)u\phi,\qquad
 O(\sqrt{\eta s})u\phi,\qquad O(1)u\phi.             \tag{LS3}
\]
All are infinitesimal because $\phi$ is bounded and $u$ is
infinitesimal. The last term has coefficient $O(suv)$ on $PN_x$.
The $1/\sqrt B$ factors are bounded and do not alter these estimates.
Consequently the whole missing normal is an infinitesimal Gram on
the existing strict frame, without using $L/\eta$ or the old
$E/\sqrt\eta$ pivot. Restore the same whole positive rows in (LD9)
on $WZ>0$; use the half-core sign exit on $WZ\le0$. This proves
the complete local parent on (LS1).

\paragraph{The original-parent state exit.}
If $Z\ne0$ and $X/Z$ is unbounded, then for infinitesimal $u$
the pure-testing scalar
\[
 \frac{1+v^2}{2}
       \big\{(X+uZ)^2-(1+u^2)Z^2\big\}              \tag{LS4}
\]
is strictly positive. Indeed division by the positive testing square
$Z^2$ leaves $(1+v^2)[(X/Z+u)^2-(1+u^2)]/2>0$.
Every remaining original packet, including both other half-core
squares, has a nonnegative pure-source coefficient. Thus (LS4)
gives a direct tensor-SOS exit, independent of the source scale.
The $Z=0$ endpoint is already a sum of the original positive packets.

For complex states satisfying the modulus version of (LS1), choose
a pure-testing phase making $Z$ real and positive. The real part
satisfies (LS1), and the imaginary part has $Z=0$. Their two real
certificates add to the original Hermitian evaluation. No bound on
$Y/Z$ or state norm is required. Literal local angle axes are included
by (LS2) and the division-free circuit rows.

\paragraph{A source-independent consequence of the negative core.}
The exact half-core Schur formula supplies a useful additional
testing bound. In the sign gauge $u,v\ge0$, write
\[
 p=uv,\quad z_t=1+v^2+u^2v^2,\quad
 H_t=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}z_t&-p\\-p&z_t\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The hard block on $(X,Y)$ is positive definite, and its Schur form
on $(W,Z)$ is
\[
 \frac{(1+u^2)(1+v^2)}{2(z_t^2-p^2)}
          \{p^2(W^2+Z^2)-2pz_tWZ\}.                 \tag{LS5}
\]
If the pure-testing half-core is nonnegative, it is itself a testing
square and the original positive events complete the tensor-SOS exit.
Otherwise (LS5) must be negative. Hence $u,v>0$, $WZ>0$, and
\[
       uv\left(\frac WZ+\frac ZW\right)<2z_t.
\]
Both summands are positive. Since $u,v$ are local infinitesimals,
$z_t\sim1$, so in particular $uvW/Z$ is bounded. This deduction
uses only a pure-testing inequality; no ordering on the tensor ring
or comparison to a source scale is involved.

\paragraph{Exact remaining normalized corner.}
Combine the unit-$\eta$ admission EU, the source admission (LD1),
the state admission (LS1), the direct exit (LS4), the half-core exit
and the inherited remote theorem. Every unadmitted real test on this
large-$\zeta$ source end can then be normalized by the nonzero
pure-testing coordinate $W$ to $W=1$, and must lie in
\[
\begin{gathered}
 a,L,\eta>0\text{ infinitesimal},\quad x=aL,
       \qquad L/\eta\text{ unbounded},\\
 u,v>0\text{ infinitesimal},\qquad W=1,\quad Z>0,\\
 X/Z\text{ bounded},\qquad Z/u^2\text{ infinitesimal},
             \qquad uv/Z\text{ bounded},\qquad v/u\text{ infinitesimal}.
\end{gathered}                                                     \tag{LS6}
\]
All original source openings and $\beta\ge0$ remain imposed, and
$Y$ is unrestricted. The new angle-ratio conclusion here is derived,
not imported from a unit-$\zeta$ theorem: exactly
\[
                 \frac vu=\frac{uv}{Z}\frac{Z}{u^2},
\]
a bounded pure-testing element times an infinitesimal. Normalization
by $W$ preserves the two state ratios and multiplies the original
form by a positive pure-testing square. No assertion that this
residual intersection is nonempty is made.

On (LS6), the second and third terms in (LS2) already have
infinitesimal Gram coefficients by (LS3). The sole unallocated
complete normal is exactly
\[
                         \epsilon U(W/Z)E\xi.          \tag{LS7}
\]
Since $s\asymp L$ on this source branch, its two coefficients on
$PN_x$ and $N_QN_x$ have orders
$\eta L\,UW/Z$ and $\sqrt{\eta L}\,UW/Z$.
The negative-core bound rewrites the latter as
$O(\sqrt{\eta L}\,u/v)$, which need not be bounded in the
independent source/testing tensor setting. Neither (LS6) nor
(LS7) is claimed to have a further certificate here.

Identity (LS2) and the original-parent accounting in (LS4) are exactly
the already checked DS identities; the new source estimates are
the sixteen receipts for (LD2)--(LD8). No duplicate checker or
historical-suite replay is needed for this corollary.
