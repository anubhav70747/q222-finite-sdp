\section{C14.2: pure-testing state exits at the two small angles}

Keep the unchanged C14.2 source and the exact quarter-parent BE10,
with $d=1$, $L$ a positive bounded source unit, $x,\sigma>0$
infinitesimal, and every original opening.  In particular $D,\beta\ge0$
may vanish.  The R18 LA and LB admissions leave $u,w>0$
infinitesimal.  Literal axes have the original direct core SOS.
The original pure-testing core-sign exit leaves $WZ>0$; consequently
$P=uW$ and $T=wZ$ are nonzero and have the same sign.

\paragraph{The two extreme state ratios.}
Choose positive real numbers $c_*,a_*,a'_* $ below the actual
source weights $C,A,\alpha$, respectively; this is possible by LA1.
Retain all positive original excesses and the entire $m,D,\beta$
packets.  The selected polynomial is exactly
\[
 H_*=c_*D_w(P-Y)^2+a_*D_uD_wY^2
       +a'_*D_u(Y-T)^2+K_w/2.                     \tag{CS1}
\]
Here LA2 gives the complete signed core, without approximation:
\[
 K_w=D_uX^2+D_uY^2+u^2T^2-2u^2TY-2PT
       +w^2\{(P-Y)^2+X^2\}+2uwX(T+P-Y).          \tag{CS2}
\]
If $P/T$ is unbounded, write $T=\epsilon P$ with the
\emph{pure-testing} infinitesimal $\epsilon=T/P$.  The bounded
Gram of CS1 on $(X,Y,P)$ has residue
\[
 \tfrac12X^2+c_*(P-Y)^2+(a_*+a'_*+\tfrac12)Y^2,
                                                               \tag{CS3}
\]
which is strictly positive.  If $P/T$ is infinitesimal, write
$P=\epsilon T$.  On $(X,Y,T)$ the residue is
\[
 \tfrac12X^2+(c_*+a_*+\tfrac12)Y^2+a'_*(Y-T)^2,  \tag{CS4}
\]
also strictly positive.  All coefficients of CS1 are pure testing
and real, so the usual finite positive-Gram lift is directly legal.
Restoring the retained original squares proves the complete parent
on both charts, for arbitrary remaining state coordinates.

\paragraph{Every unmatched unit state ratio.}
It remains to consider the positive bounded testing unit
\[
 \varrho=P/T=uW/(wZ).
\]
Put $L_0=\operatorname{res}(L)>0$ and
$\varrho_0=\operatorname{res}(\varrho)>0$.  This paragraph admits
every chart $\varrho_0\ne L_0$, using only real residues, rather
than ordering $\varrho-L$ as a mixed element.

Use the exact LA1 source weights $C_0,A_0,a_0$, with
$e_r=1-r$, $b=Lr$, and $S_0=A_0+a_0+1/2$.  Choose the same
pure-source common factor $f=1-\delta>0$ as LA4 and retain the
original nonnegative event excesses $C-fC_0$, $A-fA_0$,
$\alpha-fa_0$.  The entire remaining polynomial is the exact LA4
form.  In its completed part LA3, retain the whole nonnegative
$u^2$ packet, the full $w^2$ packet, and the coefficient excesses
after clipping $fC_0,fS_0$ at positive real lower bounds $c,s$.
The resulting bounded principal form on $(X,Y,T)$ is
\[
 \frac{fD_u}{2}X^2
   +c\{(\varrho-Le_r)T-Y\}^2+s(Y-bT)^2.         \tag{CS5}
\]
Its $(Y,T)$ determinant is exactly
\[
 cs(\varrho-L)^2,                               \tag{CS6}
\]
as a polynomial identity, since $Le_r+b=L$.  At residues this is
the positive real number $cs(\varrho_0-L_0)^2$.
The full signed remainder
$(1-f)K_0/2+(K_w-K_0)/2$ has infinitesimal bounded coefficients
on $(X,Y,T)$ after $P=\varrho T$, by CS2.  Thus the full selected
bounded Gram is strictly positive at residue.  The separated
bounded-Gram lemma applies; all previously retained positive
packets are restored.  No inverse of $\varrho-L$, or of any
source/testing sum, has been used.

\paragraph{Precise remaining state chart.}
Together with LA, LB, the literal-axis exits and the core-sign exit,
these two admissions reduce the whole C14.2 problem to
\[
 u,w>0\text{ infinitesimal},\quad WZ>0,\qquad
 \varrho=\frac{uW}{wZ}>0\text{ a bounded testing unit},\qquad
 \operatorname{res}\varrho=\operatorname{res}L.    \tag{CS7}
\]
In particular $uW/Z=w\varrho$ is infinitesimal.  The normalized
radial root is exactly
\[
 R=\frac{wZ}{q}(1-z\varrho),\qquad q^2=z=L^{-1};  \tag{CS8}
\]
CS7 does not compare the source and testing infinitesimal parts of
$L$ and $\varrho$.  It does not constrain the source sphere or
remove $D=0$ or $\beta=0$.  This is a state-chart admission and
reduction, not a complete C14.2 closure.  Applying the real statement
to the two real state parts gives the complex-state statement,
with the relevant chart chosen separately for each part.

The focused checker verifies CS6 and the new three-term circuit
identity in the accompanying note; it does not rerun the historical
LA2--LA4 checks.
