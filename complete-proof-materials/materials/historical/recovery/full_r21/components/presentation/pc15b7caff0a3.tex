\section{Small $L$: a complete native normal identity and the exact soft defect}
\label{pc15b7caff0a3:sec:r8-small-L-native-normal}

This is an exact reduction, not a new admission. Use the unchanged
original C1 source, $d=1$, $x,L=x\zeta\in\operatorname{Inf}_{>0}$,
$\zeta$ noninfinitesimal, and first suppose $\eta$ bounded. Retain
all source openings, all independent testing rates, and literal
$\beta=0$. The original $D=0$ face is empty here by (SB2).

\paragraph{Every original state normal, including the beta packet.}
Put $U=u^2,V=v^2,D_u=1+U,D_v=1+V$, and use native $r,h=1-\theta$:
\[
 X=-huZ+\xi,\qquad Y=ruW+\nu.
\]
Write $F_g/4=A_wW^2+A_zZ^2-2uv(b_0+b_1U)WZ$, with
$(A_w,A_z,b_0,b_1)$ from (WS2)--(WS3), setting $n=0$, replacing
the WS letters $(h,r)$ by native $(r,h)$, and adding
$\beta\theta^2UD_v$ to $A_z$. Then the entire original parent is
exactly
\[
\begin{split}
 F/4={}&F_g/4+2\xi(vWf_w+uZf_z)+2\nu(uWk_w+vZk_z)\\
 &+H_{xx}\xi^2+2H_{xy}\xi\nu+H_{yy}\nu^2.
\end{split}                                               \tag{SN1}
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 f_w={}&-m(1+rU)+(D+\tfrac12)(1-r)U,\\
 f_z={}&m\theta-(D+\tfrac12)V(1+hU)
                    +(\beta+\tfrac12)D_v\theta,\\
 k_w={}&mV(1+rU)-CD_v(1-r)+AD_uD_vr
             -(D+\tfrac12)(1-r)+(\alpha+\tfrac12)D_urV,\\
 k_z={}&-m\theta U+(D+\tfrac12)(1+hU)
                              -(\alpha+\tfrac12)D_u,\\
 H_{xx}={}&m+\beta D_v+(D+\tfrac12)UV+D_v/2,\\
 H_{yy}={}&mUV+CD_v+AD_uD_v+D+\tfrac12
                              +(\alpha+\tfrac12)D_uV,\\
 H_{xy}={}&-(m+D+\tfrac12)uv.
\end{aligned}                                                   \tag{SN2}
\]
There is no discarded normal or excess packet in this identity.
Its two exact first-normal contact equations are
\[
 \zeta f_w(x,y)+f_z(x,y)=0,\qquad
 k_w(x,y)+\eta k_z(x,y)=0,\qquad y=x\eta\zeta.             \tag{SN3}
\]
They follow by substituting (NC4), not by setting a testing angle
equal to a source angle in the desired tensor assertion.

The beta opening creates no unbounded product in its first normal:
\[
 2\beta\theta P_d=K(r+\eta)(xh+L)-\theta P_d,
 \qquad \beta\theta=O(L),\quad \beta\theta^2=O(L^2).       \tag{SN4}
\]
In particular, after the pure-source normalization
$\widehat\xi=\sqrt{1+\beta}\,\xi$, its cross coefficient is
$\beta\theta/\sqrt{1+\beta}=O(L)$, including every unbounded
beta opening. This does not by itself allocate the other normals.

The exact common-radial specialization
$U=\lambda x,V=\lambda y,W/Z=\sqrt{\zeta/\eta}$ obeys
\[
 \frac{F_g}{4Z^2}=(\lambda-1)^2(a_{\rm rad}\lambda+\alpha),
 \quad a_{\rm rad}=xL^2r^2(m+A+\alpha+\tfrac12)
                   +x^2yh^2(D+\tfrac12)>0.              \tag{SN5}
\]
The beta contribution is included; its degree is at most two in
$\lambda$, so it contributes through the two contact balances.
This is only a scalar source slice.

\paragraph{An opening-safe reference binomial.}
Set $e=\eta T$, $T=1+xh$, so that $j=e+r-1=\eta J>0$ exactly.
This choice is necessary: $\eta+r-1$ itself may be negative at an
original $J$ opening. Define positive source-only comparison weights
\[
 C_*={r+e\over2(1-r)},\quad A_*={e\over2r},\quad
 D_*={1\over2j},\qquad
 H_*={e(j+r)\over2r(1-r)j}.
\]
For independent indeterminates $p,t,Y$ there is the exact identity
\[
\begin{split}
 &C_*(p-Y)^2+A_*Y^2+D_*(p-Y-t)^2
                +\tfrac12(p-Y)^2-pt\\
 &=H_*\left(Y-{r(e+r)\over j+r}p
                    +{r(1-r)\over e(j+r)}t\right)^2
       +{e+r\over2e(j+r)}(ep-t)^2.                    
\end{split}\tag{SN6}
\]
The relevant separated roots are $p=uW$, $t=vZ$, and
$\sqrt e\,uW-vZ/\sqrt e$. The latter also remains legitimate
when $\eta$ is infinitesimal; no mixed inverse is introduced.
All original openings remain positive factors. Directly from (NC4),
\[
 {C\over C_*}={K(r+\eta)E\over(r+\eta T)P_d}\sim1,
 \quad {A\over A_*}={P_4\over TP_d}\sim1,
 \quad {D\over D_*}={B_5\over P_d}\sim1.                 \tag{SN7}
\]
These are uniform relative comparisons, not a license to replace
the actual packets in a semidefinite residue argument.

\paragraph{The actual quadratic base has a strictly negative soft determinant.}
For the actual coefficients set
\[
 H=C+A+D+\tfrac12,\quad
 a={A(C+D+\tfrac12)\over H},\quad
 c={D(C+A+\tfrac12)\over H},\quad
 b=\tfrac12+{AD\over H}.
\]
Exact completion of the same quadratic base gives its full hard
normal and soft form
\[
 H\left(Y-{C+D+\tfrac12\over H}p+{D\over H}t\right)^2
                        +ap^2+ct^2-2bpt.               \tag{SN8}
\]
Its determinant is
\[
 ac-b^2={8ACD-4AD-2A-2C-2D-1\over8H}.                  \tag{SN9}
\]
The unchanged native source has the following exact factorization:
\[
 8ACD-4AD-2A-2C-2D-1
 =-{\eta(r+\eta)^2KP_4\,\Delta
          \over r(1-r)jP_d^3},                         \tag{SN10}
\]
where
\[
 \Delta=R(\eta K+R)L^2+(2+xA_1)L+x(4h-1+xA_0),
 \qquad R=1+xr,                                        \tag{SN11}
\]
and the bounded polynomial arrays are
\[
\begin{split}
 A_1={}&-\eta^2h^2x^2-\eta^2h^2x
 -2\eta h^2rx^2-2\eta h^2x+2\eta hrx^2
 +3\eta hx+\eta h\\
 &+2hr^2x^2+6hrx+4h+2r,\\
 A_0={}&\eta^2h^4x^2-\eta^2h^3x^2+\eta^2h^3x-\eta^2h^2x
 -2\eta h^3rx^2-4\eta h^3x\\
 &+\eta h^2rx^2-\eta h^2rx+2\eta h^2x-4\eta h^2+2\eta h
 +h^2r^2x^2+4h^2rx+4h^2+2hr.
\end{split}
\]
Since (SB1) gives $h\sim1$, all arrays are bounded and
\[
                  \Delta/(2L+3x)\sim1.                 \tag{SN12}
\]
Indeed the $L^2$, $xL$, and $x^2$ errors divided by $L+x$
are infinitesimal, independently of the relative $x,L$ rate.
Every factor outside the minus sign in (SN10) is positive.
Thus the actual base in (SN8) has a strictly indefinite soft block
through every opening. The rank-one reference (SN6) is therefore
insufficient even at the exact quadratic level. A weighted circuit
must absorb the precise defect (SN10), using the original $m$,
$\alpha$, higher-angle terms, and the complete normals (SN1)--(SN2).
Neither scalar leading PSD nor the radial source slice proves that
allocation.

\paragraph{Unbounded eta and exact scope.}

also hold for unbounded $\eta$. The bounded-array estimate (SN12)
