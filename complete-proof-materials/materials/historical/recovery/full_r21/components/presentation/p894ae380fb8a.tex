\section{A pure-testing sign exit for the complete original parent}
The source event coefficients are nonnegative and $d>0$ is pure source.
It suffices to treat the quarter-core at $d=1$,
\[
 Q_0=\tfrac12\{(uW+uvX-Y-vZ)^2+D_u(vY-Z)^2
                   +D_v(X+uZ)^2-D_uD_vZ^2\}.
\]
All variables in $Q_0$ belong to the testing field. Put
$b=1+v^2+u^2v^2$ and $\Delta=b^2-u^2v^2$.
The hard matrix on $(X,Y)$ is
\[
 H=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}b&-uv\\-uv&b\end{pmatrix}\succ0.
\]
Indeed $b-|uv|=(|uv|-1/2)^2+3/4+v^2>0$.
The exact Schur complement on $(W,Z)$ is
\[
 S=\frac{D_uD_v}{2\Delta}
 \begin{pmatrix}u^2v^2&-uvb\\-uvb&u^2v^2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The inverse in this calculation is pure testing and is legitimate.
Thus $uvWZ\le0$ implies $Q_0\ge0$: the diagonal terms of $S$
are nonnegative and its cross contribution is also nonnegative.
At each such testing point, $Q_0$ is a nonnegative element of a real
closed field and has a pure-testing square root. Restoring $d$ and
all nonnegative source-weighted original events gives finite tensor
SOS directly. Literal $u=0$ or $v=0$ is included.

The exact sign gauges can always arrange $u,v\ge0$. Reversing the
sign of $u$ together with $Y,Z$ preserves every original squared
packet, as does reversing the sign of $v$ together with $X,Z$.
Consequently any remaining real state in the nontrivial local
argument satisfies $WZ>0$. A certificate valid for every such state,
combined with this exit, covers all real states. Applying the real
assertion to real and imaginary parts covers complex states.
This is a pure-testing routing lemma, not a comparison with a source
scale and not a new source-shape admission by itself.
