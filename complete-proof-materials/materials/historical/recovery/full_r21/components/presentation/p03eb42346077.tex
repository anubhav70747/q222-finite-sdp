\section{Weak IH: exhaustive all-tests admission}
\label{p03eb42346077:sec:weak-ih-join}
The weak-$C$ IH source leaf is admitted at every independent testing
pair. The source chart is the unchanged positive C2 source with
$d=1$, $\lambda=\beta=0$, $n\ge0$, $z=u_c^2$ infinitesimal,
$y=v_c^2$ unbounded, and $C$ weak. The source reduction proves that
$B=zb$ is unbounded and $\rho=B/y$ has residue in $[0,1]$.

For testing $u,w=1/v$ both infinitesimal, there are exactly three
source cases: $\rho_0=0$, $0<\rho_0<1$, or $\rho_0=1$.
They are proved respectively by the first endpoint theorem, the
preserved interior-corner theorem, and the second endpoint theorem.
For testing $u$ a unit and $w$ infinitesimal, the open-axis theorem
covers all three source cases. For testing $u$ unbounded and $w$
infinitesimal, (IR4)--(IR8) cover all source cases. Finally (IR1)--(IR3)
cover every finite testing $v$ and every testing $u$.
This partition is exhaustive on the two projective testing lines.

Both sign choices are included. In particular, the exact gauges
\[
 (u,W,X)\mapsto(-u,-W,-X),\qquad
 (v,W,Y)\mapsto(-v,-W,-Y)
\]
leave the entire original parent unchanged, all unlisted coordinates
being fixed. The homogeneous formulas include literal zero and
infinity without dividing by a zero testing coordinate. All source
scales are independent of all testing scales, and all normal state
coordinates are retained. Complex states split into their real and
imaginary parts. No lower bound on $n$ is imposed.

The old scope statements in the preserved revision-4 interior proof
remain a faithful record of that theorem's original local scope.
They are superseded for all-tests coverage by the present join;
they are not assumptions or obstructions in this completed argument.
