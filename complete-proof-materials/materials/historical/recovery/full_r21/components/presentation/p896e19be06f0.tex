whole C14.2 branch, not the other C1 branches or the global theorem.

\paragraph{Exhaust the testing angles first.}

Alice angle, including its projective endpoint, and every Bob angle.
The reviewed R18 LB proposition admits infinitesimal or zero Alice

\[

\]
and every original event packet is nonnegative.  Thus those axes
are covered without any limiting argument.  Exact testing sign
gauges reduce the remaining real tests to $u,w>0$.

\paragraph{Exhaust the two source types.}
Write $\sigma=1/\eta$, $z=L^{-1}$ and $H=h/\sigma$.
BE7 bounds $H$ below by a positive source unit.  It is therefore
either bounded or unbounded; neither case changes the original
source intersections.

If $H$ is bounded, split the exact pure-source coefficient

When $\lambda>0$, HP proves all states.  Its source-sphere split

If $H$ is unbounded, CF1--CF5 handle bounded $W/Z$.
