document with its mathematical sources. The principal recovered release is

positive-residue mechanism, legal normalization rules, and final finite
mixture/criterion implication are supported by the written arguments
examined here. The later full R1 construction is present. Targeted
examination supports its effective normal reserve, unrestricted state
ratio and outer testing regimes. The selected C1 endpoint and its
local/remote state-dependent join also have substantive written proofs.
No specific contradiction was found in the examined October parts.

\textbf{Review boundary.}
This is source recovery and a substantial scoped analytical audit,
not a fresh independent verification of every identity and every local
branch in 798 pages. The unconditional October theorem is the recovered
manuscript's conclusion. This report verifies its structure and selected
critical arguments while identifying dependencies not rederived in full.
No solver, sampled test, symbolic-check receipt, hash, or historical
completion label is used as a mathematical premise.

Specific issues were identified in the stronger August proof, including
a mixed-denominator assertion and an exact-versus-residue mismatch in a
reciprocal coefficient. They are documented below. They neither show
that \(\Q\) has no finite lift nor automatically invalidate the separate
October construction.
