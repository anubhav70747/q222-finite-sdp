WHY THE SCALAR NINE-SQUARE RESULT DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY END THE PROOF

This only certifies D*F. D is mixed in source and testing variables.
Strict positivity does not make such a tensor element a unit:
1+t^2 tensor s^2 maps to zero under embeddings sending s to i/t
in an algebraic closure of a source real-closed function field.
Consequently cancellation requires a new explicit lemma; it is not
ordinary division inside either field. This obstruction is not a
counterexample to Q222 representability.
