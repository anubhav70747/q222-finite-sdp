Verdict
-------
The stated scoped result is supported: a fixed 40-feature sufficient
construction, simultaneous full 8-by-8 positivity near both Bob endpoints
for each strict source, and a compatible positive-semidefinite contact
anchor. None of these proves positivity at every intermediate Bob angle.
Equation (18), hence this sufficient route to H1, remains unproved.

1. Parent, physicality and the sign issue
---------------------------------------
Equation (1) is the literal SG2 parent with lambda=n=0, not a restriction
to the contact state. The endpoint expansions retain all four independent
state coordinates and both angle variables. Their identities hold for
seven algebraically independent positive weights.

Physical global nonnegativity is needed only for the contact PSD anchor.
It is imported for genuine native sources from SG7 and the original
native stationary formulas; it is not inferred from positivity of seven
arbitrary weights. SG7 assumes the actual state kernel and both angular
stationarity equations. Its previously reviewed domain remains the
domain of this import.

For the actual parent, direct simultaneous substitution gives exactly
 F(-u,v; W,X,-Y,-Z)=F(u,v; W,X,Y,Z),
 F(u,-v; W,-X,Y,-Z)=F(u,v; W,X,Y,Z).
The additive verifier checks both polynomial identities, without changing
the source. Thus positive-angle physicality extends to negative Alice u
at the fixed positive contact Bob v. The leading u coefficient in (3)
supplies the homogeneous endpoint u=infinity. Consequently the
homogenized fixed-Bob parent is nonnegative for every real two-vector
and every real four-state vector, as required by the 2-by-4 theorem.

2. Endpoint matrices and the complete soft Schur calculation
----------------------------------------------------------
All four endpoint formulas (3) are exact. P0(v)>0 for v>0 follows by
successively forcing X,Y,Z,W to zero in its positive-weight roots.
For P2(v)>0, the roots force W,Y,Z,X to zero. At v=0 the kernels are,
respectively, W and X.

G0 is a positive sum of six independent squared linear forms on
R=span(X0,Y0,Z0,W1,Y1,Z1), with kernel span(W0,X1).
Its range determinant is
 256 A^2 alpha^2 (4C+4D+2d)(4m+4beta+2d)>0.
Positivity follows from the independent positive-weight rows, rather
than from the determinant alone. Ginf is positive definite: its d rows
first force X0,X1,Y0,Y1 to zero, and the remaining positive rows then
force W0,W1,Z0,Z1 to zero.

With the stated S1, the kernel-to-range derivative has precisely the
two rows -4m(X0+Z1) and (4D+2d)(W1-Y0). They lie in disjoint blocks of
G0 restricted to R. Hence their inverse-Gram mixed product is zero.
The two Schur corrections are
 (4m)^2/(4m+4beta+2d),
 (4D+2d)^2/(4C+4D+2d).
Subtracting them from the direct second-order entries 4m and 4D+2d gives
 4m(2beta+d)/(2beta+d+2m),
 4C(2D+d)/(2C+2D+d).
The order-v kernel block vanishes. The only potential order-v^2 mixed
entry is removed by (S2)_WX=0. All other S2 and S3 entries remain free.

For each fixed strict real source and fixed finite S2,S3 satisfying that
one constraint, continuity keeps the six-dimensional range positive.
The full Schur complement is v^2(Sigma0+O(v)), with Sigma0>0. This proves
the full 8-by-8 matrix is positive definite for sufficiently small
positive v. Its v^4 coefficient is Ginf>0, which proves positive
definiteness for sufficiently large v. These source-dependent
neighbourhoods do not imply a uniform cutoff over singular source
limits, nor do they cover the remaining interval.

3. The contact anchor and source-only interpolation
--------------------------------------------------
Calderon's 2-by-n theorem applies to the physical homogeneous fixed-Bob
form just established. A bilinear SOS gives an 8-by-8 PSD Gram. Its
diagonal blocks must equal the prescribed D_* P0 and D_* P2 by coefficient
comparison; its cross block has prescribed symmetric part D_* P1/2.
Its remaining freedom is exactly a skew matrix S_c. The factor D_*>0
is absorbed by scaling the Gram. The zero contact vector lies in the
kernel of this PSD Gram.

The explicit rational S_c^ker in (16) supplies only a kernel; no PSD
property is claimed for it. The proof correctly uses a possibly
different PSD S_c from the biquadratic theorem in (17).

The feasible skew matrices are described by finitely many polynomial
equalities and principal-minor inequalities over the source coefficient
field. A nonempty real feasible set has a point in that field's real
closure by real-closed-field transfer. This is enough for source-only
algebraic coefficients. No global-in-v algebraic selection follows.

The interpolation formula exactly matches any selected S_c at v_*>0,
keeps S0,S1,S4 unchanged, and enforces (S2)_WX=0. All its denominators
are positive nonzero source contact coordinates. The same polynomial
therefore retains both endpoint positivity results and its contact PSD
anchor. No neighbourhood of the possibly singular contact is proved.

4. The fixed 40-feature implication and precise external import
-------------------------------------------------------------
If the quartic H(v) is PSD for every v>=0, H(t^2) is a real symmetric
univariate polynomial matrix PSD for every real t. Hanselka and Sinn,
"Positive Semidefinite Univariate Matrix Polynomials," Corollary 2.8,
gives a polynomial matrix SOS also when the matrix is singular:
 https://arxiv.org/html/1707.08261v1

The following degree bound is an independent deduction, not an extra
unstated hypothesis of that theorem. Each diagonal of R(t)^T R(t) is a
sum of real polynomial squares. Its largest-degree nonzero squares have
positive leading coefficients and cannot cancel. Since H(t^2) has
degree at most eight, every entry of R has degree at most four.

Write R(t)=E(t^2)+t O(t^2). Averaging its square identities at t and -t
cancels the mixed term. It gives
 H(v)=E(v)^T E(v)+v O(v)^T O(v),
 deg E<=2, deg O<=1.
Substitution of x=(psi,u psi) therefore gives 24 even features and 16
half-integer Bob features. Their sixth-root indices are
 i in {0,6}, j in {0,3,6,9,12}, four state components.
They are a fixed source-independent subset of the 364-row grid.
The polynomial equality extends from v=t^2 to all v by coefficient
identity. Coefficient Gram feasibility has only finitely many
polynomial constraints, so the same real-closure argument gives
source-algebraic coefficients. There is no testing-dependent Gram
coefficient or division by a source/testing multiplier.

For the contact anchor, Calderon's original statement was checked
against the publisher's indexed abstract:
 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0024379573900529
 DOI 10.1016/0024-3795(73)90052-9.
The publisher full proof was not retrieved; the standard theorem is
an explicit external import, not independently reproved here.
See c1_bob_harmonic_external_imports_2026_10_26.txt for the primary-source
retrieval record.

Remaining constructive target
-----------------------------
The new finite matrix problem is a sufficient route:
for every genuine strict oriented source, choose source-only skew S2,S3
with (S2)_WX=0 such that H(v)>=0 for every v>=0.
Endpoint and contact control leave that implication open. It is
stronger than direct H1 admission into K364; its failure would not
refute H1. Existing reflection, density and conditional global pencil
results remain at their previously verified scopes. No Git file was
edited in this review.
