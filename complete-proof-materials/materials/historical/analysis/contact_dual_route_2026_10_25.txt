Result. There is an exact compact dual-margin formulation of the missing
admission theorem. However, classifying the FULL PSD moment functionals
that annihilate a contact source is not, by itself, enough to prove strict
feasibility on its contact complements. The correct dual condition is
positivity AFTER contact compression. An explicit closed SDP-cone model
below separates these two conditions. No Q222 counterexample is asserted.

1. Exact reduced SDP alternative.

Let E be the412-dimensional coefficient space of one154-feature support
face. For its four blocks write z_b for the feature vector, v_b=z_b(x_c)
for the nonzero contact vector, and choose V_b whose columns span v_b^perp.
Let A(G)=sum_b z_b^t G_b z_b. For a coefficient functional ell on E,
define the structured moment blocks

 M_b(ell)[i,j]=ell(z_bi*z_bj),
 C_b(ell)=V_b^t M_b(ell)V_b.

After diagonal monomial normalization to contact all-ones, these data
give one fixed map

 A_c: direct_sum Sym(n_b-1) --> V,
 V={forms with zero contact value/first jets}, dim V=406.

The rank theorem makes A_c surjective. Its annihilator J=V^perp in E*
has dimension6 and is generated by the two angle and four state derivative
functionals; value is redundant by degree-two state homogeneity.
Thus ell is determined on V modulo J, and C_b(ell)=0 for every b
if and only if ell belongs to J.
The reduced domain has3021 symmetric coordinates (1326+630+630+435),
so its coefficient-map kernel has dimension2615.

The exact strict-admission criterion is

 F=A_c(G), all G_b positive definite
 iff ell(F)>0 for every ell with all C_b(ell)>=0 and not all C_b zero. (D)

Only compressed positivity appears here. If F is already feasible,
ell(F)>=0 for these functionals; strict admission then fails precisely
when a nonzero compressed positive functional has ell(F)=0.

For continuation it is enough to prove the following facial lemma at every
feasible limit of the nondegenerate native sources:

  all C_b(ell)>=0 and ell(F_c)=0  ==>  all C_b(ell)=0.               (DF)

At such a limit, closedness gives a full feasible Gram; its zero contact
evaluation forces all block contact vectors into its kernel, so it is
indeed reduced-feasible. Then(DF) gives reduced strict feasibility by(D),
establishes ND, and the existing connectedness argument completes contact
admission. For direct admission without the feasible-limit argument, the
strict-positive version(D), including exclusion of negative ell(F), is needed.

2. A compact normalized dual margin.

On V*, A_c* is injective. Set

 D={ell: A_c*ell>=0, sum_b tr(A_c*ell)_b=1},
 eta(F)=min_{ell in D} ell(F).

D is nonempty and compact. Nonemptiness follows by integrating squares
of the independent reduced roots on a fixed positive testing box/state
sphere: the resulting moment blocks are PD. Injectivity gives
||ell||<=K||A_c*ell||<=K on D; closedness then proves compactness.

The equivalent primal is

 eta(F)=max{epsilon: A_c(G)=F, G_b>=epsilon I for all b}.            (M)

Because A_c is surjective, a linear solution G0 always exists; choosing
epsilon sufficiently negative makes this primal strictly feasible.
The positive integral functional also gives a strictly feasible dual.
Finite-dimensional SDP duality therefore proves(M), with attainment.
The same evaluation trace bound makes the reduced PSD image closed.
Consequently

 eta>0 iff strict admission; eta>=0 iff admission; eta=0 iff boundary
 admission. Eta<0 detects nonadmission.

Eta is concave, positively homogeneous and globally Lipschitz on V:
it is the minimum over one fixed compact set of linear functionals.
The normalized native target is continuous/analytic in its native
parameters. This proves continuity of its margin, not positivity or ND.
The four strict seeds give eta>0 at those seeds only.

3. What the contact evaluations actually generate.

The four root-cover sign evaluations are NOT four independent coefficient
functionals: all product exponents in E are even in the sixth-root
variables, so the sign-orbit evaluations coincide on E.

Even allowing separate rank-one moment blocks rho_b*v_b*v_b^t, their
Hankel compatibility forces all rho_b equal. At normalized contact,
s^2Y^2 is represented both in the even-even and odd-even blocks;
t^2Y^2 similarly equates the even-even and even-odd weights;
s^2t^2Y^2 equates the odd-odd weight. The Y grid is full in both faces.
Thus the compatible PSD moment blocks supported on the four contact
vectors form one ray: ordinary contact evaluation.

There is a useful structural check: among the six-dimensional jet
annihilator J, the only functionals with FULL M_b(ell)>=0 are this
nonnegative evaluation ray. At normalized contact a derivative functional
has M_b=1*h_b^t+h_b*1^t. Such a matrix is PSD only when h_b is a
nonnegative scalar multiple of1. The varying angular powers in the
Y grid force both angle-derivative coefficients to vanish; the state
types in each block then force one common Euler-state coefficient.

At an already strictly admitted contact, complementarity therefore
implies that every full PSD moment functional annihilating F is a
contact evaluation. The converse is the problematic direction.

4. Why FULL PSD annihilators do not suffice.

For a compressed positive moment block, use contact-plus-complement
coordinates to write

       M=[[a,b^t],[b,C]], C>=0.

Adding a contact evaluation only increases a. A PSD extension exists
only if b lies in range(C). If C is singular and this compatibility
fails, no multiple of the contact evaluation makes M PSD. In the
actual problem one may also add the other jet functionals in J, but
their ability to repair every such witness is a new extension theorem,
not supplied by the rank result.

An exact closed-cone countermodel makes the logical issue concrete.
Let K be the Lorentz cone in R3 plus the ray p=(1,5/3,0). It is the
linear image of Q>=0,mu>=0 under

 (Q,mu) -> (tr(Q)/2+mu,(Q11-Q22)/2+5mu/3,Q12).

It is closed, since its first coordinate bounds tr(Q) and mu; it also
has a globally strict SDP point. Put q=(1,3/5,4/5). Every full dual
functional nonnegative on K and annihilating q is a nonnegative multiple
of y0=(1,-3/5,-4/5), by equality in the Lorentz Cauchy-Schwarz bound.
Nevertheless q is not in the relative interior of its exposed face:

       K intersect y0^perp = cone{q,p}.

Indeed its SDP representations have mu=0 and
Q=(2/5)*(2,1)^t*(2,1). Strict positivity on that facial complement
would require mu>0, which is impossible at q.

The missing reduced dual witness is y1=(-1,5/3,0). Its compressed
Q block is zero and its mu block is16/9>0, while y1(q)=0. Its full
Q-adjoint is diag(1/3,-4/3). Adding t*y0 changes this by
t/5*[[1,-2],[-2,4]], but the quadratic value on v=(2,1) remains zero
and the matrix times v remains (2/3,-4/3), nonzero. It can never be
PSD. Thus all ordinary full PSD annihilators are generated by the
contact functional, yet strict feasibility in the exposed face fails.

This is not a Q222 or Puiseux-cone counterexample. It shows exactly
why the proposed ordinary-annihilator classification does not imply
the required facial conclusion without another structural property.

5. Precise remaining structural target.

For exact admission the weakest sufficient target is eta(F_c)>=0
throughout both required native families. Strict positivity is NOT
necessary: zero margin still gives an attained singular PSD certificate.
The next two formulations are stronger targets for an ND/continuation proof.

One can work directly on the fixed406-dimensional map and prove(DF),
or prove strict positivity of the compact dual margin(M) for every
normalized native target. Alternatively the full-moment proposal needs
both a classification of its annihilators and a positive extension
lemma from compressed moments modulo J to full PSD moments. Relative
facial exposedness sufficient to extend the relevant normals would
also close that implication.

No such extension/positivity theorem follows from physical pointwise
nonnegativity, unique contact, constant coefficient rank, global Slater,
closedness, or the imported qualitative tensor-SOS result. PSD truncated
moment functionals are not assumed to be positive measures, so one
cannot localize their support to the physical zero merely from F>=0.
The bounded route therefore yields an exact dual formulation and a
precise obstruction to the weaker proposed inference, not admission.
