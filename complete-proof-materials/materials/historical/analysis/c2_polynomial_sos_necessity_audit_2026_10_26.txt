Here is the direct proof. Fix one native real source and suppose F=sum_j R_j^2 with arbitrary ordinary real polynomial roots in u,v,W,X,Y,Z. Since F has degree at most two separately in u and v, each root has degree at most one separately in those variables: a higher leading degree would contribute a sum of squares of nonzero leading coefficient polynomials, which cannot cancel identically. The same leading-homogeneous argument in the four state variables gives state degree at most one. Since F(u,v,0,0,0,0)=0, every root's state-independent term vanishes. Consequently
  R_j=sum_{l=W,X,Y,Z}(A_jl+B_jl u+C_jl v+D_jl uv) psi_l.
This rules out any higher-degree escape, irrespective of the number or source dependence of the roots.
