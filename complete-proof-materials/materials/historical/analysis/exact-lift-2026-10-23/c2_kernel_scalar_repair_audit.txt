1. Exact determinant factorization

Write B_s=H+L, k0=m B_s^2/z^2, and let
 Pker=p2(x)w^2+p1(x)w+p0(x)
     =k0*w*(x-z)^2+x[g2(x)w^2+g1(x)w+g0(x)],
where
 g0(x)=a+b*x, a=(C+D+1/2)L^2+A H^2, b=A H^2,
 g2(x)=D+[2Dr+(n+1/2)r^2]x+f*x^2,
 f=(D+n+1/2)r^2,
 c=n r^2,
 g1(x)=[p1(x)-k0(x-z)^2]/x.
The last quotient is polynomial since p1(0)=k0*z^2.
The matrix in question is M(x)=[[g0,g1/2],[g1/2,g2]].

Put G=g2(z), Gp=g2'(z), delta=x-z. Exact contact and first stationarity
give
 g0(z)=y^2 G, g1(z)=-2yG,
 g0'(z)+y g1'(z)+y^2 Gp=0.
Thus, since g1 has quadratic coefficient c,
 g0=y^2G+b*delta,
 g2=G+Gp*delta+f*delta^2,
 g1=-2yG-(b/y+yGp)*delta+c*delta^2.
Direct multiplication proves
 4 det M(x)=(x-z)^2 [Q0+Q1(x-z)-c^2(x-z)^2],          (1)
 Q0=4y^2Gf+4yGc-(b/y-yGp)^2,
 Q1=4bf+2c(b/y+yGp).
Here Q1>=0 but Q0 need not be nonnegative. The negative leading
coefficient is -n^2 r^4 when n>0.

2. The proposed original subtraction already fails next to contact

A simple limiting explanation: hold admissible positive z,y,H,t fixed
and let r tend to zero through positive values. GE1 has n=O(1/r),
while A,D approach positive finite values. Consequently c,f,Gp tend
to zero but b/y stays positive. Hence Q0 tends to -(b/y)^2<0.
All printed gaps and n>0 hold near r=0 when the other positive
parameters are chosen as in the following exact family.

For a completely rational one-parameter specialization take
 H=t=1/2, z=y=r=s, L=s/2, 0<s<=min(epsilon/2,1/1024).
Then

All denominators are positive. In particular

       [s(1+s)(1+3s)(4s^2+s+3)]>0,
since its numerator is >=1-15s^2>0. The factors 1-s in m,D
are positive, and the GE1 formulas make m,C,A,D,alpha positive.
The source rates satisfy z,y<=epsilon H.

Exact substitution in (1) gives
