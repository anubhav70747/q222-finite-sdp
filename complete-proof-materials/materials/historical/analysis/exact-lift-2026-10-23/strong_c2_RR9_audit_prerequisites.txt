Strong-C2 RR9: prerequisites for auditing a proposed finite completion
====================================================================
Public input: github-q222-finite-sdp/latex/certificates/c2_short.tex.
Only the indicated source and testing formulas were read; no new
construction or announced Pro result is assumed. No numerical work.

1. Parent and coefficient conventions
------------------------------------
Lines 3-20: independent real closed source/testing fields Kc,Kt; d=1,
F=4P, with full state (W,X,Y,Z). The native C2 source has lambda=beta=0,
n>=0 and m,C,A,D,alpha>0 (lines 46-55).
Use eq:c2-master-parent (15-20), or the exact normalized form RR3
(1543-1555): F/(4DuDv) is
  nX^2+m(bcW-bdX+acY-adZ)^2+CE^2+AY^2
  +(D+1/2)J^2+(alpha+1/2)Q^2+(L^2-Z^2)/2,
where E=aW-bY, K=aX-bZ, L=bX+aZ, J=dE+cK, Q=cY-dZ.
Here a=u/sqrt(Du), b=1/sqrt(Du), c=v/sqrt(Dv), d=1/sqrt(Dv).
The letter d in these homogeneous testing coordinates is NOT the source
coefficient d=1. Likewise RR2's beta_0 is a real cap below alpha, not the
original beta event coefficient.

2. Exact source domain behind RR9
---------------------------------
GE1 and definitions: lines 4506-4530; II specialization DI7: 4596-4625.
Source contact coordinates are z=u_c^2>0,y=v_c^2>0 infinitesimal,
H=z*h_src in (0,1), L=y*t_src>0, B=z*b_src=H+L a positive bounded unit,
A_c=1+zr, t=L/y, j=A_c-t>0, 0<r<1,
G=B+z(H+r-1)>0, B-zyr>0, and n>=0. The six original rational weights are exactly GE1,
not independently variable approximations. Contact and stationarity
are native-entry inputs (1494-1495).
RR1-RR2 (1507-1531): with h=res H=res B in (0,1],
  Cyt ~ Dyj ~ h/[2(1+h)], A/y ~ 1/[2h(1+h)],
  gamma=yCD/(C+D) ~ h/[2(1+h)].
C,D are unbounded. If h<1, alpha ~ h^2/[2(1-h^2)]; if h=1, alpha
is unbounded. For any real 0<h0<h, beta_0=h0^2/[2(1-h0^2)]<alpha.
No lower bound is imposed on r,t,j,1-H; their arbitrarily small positive
openings and literal n=0 are included. This does NOT assert literal
r=0,t=0,j=0,H=1, or C=D=alpha=0 in the rational GE1 formulas.

3. What RR9 actually decomposes
--------------------------------
Remote strip: v infinitesimal (possibly zero), u noninfinitesimal
(possibly infinity), so a is a testing unit (1581-1589).
Put s=C+D, theta=D/s, N=sqrt(s)(E+theta vK),
V=vK/sqrt(y), q=sqrt(y)Y, x=bX, z0=Z, ell=vY.
RR5 is the exact harmonic completion; RR6 is
  a*x*ell-b^2*z0*ell=b*V*q.
RR7 (1601-1607) retains ALL of
  nDvX^2, m{b(vW-X)+a(vY-Z)}^2, N^2, Cv^2E^2,
  (E+vK)^2/2, (alpha-beta_0)(ell-z0)^2.
The remainder G is RR8, and RR9 (1623-1635) is the exact identity
  G=Dv*M_k + gamma V^2+(A/y)Dv q^2-kDv*b*V*q
                       -v^2(beta_0+1/2)(ell-z0)^2.
The M_k Gram on (x,z0,ell), writing t0=beta_0+1/2, is
  [[1/2,a/2,ka/2],
   [a/2,t0-b^2/2,-t0-kb^2/2],
   [ka/2,-t0-kb^2/2,t0]].
The other block on (V,q) is
  [[gamma,-kDv*b/2],[-kDv*b/2,(A/y)Dv]].
The final negative rank-one term belongs to the first block and cannot
be omitted. The identity must hold with all four independent state
coordinates, not just on a contact graph or at a state minimizer.

4. Testing cover and literal boundaries
---------------------------------------
RR3-RR4 (1533-1579): if v is noninfinitesimal, c is a unit and the
three-root strict Gram covers EVERY u, including u=0,infinity, and
v=infinity. RR9 covers v infinitesimal, u noninfinitesimal, including
v=0 and u=infinity (1615-1678). The double-infinitesimal chart, including
u=v=0, is the separate local LII theorem (1195-1204,1482-1495).
Homogeneous endpoints mean (a,b)=(1,0) or (c,d)=(1,0), modulo simultaneous
sign. Use the original parent, not a raised-degree graph limit. Both
finite testing signs are included. The strong-II/HH reflection and its
full homogeneous identity are at 63-77 and 1681-1688; the B-infinitesimal
weak-source face is explicitly excluded from RR's scope.

5. Current pointwise choices versus a fixed source-affine completion
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For res b=b0 in (0,1), RR10-RR12 (1637-1663) choose a real h0<h
depending on b0, then k=-kappa in the displayed strict interval.
For b infinitesimal or zero, RR13 (1665-1676) uses k=-1 and the three
exact squares. These are pointwise strict-residue arguments; they do
not already supply one source-only auxiliary tuple valid for all tests.
A proposed fixed completion must prove its own quantifier order:
  for each source, choose auxiliary coefficients, then cover ALL tests.
Its matrices must be affine in the source and auxiliary variables.
Harmonic gamma, sqrt(C+D), source y, and the cap/k choices are not
themselves affine source expressions. They may serve as source-only
witness assignments, but their embedding and every product relation
must be explicit. Testing functions must be a fixed source-independent
family. Pure-source inverses are legal; inverses of mixed expressions
and identifying testing u^2,v^2 with contact z,y are not.

6. Restoring the two omitted event weights
------------------------------------------
For nonnegative openings lambda_event,beta_event, keep the same base
source and add exactly
  4 lambda_event Du(W+vX)^2 + 4 beta_event Dv(X+uZ)^2
to F. In RR3 this adds lambda_event(dW+cX)^2+beta_event L^2.
In RR7's F/(4Du) normalization it adds
  lambda_event(W+vX)^2+beta_event Dv L^2.
Both are retained source-positive squares, including literal zero and
unbounded event weights; no division by either opening is needed.
This proves monotone extension from an admissible GE1 base. Removing
lambda,beta from an arbitrary physical tuple need not leave such a base.
For d>0 arbitrary, first divide every source coefficient by d and restore
the pure-source positive factor d. A claim at d=0 needs its own event-face
certificate. Any exported full-state/pencil claim must state these
source-domain and event-restoration conditions explicitly.
