  h:(A0,A1,B0,B1) -> (A0,-A1,-B1,-B0).
The latter acts by i->7-i and swaps
  (lambda n)(m C)(A alpha)(D beta).
Arbitrary CHSH rotations followed by recanonicalizing this same zero

Alice/Bob reciprocal normalizations within canonical C1.
If S is not required to stay fixed, the128 physical relabellings have
four set stabilizers of these two raw events: independent choices of

2. COMPLETE NATIVE TRANSPORT
Write x=k^2, theta=1-h, a=(1-r)/eta, t=theta/zeta and
  rho=r+eta, G=h+zeta, V=1+x*eta*zeta,
  E=1-x*eta*h, Q=1-x*zeta*r,
  M=1+x*r-t, J=1+x*h-a.
Then h has the exact rational action
  x'=1/x,
  eta'=x*zeta*rho/G,   zeta'=x*eta*G/rho,
  r'=Q/G,             h'=E/rho,
  a'=M/(x*rho),       t'=J/(x*G),
  M'=a*V/(x*G),       J'=t*V/(x*rho),
  E'=h*V/G,           Q'=r*V/rho.
All strict native gaps are preserved. In particular r',h' are in(0,1)
because G-Q=zeta*M>0 and rho-E=eta*J>0. The transformation is an
involution. The extra oriented estimates a,t<2 need not survive when
x'>1; positivity of the exact gaps does survive.

For the native contact psi=(S,-kh,kSr,1), S=sqrt(zeta/eta),
the unnormalized transformed state is (kG,-SE,Q,kS*rho). Its last
coordinate is positive, and normalization gives S'=G/(S*rho), k'=1/k,
with precisely r',h' above. This proves actual-contact transport.
The useful invariant and scale identities are
  v_c'^2=x'*eta'*zeta'=x*eta*zeta=v_c^2,
  L'=x'*zeta'=eta*G/rho,
  (x*eta)'=zeta*rho/G,
  e'=rho/(e*G), where e=k*sqrt(eta/zeta).
These show why eta and zeta cannot generally be interchanged without
their shape factors.

3. ACTION ON EXISTING SIZE BRANCHES
I=positive infinitesimal,U=positive bounded unit,O=unbounded.
For k I, h sends k to O, outside every canonically oriented partition
row. Applying the orientation again returns the original source.
This includes every small-k C1.1/C1.4 branch, whatever L or eta does.

For k U the physically permitted eta/zeta types in the first source
partition and NI7--NI10 transform as follows:
  (I,I) -> (I,I), exchanging relative state scale up to positive units;
  (U,I) <-> (I,U), the already-used NI7 representative exchange;
  (U,U) -> (U,U), already in the five-consecutive-event exit;
  (O,U) -> (O,U), since rho~eta and G is a unit;
  (U,O) -> (U,O), since rho is a unit and G~zeta;
  (O,O) -> (O,O), since rho~eta and G~zeta.
In the first row rho,G are units and Delta'=G/(Delta*rho), so its
unbounded/bounded reciprocal state subcases are precisely the AS5
exchange already used. The non-unit shape factors in the last rows
prevent a naive eta<->zeta swap.

4. APPLICABILITY AND LIMIT
Both new completions require x small (quantitatively x<=a fixed
epsilon/(1+z), or qualitatively x infinitesimal). But every oriented
source has 0<x<=1, hence its only nontrivial stabilizer image has x'>=1.
It therefore fails their x hypothesis for every source, independently
of the other openings. A formal change from small L to unit L' does
not repair this failure. There is no symmetry-based deletion of another
small-k source branch. Since v'=v, inverse62's reciprocal-Bob strip is
also unchanged; only its Alice testing interval is inverted. The
all-tests theorem already needs no further Alice testing reduction.

This is the stabilizer of the designated generic zero edge {lambda,n}.
Choosing another edge on an additional-zero boundary is a different
entry problem, not a third generic stabilizer; no extra reduction on
such faces is claimed here. The present map exchanges the two retained
event-zero faces exactly.
