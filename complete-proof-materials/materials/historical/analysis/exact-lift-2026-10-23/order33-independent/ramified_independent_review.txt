Independent bounded review: ramified order-49 completion and two exact rescues
23 October 2026

VERDICT. No mathematical blocker found in the ramified construction, its
coefficient elimination, the sparse pencil, either rational rescue, or the
forty-function compact representation. This review does not audit the
order-33 separating functional and does not establish universal adequacy.

1. Exact cone and elimination

For a symmetric quartic Q(s) positive semidefinite on s>=0, apply real-line
matrix Fejer--Riesz to Q(t^2), of degree <=8. Degree <=4 factors, followed
by real/imaginary splitting and t-parity averaging, give
 Q(s)=R_even(s)^T R_even(s)+s R_odd(s)^T R_odd(s),
with degrees <=2 and <=1. Their coefficient Grams have orders 12 and 8.
This proof includes singular, degree-deficient, and zero matrices. Thus the
two Gram pairs are equivalent to the specified quartic-in-s selector
condition, not merely sufficient for it. The package could add these two
sentences where it currently invokes matrix half-line factorization.

The displayed parameterization solves all five coefficient identities:
Q0=A00, Q1=A01+A01^T+B00, Q2=A02+A02^T+A11+B01+B01^T,
Q3=A12+A12^T+B11, Q4=A22. The two skew matrices are needed and present.
The 64 freedoms per side plus 20 selector coefficients give 148 auxiliaries.
After selector elimination there are 80 equations in 228 symmetric Gram
entries; no additional equations remain in the actual affine pencil.
I checked the orders (12,8,12,8), source signs, upper-triangle convention,
411 rational coefficient records, and the exported auxiliary reconstruction.

Both inclusions hold for this specified cone. Positive Gram matrices imply
the two-LMI identity for s>=0 and hence the full parent. Conversely a
quartic selector with PSD completion matrices has the stated half-line
Grams, and their coefficient entries have exactly the displayed free
parameterization. A C33 certificate embeds directly after v=16s^2:
its [I,vI] square uses the 0 and 2 coefficient slots of A, and its vT term
is the PSD addition 16T to A11; B may be zero. No strictness is needed.

2. The exact rescues

The bundled source JSON matches the current public
strict_outer_physical_source.json byte for byte; the ramified cstar vector
equals those coefficients with lambda=n=0 inserted. The cbar data match the stated
all-positive source. I independently reconstructed all four congruence
Grams from the face matrices, checked their rational reduced LDL factors
with every pivot >2^-18, and checked both 148-coordinate affine lifts.
Every diagonal scaling inverted in this reconstruction is a nonzero fixed
rational constant. The cbar Grams equal the cstar Grams plus the supplied
nonnegative rational event outer products; the other two Grams are unchanged.
No solver status or old large certificate is used in this PSD argument.

3. Forty compact functions and the exact target

I rederived the finite features from eta=L(u)psi and the split with
D_U=diag(1,1,u^2,u^2). Their unnormalized Gram sum is u^2*Du*F.
Dividing by Du^(3/2)*sqrt(Dv) gives (u^2/Du)*F/(Du*Dv).
For the compact cover, s=(rho/tau)^2, and the five Bob factors become
g=(tau^4,rho^2*tau^2,rho^4)/kappa and
r=(rho*tau^3,rho^3*tau)/kappa. The printed P_G,P_H are exactly the
corresponding cubic Alice numerators. Thus the target is a1^2*Fh on
a0^2+a1^2=1, not the unmultiplied physical slack.

My checker imports no archive code. It independently expands the original
homogeneous SG2 parent and the 40 literal JSON functions as multivariate
Fraction polynomials. For all 157 source/auxiliary basis matrices and both
epsilon signs it proves the stronger polynomial identity before imposing
the Alice circle:
 kappa^2*featureGram = a1^2*(a0^2+a1^2)*kappa^2*Fh.
All 314 coefficient-basis identities pass; there are no numerical angle tests.

The cover is complete for the two projective angle pairs. Its denominator
is pure testing and kappa^2>=rho^8+tau^8>=1/8. Each Alice component has
coefficient-vector norm <=1 on the Alice circle; each Bob monomial numerator
has modulus <=1. Hence every feature is bounded by sqrt(8)<3 on unit states.
The endpoint g/r values and Alice polynomial specializations are correct.
At a1=0 every feature vanishes: validity there follows by continuity from
the dense a1!=0 set, not by cancellation of this zero multiplier. Both
epsilon sectors and all state signs are retained, including complex states
by real/imaginary splitting. All testing functions and their algebraic cover
are source-independent; no mixed inverse is introduced.
