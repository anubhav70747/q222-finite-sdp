------------------------------------------------

checks their stated congruence from all six hatted matrices, and parses the
six printed TeX matrices to confirm exact agreement with the data. It
forms the four dual blocks M_G,M_H,R_G,R_H and computes fresh exact LDL
pivots. Every pivot is positive. Supplied LDL factors are not relied on.
The full order-33 dual is their direct sum with nine scalar zero blocks.

The checker contracts that dual with EVERY printed COO coefficient,
using the actual full-symmetric storage convention once per entry. For

3. Why every polynomial selector is excluded
-------------------------------------------
The conclusion is stronger than failure of one chosen degree. If any
polynomial J(v) made G_J(v) PSD for all v>=0, its two-by-two principal
minors would imply
  |J_ij(v)|^2 <= (P+K)_ii(v)(N+K)_jj(v)=O(v^4).
For a fixed source the right side is a fixed polynomial of degree at
most four. A nonzero polynomial J_ij of degree greater than two cannot
satisfy that bound as v tends to positive infinity. Thus J has degree
at most two.

For a quadratic real symmetric matrix polynomial nonnegative on the
half-line, the univariate matrix-SOS theorem gives exactly
  Q(v)=[I;vI]^T S[I;vI]+vT, S>=0,T>=0.
One can see the degree and form by factoring Q(s^2), which is an even
globally PSD quartic matrix polynomial, then averaging its polynomial
SOS at s and -s and separating even and odd roots. No nonsingularity
assumption is required. Therefore any polynomial selector would produce
the four Gram blocks of the evaluated order-33 compiler, contradicted by
the exact separating trace. The result excludes polynomial dependence
on the native v coordinate of every degree; it does not exclude rational,
piecewise, or ramified algebraic selectors.
