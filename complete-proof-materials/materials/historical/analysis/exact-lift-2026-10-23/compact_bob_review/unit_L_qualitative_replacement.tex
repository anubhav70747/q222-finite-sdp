\subsection{Unit \texorpdfstring{$L$}{L} and unbounded \texorpdfstring{$\eta$}{eta}: all testing angles}
\label{component:pb0d9d28d0849}
\label{component:pda4a21937f64}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:c1-unit-L-all-tests}
Keep the native C1 source with $d=1$, $x,\sigma=1/\eta$ positive
infinitesimal, $L=x\zeta$ a positive bounded unit, and all original
strict gaps and nonnegative event coefficients. The complete parent
is a tensor sum of squares at every independent projective angle pair
and every real or complex state, including either permitted face
$D=0$ or $\beta=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The source formulas (P1) and opening-safe estimates (BE3)--(BE9) give
\[
 C/C_0,A/A_0,\alpha/a_0\sim1,\qquad
 C_0=\frac1{2L(1-r)},\quad
 A_0=\frac{1+L(1-r)}{2Lr},\quad
 a_0=\frac L{2(1-Lr)}.
\]
Choose real $0<\ell\le L\le U$ and a real
$0<\mu\le\min(1/4,C,A)$. Such a $\mu$ exists uniformly through
the source openings because $C_0,A_0\ge1/(2U)$.

First let $u$ be bounded. Put
$c=(1+v^2)^{-1/2}$, $s=vc$, $P=uW$, $T=cZ$.
When $u,c$ are both infinitesimal, $w=1/v$ is infinitesimal and
Theorem~\ref{thm:c1-unification-bounded-z-all-H} applies, since
$z=1/L$ is bounded. This includes the literal corner.
Otherwise put $(u_0,c_0,s_0)=\operatorname{res}(u,c,s)$ and choose
real $M\ge1$, $\tau>0$ with $|u_0|\le M$ and
$u_0^2+c_0^2\ge\tau^2$. The next reserve calculation is evaluated
at this real testing center, so its matrices are pure source.

The leading selected quarter-parent in the four raw roots $(P,X,Y,T)$ is
\[
 S_0=C_0(P-Y)^2+A_0(1+u^2)Y^2
                   +a_0(1+u^2)(sY-T)^2+K_B/2,
\]
\[
 K_B=X^2+c^2(P-Y)^2+s^2Y^2-2sPT
 +2ucX(sP-sY+T)+u^2s^2X^2+u^2(sY-T)^2.
\]
Its $T=0$ block is strictly positive. Its determinant is
\[
 \det G_0=\frac{(1+u^2)^2(Lc^2+u^2s^2)
                 \{1+L(1-r)\}^2}{16L^2r(1-r)(1-Lr)}>0.
\]
All source-opening denominators cancel from the cofactor ratio:
\[
 \operatorname{tr}G_0^{-1}
 =\frac{2(L^2N_2+LN_1+N_0)}
 {(1+t)\{Lq+t(1-q)\}\{1+L(1-r)\}},\quad t=u^2,\ q=c^2,
\]
where, writing $e_r=1-r$,
\begin{align*}
 N_2&=1+r^2+e_rq\{1+r(1-q)\}+e_r(2-qr)t+e_r^2(1-q)t^2,\\
 N_1&=2e_rq+\{2-rq(3-q)\}t+e_r(1-q)t^2,\qquad
 N_0=1+(2-q)t.
\end{align*}
The numerator is bounded by a real constant depending only on $U,M$.
Moreover $(1+t)\{Lq+t(1-q)\}\ge\min(\ell,1)(q+t)$.
Thus $G_0\succeq\kappa I$ for some real $\kappa>0$, uniformly
through every source opening.

Set $d_s=|C/C_0-1|+|A/A_0-1|+|\alpha/a_0-1|$ and $f=1-d_s$.
Retain the exact original event excesses $C-fC_0,A-fA_0,\alpha-fa_0$.
The remaining actual form is exactly $fS_0+(1-f)K_B/2$.
The core at the real center has bounded coefficients, whereas $d_s$
is source infinitesimal. Its actual source Gram therefore has a
positive real lower bound.

Use the moving event coordinates $e=(P-Y,Y,sY-T,X)$.
Both coordinate maps have squared Frobenius norm at most six.
Clip $C,A,\alpha$ at a sufficiently large real $N$, retaining each
excess as its whole original event. In these coordinates the core
has norm at most a real $K$, independently of source openings.
If the unmodified Gram has reserve $k>0$, its complementary block
remains at least $k$, its clipped large block is at least $N-K$,
and the off-diagonal norm is at most $K$. Taking
$N>K+2K^2/k+1$ preserves a positive real reserve, by
$2Kab\le(2K^2/k)a^2+(k/2)b^2$.

The clipped coefficients are bounded source elements. Its entire
live Gram, including both state normals, is a polynomial in bounded
testing $u,c,s$ in the moving coordinates $e$. Replacing its testing
coefficients by $u_0,c_0,s_0$ changes the Gram by bounded zero-residue
coefficients. The source matrix at that center has positive-definite
real residue by the preceding reserve.
The separated bounded-Gram lemma applies. Restore all clipped
excesses and the complete original $m,D,\beta$ terms.

It remains to treat unbounded $u$. Put $p=1/u$ and use two Bob charts.
For $|v|\le1$ take $(W,Y,B,R)$ with $B=pX+Z$, $R=vX$; the exact core is
\[
 K_p=B^2-2pvBW-2vBY+(1+p^2)R^2+2(1+p^2)RW
                         +(W-pY)^2+(1+p^2)v^2Y^2.
\]
Select only $C=A=\mu$. At $p=0$ the selected quarter-parent is
\[
 \mu(1+v^2)(W^2+Y^2)+\tfrac12(W+R)^2
                             +\tfrac12(B-vY)^2\succeq(\mu/3)I.
\]
For $|w|=|1/v|\le1$ use $(W,Y,X,H)$, $H=wZ$, and the exact core
\begin{align*}
 K_{pw}={}&H^2-2pHW+2pwHX-2HY+w^2W^2+2wWX-2pw^2WY\\
 &+(1+p^2+p^2w^2)(X^2+Y^2)-2pwXY.
\end{align*}
The corresponding selected value at $p=0$ is
\[
 \mu(1+w^2)(W^2+Y^2)+\tfrac12(X+wW)^2
                               +\tfrac12(H-Y)^2\succeq(\mu/3)I.
\]
In each chart every remaining coefficient is bounded infinitesimal.
The complete selected testing Gram is therefore positive definite
and can be factored entirely in the testing field. Restore $C-\mu$,
$A-\mu$ and all other original nonnegative event terms. The two
charts cover every Bob angle, including their literal endpoints.

All normalizations are positive pure testing factors. The exact
sign gauges are $u\mapsto-u$, $(Y,Z)\mapsto(-Y,-Z)$, and
$v\mapsto-v$, $(X,Z)\mapsto(-X,-Z)$; they preserve the full parent.
Real and imaginary splitting treats complex states. No source
coefficient $D$ or $\beta$ was divided by or capped.
\end{proof}
