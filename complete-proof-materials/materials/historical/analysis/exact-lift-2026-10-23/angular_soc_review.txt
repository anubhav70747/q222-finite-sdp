Independent algebra review: the eight-exponent Laurent coefficient cone
23 October 2026

VERDICT. The proposed order-49 affine lift is exact for
 K={ (h,q): q_e>=0, h+sum_{e in E}q_e U^{e_1}V^{e_2}>=0 for all U,V>0 },
 E={-1,0,1}^2\{0}. The earlier order-57 construction is also exact.
I first derived the three block projections and the reduced assembly from
the proposed formulas, then compared the minimal-face argument with the
existing global_weighted_certificate.tex. No new draft or checker was used.

1. EXACT CIRCUIT PROJECTIONS

For an opposite pair, the infimum of r1 U^e+r2 U^{-e} is
 k=2 sqrt(r1*r2). The proposed matrix [[2r1,s],[s,2r2]] is PSD precisely
when r1,r2>=0 and |s|<=k. Hence its circuit polynomial minus s is
nonnegative. Positive s=k is feasible, including zero coefficients.

For e+f+g=0, the infimum is k=3(r1*r2*r3)^(1/3). Put t=s/3.
The three PSD blocks imply x,y,t>=0, x^2<=r1*r2, y^2<=r3*t,
and t^2<=x*y. If t>0, these give t^3<=r1*r2*r3. Conversely,
if 0<t^3<=r1*r2*r3, choose x=sqrt(r1*r2) and y=t^2/x.
For t=0 choose x=y=0. Thus this lift projects exactly to
 r_i>=0, 0<=s<=k. No negative threshold is needed for necessity.

For e+f+2g=0, the infimum is
 k=4(r1*r2*(r3/2)^2)^(1/4).
With t=s/4, the two PSD blocks imply 0<=x<=sqrt(r1*r2) and
 t^2<=x*r3/2. These are equivalent to r_i>=0 and |s|<=k:
for sufficiency choose x=sqrt(r1*r2). Thus the r3/2 convention,
s/4 convention, and factors of two are all correct.

All three caps follow from weighted AM-GM. For a triangle the two
independent exponents allow equality at a positive point when all
coefficients are positive. A zero coefficient gives cap zero and
infimum zero; the block proofs above cover this case directly.

2. REDUCED ASSEMBLY AND NECESSITY

Keep only the 24 triangle incidences as allocation variables. Define
 R_e=q_e-sum_{triangles C containing e}r_Ce.
Use the unique opposite-pair block for e and -e with diagonal entries
2R_e,2R_{-e}; keep all triangle blocks and the scalar h+sum_C s_C.
Triangle allocations are nonnegative from their own block diagonals;
R_e>=0 follows from the pair block. Thus q_e>=0 is itself implied.
Every coefficient is allocated exactly, and
 L=(h+sum_C s_C)+sum_C(sum_{e in C}r_Ce U^e-s_C).
The preceding circuit bounds prove sufficiency.

For necessity, h>=0 has the immediate witness: zero triangle
allocations and thresholds, pair diagonals 2q_e. For h<0, use the
minimal face J of the active exponent hull containing the origin.
An exposing ray discards off-face terms without changing the face
terms. On span(J), the face exponential sum has an attained minimum.
At its minimizer a=(U_c,V_c), w_e=q_e a^e is balanced. Decompose w
into positive circuits and allocate r_Ce=lambda_C b_Ce/a^e, with
threshold s_C=lambda_C sum_e b_Ce. Each circuit is exactly at its
AM-GM cap, and h+sum_C s_C is the nonnegative face minimum.
Off-face coefficients and any remaining capacity may be placed in
their opposite-pair diagonals; adding nonnegative diagonal entries
preserves PSD. This proves necessity even for minima approached only
at infinity. If zero is outside the active hull, h<0 is impossible.

The primitive circuits are exactly four opposite pairs, four triangles
{(a,b),(-a,0),(0,-b)} with weights (1,1,1), and four triangles of two
adjacent corners and their opposite axial point with weights (1,1,2).
No extra circuit or threshold-sign constraint is missing.

3. COUNTS, CONVENTIONS, AND SCOPE

There are 4+4*3+4*2=24 order-two blocks and one scalar block:
total PSD order 49. Auxiliaries are 24 triangle allocations,
12 thresholds, 8 ordinary-triangle x/y variables, and 4 weighted
triangle x variables: 48. There are no coefficient equations left.
All block entries are affine over the rationals in the nine inputs
(h,q) and these auxiliaries. The earlier version has 32 allocations,
56 auxiliaries, and eight additional scalar residual blocks, hence
order 57, as claimed.

The result is an exact affine semidefinite lift of this Laurent
coefficient cone (indeed using only order-two PSD cones and one ray).
Substituting the existing quartic coefficient map gives an exact
quartic pullback test on the canonical source domain where the
determinant/validity equivalence was already proved. It does not make
that substitution affine, and does not establish an affine lift of
the full Bell-source cone or Q222. No solver or script was run, and
no broader proof or source-map audit is claimed here.
