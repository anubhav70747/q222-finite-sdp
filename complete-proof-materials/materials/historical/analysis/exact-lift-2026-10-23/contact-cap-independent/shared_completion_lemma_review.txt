SHARED EXTRACTION LEMMA FOR THE TWO CONTACT COMPLETIONS
Private bounded review, 24 October 2026

There is one useful common lemma. It combines the fixed-gate identity with
the passage from source-dependent rows to fixed testing spans. It removes
duplicated extraction reasoning, but not the two different completion proofs.

Lemma (fixed testing functions from a uniform matrix reserve).
Let X be a fixed testing/state domain; let D(x)>0 and fixed real function
columns Psi(x),Theta(x) satisfy Theta=E Psi for a fixed matrix E.
Let g_1,...,g_m be fixed functions with |g_k|<=1 on X. Suppose, for each
admitted source s,
  D S_s=f_s^T Q_s f_s+Psi^T R_s Psi,
  f_s=A_s Theta, R_s>=0,
  Q_s=Q_s,0+sum_k B_s,k g_k,
where every matrix subscripted s depends only on the source, Q_s is symmetric,
Q_s,0>=mu I, ||B_s,k||op<=rho_k, and sum rho_k<mu.
Then S_s has a positive Gram representation in the fixed blocks
  Psi/sqrt(D),
  sqrt(1+g_k) Theta/sqrt(D), sqrt(1-g_k) Theta/sqrt(D).
No boundedness of A_s or R_s is required.

Proof. Put V_s=Q_s,0-sum rho_k I and
W_s,k^±=(rho_k I±B_s,k)/2. All these matrices are PSD, and
Q_s=V_s+sum[W_s,k^+(1+g_k)+W_s,k^−(1-g_k)].
The base Gram is E^T A_s^T V_s A_s E+R_s; the signed gate Grams are
A_s^T W_s,k^± A_s. Substitution proves the identity. The argument uses no
inverse of A_s or positive lower bound on its singular values. Therefore
zero rows, zero source weights and vanishing gates cause no exception.

Weak substitution.
Use Contact_Completion.tex equations eq:complete,eq:f,eq:retained,eq:RFN19,

  D=Dv; Psi=psi34; Theta=psi[I_*] (the displayed16-element sublist);
  f_s=the eleven rows in eq:f;
  g_ab=(2^12 u)^a(2^25 v)^b;
  B_s,ab=2^(-12a-25b)Q_ab; rho_ab=2^(-12a-25b)r_ab.
The eight pairs and r_ab are precisely eq:bounds. Their sum is <1/1024,
and mu=1/4, giving reserve >=255/1024.
The eleven displayed supports supply A_s. R_s is the positive base Gram
of the retained term,16x²y W and the two E remainders in eq:complete;
eq:RFN19 and the fixed-span paragraph explicitly supply its roots/matrices.
Thus the lemma produces base34 plus sixteen order16 gate blocks.
The domain is the fixed positive cap with w*zeta>=0. Literal n=0 causes a
zero fourth row; the lemma makes no division by that row or its coefficient.

Editorial use and limit.
State this lemma once, then replace weak Lemma lem:gates and its concluding
span-to-Gram paragraph, and the strong final section's repeated gate proof
and pushforward explanation, by the two substitutions above. Keep all source
bounds, harmonic purchases, monic divisions, conductor and six-row shear:
they establish the lemma's hypotheses and do not follow from it.
The weak sign/core argument and remote gluing also remain separate.
This is an actual shared extraction argument, not a shared source-admission
theorem; it yields no admission outside the proved native domains and no
full canonical coverage.
