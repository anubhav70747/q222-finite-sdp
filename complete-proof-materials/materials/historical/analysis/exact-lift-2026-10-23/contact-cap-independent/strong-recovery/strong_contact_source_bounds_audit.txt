 1<=w_Delta:=Delta/[H(1+H)]<=1+10epsilon.             (1)

3. Why the inspected interval-bound code is mathematically valid

After the exact conductor and shear, set hh=sqrt(H). The admitted source
lies in hh in[1/64,1], |r|<=1, |tt|<=2, and the SC11 error box.
The replacements zz=z/H and kk=sqrt(y/H) have bounds epsilon and
sqrt(epsilon)=2^-32. Every native source substitution in bound_lII.py
matches SC10 exactly.

For each rational entry, source=entry-lead and testing=lead-base split
the actual difference from Q_*. The script expands their numerators
only after rational cancellation. The ONLY surviving denominator
factors are
 const * hh^a*(1+hh^2)^b*(1+db)^c*(1+ds)^d.
The complete replay found no unexpected factor (the code raises on one).
Here const is positive in every nonzero bound, as also verified by
nonnegative exact receipts. All factors are positive; 1+hh^2>=1 and
1+db,1+ds>=1/2 on the proved source box.

For each numerator monomial, the code subtracts the denominator
hh exponent before bounding. A negative net exponent is maximized
at hh=1/64; a nonnegative one is bounded by1. Every other variable
is bounded by its displayed rational absolute endpoint. The sum of
absolute term bounds is therefore a rigorous rational upper bound,
including errors of either sign. No cancellation in a sign-changing
testing variable is presumed.

For each symmetric coefficient matrix, summing diagonal bounds once
and off-diagonal bounds twice dominates its operator norm. That is
exactly how rational_bound and groups aggregate their records. The
latter also collects each testing exponent separately and includes
the corresponding testing-cap scale in its bound. This justifies
the coefficient norms, not merely a pointwise PSD test.

The independent source/identity reviewer confirmed that dp,ts,js
occur only in exact contact remainders and not in generic Q, so their
absence from the bound script's substitution is legitimate. Native
remainder checks justify dropping them on the actual source.

5. Limits of the audited result

This verifies the quantitative strong Gram admission on precisely
H>=2^-12,z,y<=2^-64H and the fixed small testing box. The formulas
introduce no lower bounds on r,t,j,1-H or on n; n=0 is literal because
the scaled variable nu=nr is never inverted. The full source-only

Combining this quantitative result with the separately checked

change its endpoint conventions, prove a new global fixed family,
