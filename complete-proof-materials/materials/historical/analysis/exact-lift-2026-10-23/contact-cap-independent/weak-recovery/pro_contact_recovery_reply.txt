### 1. Domain and exact source change

Use the original native **GE1** source, normalized by \((d,\lambda,\beta)=(1,0,0)\), with
\[

\]
Set
\[
B_s=H_o+y_ot_o,\quad h_s=H_o/B_s,\quad c_s=1+z_or_o,\quad R_s=1+h_sz_o,
\]
\[
B=B_sR_s/c_s,\quad j=z_oc_s/(B_sR_s),\quad
T=y_oc_s/(B_sR_s),\quad p=z_o(1-r_o)/(B_sR_s),\quad H=t_o/c_s.
\]

\[
x=jB=z_o,\quad y=TB=y_o,\quad p/j=(1-r_o)/c_s,

\[

### 3. Source inequalities needed by the admission proof
