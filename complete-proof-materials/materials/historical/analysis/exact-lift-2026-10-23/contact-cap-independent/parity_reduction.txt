Equivalent elimination of mixed radical terms
24 October 2026

The coefficient algebra has the independent square classes u, v and
w*zeta. Let the eight sign automorphisms change the signs of their three
formal square roots independently. Every prescribed feature has one
character under this finite group. The target polynomial and every signed
gate are fixed by the group.

If G is a feasible positive semidefinite Gram matrix, conjugate G by the
diagonal feature-character matrix for each automorphism and average the
eight matrices. Positivity is preserved. Applying the same automorphism
to the polynomial identity shows that every conjugate is feasible.
Orthogonality of the characters removes precisely those entries joining
different characters. Conversely, a matrix with these entries zero is
already an allowed original Gram matrix. Thus the projected source cones
are exactly equal. This argument is algebraic: it does not require giving
a negative value to a principal physical square root.

All strong features have trivial character. The weak base partitions into
index groups 1--26, 27--28, 29--32, 33--34. Each gated weak principal list
partitions into its first14 and last2 entries. Hence the new block orders
are20, 26 copies of11, then26,2,4,2, then16 pairs14,2. There are63 blocks,
of total order596, or605 including the9 source signs.

Exact filtering of the independently reconstructed coefficient arrays
gives4021 symmetric Gram variables,619 polynomial coefficient equations,
20610 Gram records and120 source records. All nontrivial-character source
rows are absent. Every dropped Gram record belongs to a cross-character
column, and every retained one belongs to a same-character column; the
script checks both implications for every record.

This reduces4697 variables and948 equations without changing the cone.
Adding the preserved remote matrices still gives total order635 and4024
auxiliaries. It does not establish source admission beyond the original
construction. In particular, no universal canonical coverage follows.

Reproduction: run reduce_parity.py after reconstruct.py.

constructs the reduced witness directly.
