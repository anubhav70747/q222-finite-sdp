Finite source limits
--------------------
The closure claim is valid for this fixed family without trusting an
unprovided bound. Each block's own feature list is linearly independent on
the strict real chart u,v>0,w*zeta>0 with all gates positive. For strong
blocks this follows from distinct state monomials before the invertible
state change; for weak blocks it follows from distinct monomials and the
independent radical parities. A common finite set of chart evaluations
therefore gives a positive-definite evaluation Gram in every block. Summing
the coefficient identity at those points uniformly bounds all PSD block
traces whenever source coefficients converge. Subsequence limits yield
a feasible limiting tuple. This is closedness after fixing the functions;
it neither selects functions nor proves any missing source admission.
