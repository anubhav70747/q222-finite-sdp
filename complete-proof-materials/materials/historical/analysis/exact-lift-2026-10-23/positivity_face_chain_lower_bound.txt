SINGLE-PSD-CONE ORDER LOWER BOUND FOR Q222
23 October 2026

Conclusion
==========
Every representation of the full eight-coordinate Q222 as an affine
projection of an affine section of Sym_N^+ has N >= 9.  Thus the fixed-order
synthesis search can discard N=4,...,8 as well as N<4.  With the previously
proved auxiliary bound p <= D-9, D=N(N+1)/2, the first unexcluded template
is N=9, D=45, p<=36, with at most D^2=2025 matrix coefficients.
This is a necessary lower bound, not an order-nine construction.

The proof is elementary: eight strict event-positivity face inclusions,
followed by a minimal-PSD-face argument.  No strict feasibility assumption
on the original lift is needed.  This does not lower-bound the largest
block of a product lift: it bounds the order of a single PSD cone, or the
sum of block orders after a block-diagonal embedding.

1. An exact nine-face flag
=========================
Write a deterministic vertex as four bits v=(a0,a1,b0,b1), with observable
values (-1)^a_x and (-1)^b_y.  For an event e=(x,y,a,b), its probability
at v is 1 exactly when a_x=a and b_y=b, and otherwise is 0.  For a general
behaviour q it is the affine functional

 p_e(q) = [1+(-1)^a A_x+(-1)^b B_y+(-1)^(a+b) E_xy]/4.

All sixteen p_e are nonnegative on Q222.  Put F0=Q222 and successively
impose the eight zero-event conditions in the following table:

 k   added zero probability     witness in F_(k-1)\F_k   surviving vertices
 1   P(1,0|0,0)=0               1000                    12
 2   P(0,1|0,0)=0               0010                     8
 3   P(1,1|0,1)=0               1011                     6
 4   P(1,1|1,0)=0               1110                     5
 5   P(1,1|0,0)=0               1010                     4
 6   P(1,1|1,1)=0               0101                     3
 7   P(0,1|0,1)=0               0001                     2
 8   P(1,0|1,0)=0               0100                     1

Here F_k is Q222 intersected with the first k zero conditions.  It is
an exposed face: it is the zero set of the sum of the first k nonnegative
probabilities.  Each displayed witness has every preceding event zero
and the newly added event equal to one.  Every F_k contains the vertex
0000.  Therefore

          F0 strictly contains F1 strictly contains ... strictly contains F8

is a chain of nine nonempty exposed faces.  No assertion about unlisted
quantum points on those faces is required for strictness or nonemptiness.

The checker enumerates all 65536 subsets of the sixteen zero conditions.
They give 1722 distinct survivor sets on deterministic vertices, including
the empty set.  A recursion deleting one event's four vertices at a time
finds a maximum of eight strict nonempty transitions.  Its 1721 states are
exactly all nonempty survivor sets.  Allowing several conditions at one
step cannot give a longer chain: insert them one at a time and discard
steps that leave the survivor set unchanged.  The table attains the
maximum.  Independently, every survivor set is the vertex set of an
exposed face of the eight-dimensional local polytope; a strict nonempty
face chain in an eight-dimensional convex set has at most nine terms.
Exact rational elimination also confirms dimensions 8,7,...,0 for the
classical faces along this flag.

2. Minimal PSD supports strictly decrease
=========================================
Let K be a convex set represented as pi(T), where

                T = A intersect Sym_N^+

for an affine subspace A of Sym_N and an affine map pi.  For any nonempty
convex subset C of T define its support space

                U(C) = sum_{X in C} range(X).

The smallest face of Sym_N^+ containing C is the set of PSD matrices with
range contained in U(C).  There is an X_C in C positive definite on U(C):
choose finitely many points whose ranges span U(C), and average them.
The kernel of a sum of PSD matrices is the intersection of their kernels.

Claim. If C is a face of T, D is a subset of T, C is a proper subset of
D, and both are nonempty, then U(C) is a proper subspace of U(D).
(We only apply this with both C and D faces of T.)

Proof. Inclusion is automatic.  If the support spaces were equal, choose
X_C as above.  For any Y in D, a sufficiently small theta in (0,1) makes
X_C-theta Y PSD: both matrices are supported on U(C), where X_C is
positive definite.  The matrix

                   Z = (X_C-theta Y)/(1-theta)

belongs to A, because its affine coefficients sum to one, and is PSD.
Thus Z belongs to T and X_C=theta Y+(1-theta)Z.  Since X_C belongs to the
face C of T, Y belongs to C.  This for all Y in D contradicts properness.
The claim follows.  In particular it is valid when T has no point positive
definite in the full ambient Sym_N: the proof works in its actual support.

For a nonempty face F of K, the inverse image

                  T_F = {X in T : pi(X) in F}

is a nonempty face of T.  A strict inclusion F strictly contains G gives
a strict inclusion T_F strictly contains T_G, because pi is onto K.
Consequently every strict nonempty face inclusion in K lowers the PSD
support dimension by at least one.

3. Compactness supplies the last positive rank
==============================================
Suppose K is compact and has more than one point.  Then 0 is not in T.
Indeed, if 0 were in A, the affine subspace A would be linear and T would
be a cone.  Writing pi(X)=q0+L(X), every X in T would give the ray
q0+t L(X), t>=0, in K.  Compactness would force L(X)=0 for every X in T,
so K would be the singleton {q0}, a contradiction.

Thus every nonempty T_F has support dimension at least one.  For the
nine-face flag constructed above, writing r_k=dim U(T_(F_k)) gives

             N >= r_0 > r_1 > ... > r_8 >= 1.

Therefore N>=9.  This proves the conclusion for every affine-section
PSD lift and handles projections and missing strict feasibility directly.

The same conclusion applies to a pencil presentation
K={q : exists y, A0+sum_i q_i A_i+sum_j y_j B_j >=0}.
For compact K, q is determined by the resulting matrix: if a kernel
direction changed q while leaving the matrix fixed, any feasible parameter
point would extend to a full feasible q-line.  Hence projection from the
affine space of pencil matrices is well-defined and the preceding argument
applies.  Extra affine equations can first be eliminated by parametrizing
their nonempty affine solution space.
