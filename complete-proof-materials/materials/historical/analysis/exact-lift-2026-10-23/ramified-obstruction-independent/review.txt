3. Validity transport and its retained assumptions
-------------------------------------------------
As requested, the already checked old cbar/cstar validity proof was not
repeated. The new transport is consistent with the full parent, rather
than an arbitrary permutation of source coordinates. Under
  (u,v;W,X,Y,Z) -> (-1/v,-1/u;W,Y,X,Z)
and clearing u^2 v^2, the event packets permute as
  lambda <-> C, n <-> A, alpha <-> beta,
with m,D fixed (their individual roots change sign). The negative core
term is invariant, and the three positive d terms permute or stay fixed.
Hence d is unchanged and the full polynomial source identity holds.
Homogenizing the angles gives the global substitutions
  a -> (b1,-b0), b -> (a1,-a0), psi -> (W,Y,X,Z),
without division. Axes, projective endpoints and all state signs are
therefore included. Thus canonical validity is invariant under
  pi=(0,4,5,3,1,2,6,8,7).
The own checker verifies cdagger=pi(cbar), the exact transported boundary
source, and cdagger>=pi(cstar) coordinatewise with d unchanged. Positivity
of cdagger follows from its displayed rational coordinates. On the
established validity inputs, both excluded sources are physically valid.

4. Slightly stronger scope: all polynomial degrees in the same s
----------------------------------------------------------------
There is an immediate extension beyond failure of a degree-four search.
If a polynomial selector J(s) of ANY degree made G_J(s),H_J(s) PSD for
all s>=0, every entry would obey the principal-minor bound
  |J_ij(s)|^2 <= (P+K)_ii(16s^2)(N+K)_jj(16s^2)=O(s^8).
The diagonal source blocks have degree at most four in s, so each
polynomial J_ij must have degree at most four. Half-line matrix-SOS
factorization of that quartic gives precisely the order-49 Gram form
with features (I,sI,s^2I) and sqrt(s)(I,sI), including singular cases.
Therefore this exact counterexample excludes EVERY polynomial selector
in the same coordinate s=sqrt(v)/4. Increasing its polynomial degree
cannot repair it. This argument is an analytical consequence of the
verified certificate, not a separate numerical test.

The result does not exclude rational selectors, finer algebraic
ramifications, Alice-dependent feature enlargements, or the qualitative
existence theorem. It does not prove that any particular new feature is
the unique necessary repair. Global order-392 strictness follows only
when combined with the separately established polar assembly theorem;
that geometric theorem was not re-audited in this task.
