These bounds are sign-independent in lambda, require neither H bounded

2. Raw full-parent identity. If p=1/u, define F_pw by homogeneous

       -2pE(G+w^2F)+2qwS(G-F),

This identity can be expanded directly in all four state coordinates.
The six event amplitudes are

with the original positive measurement factors. Thus no normal has been
lost by passing to the raw frame.

3/64+2^-22+2^-13<1/18. The base reserve therefore exceeds the full
perturbation. This is a coefficient bound, not a discarded-normal estimate.
