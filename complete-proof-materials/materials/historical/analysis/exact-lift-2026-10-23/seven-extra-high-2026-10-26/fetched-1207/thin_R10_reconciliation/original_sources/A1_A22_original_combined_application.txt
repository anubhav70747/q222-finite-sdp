1. Both contact charts, with distinct source names
To prevent collisions write w_s=ae for the source variable, omega for the independent testing coordinate, j_R for the actual R10 source gap, and j_s for the C1 contact parameter. Let
$s=1/a,\quad b=a^2B,\quad x=sb,\quad e=s w_s,\quad r=1-s w_s,\quad B=s^2b,\quad \rho=n/e^2>0.$R10.2 gives b bounded; TL1 gives b/w_s and w_s/b positive bounded units, with t,g,j_R units and s,h,x,e infinitesimal. Put
$K=1+k^2,\ k^2=x=sb,\quad R_1=1+hk^2,\quad Q=K^2/R_1,\quad E=tK/R_1,$$\delta_c^2=\frac{R_1^2}{tK^2b},\quad \varepsilon=\delta_c^{-1},\quad j_s=\delta_c k.
\tag{A1}$The original C1 source coordinates are (eta_1,zeta_1,r_1,t_1)=(t,s,h,w_s). Substitution into CA3, r_1=ell/Q, eta_1=KE/Q, zeta_1=delta_c^2 k^2 Q E/K, gives exactly
$\ell=hQ,\quad
s=j_s^2 QE/K=\frac{j_s^2tK^2}{R_1^2},\quad
k=\varepsilon j_s,\quad K_c=sK/Q=j_s^2E,
\tag{A2}$not merely equalities of residues. The inherited source coefficient and its denominator reduce to
$a_1=(K-\ell)/E=(1-h)/t,\quad
T_1=K-k^2e,\quad
h_1=a_1/T_1=\frac{1-h}{tT_1},\quad
p=w_s/R_1.
\tag{A3}$Here T_1 is a positive bounded unit. So are t, K, R_1, Q, E, 1-h, h+t, Q_c=1-hB, g, j_R, and the denominators of the native short polynomials P_kappa,P_D,P_alpha in TL3. The letter delta_c is the CA angular source scaling; Delta L=L_2-kappa L_1 is the entirely different coefficient difference (candidate text reused delta for both).
When b is a positive unit, delta_c and delta_c^{-1} are units and the CA dictionary is
$(A,B_0,C,D_0)=(\omega v,\delta_c v,\delta_c E u\omega,\delta_c^2k^3E).
\tag{A4}$It matches original CC with d=delta_c and b_CC^2=E; its contact is (u,omega,v)=(k,delta_c k,delta_c k^2 E).
When b is positive infinitesimal, epsilon,j_s are positive infinitesimals. Define the source-only unit tau=w_s/epsilon^2, which is a unit because epsilon^2=tK^2 b/R_1^2 and w_s/b is a unit. The SE dictionary is
$(A,B_0,C,D_0)=(\varepsilon\omega v,v,E u\omega,\varepsilon^2j_s^3E),\qquad
(u_c,\omega_c,v_c)=(\varepsilon j_s,j_s,\varepsilon j_s^2E).
\tag{A5}$Every CA monomial is exactly delta_c times the corresponding SE monomial. The soft differences and the two circuit radicals scale by the same factor; the original hard normals N,M_B do not. No CS1 optional scaling delta_c M_B is substituted into SE.
2. The precise bounded source coquotients
Use nu=n/rho=e^2, Xi=nu x, Lambda=Theta/rho=w_s^2/b, gamma=r/K_c. In CA,
$\Lambda=\frac{\delta_c^2QEw_s^2}{K},\quad
\nu\gamma^2=\frac{w_s^2r^2Q^2}{K^2},\quad
\nu\gamma=\frac{\delta_c^2Ek^2w_s^2rQ^2}{K^2},\quad
\frac{\Xi}{D_0^2}=\frac{w_s^2Q^2}{K^2}.
\tag{A6}$All are bounded in CA because w_s,b,delta_c,E,Q,K are units/bounded. In SE, substituting w_s=epsilon^2 tau and (A2) yields exactly
$\begin{aligned}
\Lambda&=\varepsilon^2\tau^2 QE/K,&
\nu&=\varepsilon^4j_s^4\tau^2Q^2E^2/K^2,\\
\nu\gamma^2&=\varepsilon^4\tau^2r^2Q^2/K^2,&
\nu\gamma&=\varepsilon^4j_s^2E\tau^2rQ^2/K^2,\\

p&=\varepsilon^2\tau/R_1.
\end{aligned}\tag{A7}$In particular gamma=r/(j_s^2 E) is unbounded but occurs only in the combinations of (A6)–(A7). The apparent powers 1/j_s are canceled before entering a coefficient ring. These are source identities, not estimates comparing source and test variables.
Write the TL3-derived starred coefficients as in the candidate:
$\begin{aligned}
c_*&=-\Lambda\frac{c_a}{1-h},&
a_*&=-\Lambda\frac{tc_a S_c}{K(h+t)},\\

\end{aligned}\tag{A8}$All five have bounded CA values and belong to epsilon^2 B_c in SE. Crucially, this alone is not enough for f_0: one must retain the last difference in (A8) exactly, giving
$\boxed{f_0=(z_*-d_0)\frac{D_u}{D_0^2}
=-\frac{\varepsilon^2\tau^2Q^2P_\alpha}{K^3Q_c}D_u\quad\text{(SE)}.}\tag{A9}$The canceled coefficient has only unit denominators. Declaring an arbitrary (z_*-d_0)/D_0^2 bounded is false without this extra native relation. Other scalar coefficients, both full first-normal rows and all hard entries are epsilon^2 times bounded forms by (A7)–(A8), (A3) and the explicit expansions in the next section. 3. Exact complete TL6 coefficient identity
Set D_u=1+u^2, D_v=1+v^2, A_t=v omega, V_0=1-puv omega, and use the literal original TL7 inverse, with Z=1:
$X=N+pD_uA_t-u,\quad q_B=v(D_u-uN-puD_uv\omega),$$W=\omega+\frac{u(M_B+h_1q_B)}{D_u},\quad
Y=u\omega-\frac{M_B+h_1q_B}{D_u}.
\tag{A10}$Hence uW-Y=M_B+h_1 q_B, R_{D,event}=M_B+(h_1-1)q_B and X+u=N+pD_uA_t exactly. After TL3 and retention of the complete TL4 contact-zero square and centered-A positive packet, the original quarter-parent TL6 divided by rho is
$\begin{split}
\mathscr E/\rho={}&
2\nu\gamma D_uD_v uA_t
-\nu D_uD_v(2\gamma pD_u+\gamma^2)A_t^2
-2\nu\gamma D_uD_v A_t N\\
&-\nu D_v(N+pD_uA_t)^2
+c_*D_v(M_B+h_1q_B)^2
+d_*(M_B+(h_1-1)q_B)^2\\
&+z_*D_u(vY-1)^2-d_0D_uD_v
+a_*D_uD_v(2(uW)Y-h(uW)^2).
\end{split}\tag{A11}$It is not enough to omit either the positive TL5 term 2 nu gamma D_u D_v u A_t or the scalar piece -d_0D_uv^2. They become, respectively, the +2nu gamma D_uD_v/(alpha_B alpha_C) term in f_BC and the -d_0D_u/alpha_B^2 term in f_BB.
For (alpha_A,alpha_B,alpha_C)=(1,delta_c,delta_c E) in CA and (epsilon,1,E) in SE, introduce the fully specified bounded linear forms
$\begin{array}{c|ccc}

Y&Y_B=-h_1V_0/\alpha_B &Y_C=1/\alpha_C &Y_N=h_1uv/D_u,\quad Y_M=-1/D_u
\end{array}\tag{A12}$where the first entry of the table denotes uW, not the source scale nu (the checker's code uses UW). Also write
$t_B=h_1D_uV_0/\alpha_B,\quad r_B=(h_1-1)D_uV_0/\alpha_B,\quad
t_N=-h_1uv,\quad r_N=-(h_1-1)uv.
\tag{A13}$Then (A11) is polynomial of degree at most two in the formal variables N,M_B. Its scalar support has exactly
$F_0=f_AA^2+f_{BB}B_0^2+f_{BC}B_0C+f_{CC}C^2+f_0D_0^2,$its two complete first-normal rows have the form

A direct symbolic collection of (A11) using (A12)–(A13) equals every one of the candidate's original coefficient formulas (12)–(15), including the p^2 contribution of -nu D_v(X+u)^2 and the z_*-d_0 term. The independent script verify_r10_TL6_and_conductor_hypotheses.py performs this universal coefficient identity check with algebraically independent source letters; the check is not a rational sample. 4. Exact formal jets, before residues or asymptotic reductions
The test contact (A5) is the same for both original parents. Its full projective state is
$\boxed{(W_c,X_c,Y_c,Z_c)=
\left(\frac{j_sK}{R_1},-kr,\frac{kj_sKh}{R_1},1\right).}\tag{A15}$Verify directly from (A10): at contact V_{0,c}=T_1/K, and (A2)–(A3) give
$pKj_s^2E=1-r,\qquad
h_1=\frac{1-h}{tT_1},\qquad E=\frac{tK}{R_1}.$Hence omega_c=j_s, N_c=M_{B,c}=0, and all four entries of (A15) are recovered exactly by the TL7 inverse. No residue is taken and no source gap set to zero.
Now use the algebraic (not positivity) content of OP4/OP5: both the actual native C2 parent and the formal C1 parent obey their complete four state-kernel and two angular stationarity equations at (A15). TL3 is the exact coefficient difference; TL4 and the centered-A excess are squares that vanish at the contact. Subtracting their full parents and square packets therefore gives the formal equalities
$F_{0,c}=\partial_uF_{0,c}=\partial_\omega F_{0,c}
=\partial_vF_{0,c}=0,\qquad
(L_N)_c=(L_M)_c=0.
\tag{A16}$These are before specialization to infinitesimal sizes and are full-state identities, since the map from the three state coordinates to (omega,N,M_B) has exact inverse (A10). The differentiation in (A16) does not turn any source parameter into a test parameter. This step depends explicitly on the exact TL3 and OP4/OP5 source identities; their independent auditing is outside the conductor theorem.
At contact A_c=kC_c, B_{0,c}=C_c\ne0, D_{0,c}=k C_c. Thus (A16) gives S_c=0 for the scalar's first conductor and both normal S_{N,c}=S_{M,c}=0 by cancellation in the source field, without inserting an inverse of C_c into a matrix coefficient. 5. CA: the original CC conductor applies literally
Use the CA source ring with d_CC=delta_c, b_CC^2=E. The source generators are bounded and delta_c,E,delta_c^{-1} are positive units because this branch has b a unit. Clear the finite source-unit denominators in (A11), and clear powers of D_u=1+u^2, a positive pure-testing unit, before division. These clearings are even in test angles and independent of omega and do not introduce mixed denominators.
Equations (A13)–(A14) give bounded polynomial coefficients with restricted scalar support (A,B_0,C)^2+(D_0^2) and both normals in (A,B_0,C). The contact jets (A16) give the exact CC premises. CC1–CC6 therefore write the scalar as a quadratic on (x_0,y_0,z_0,h_0,q_1) and each normal as a linear form in (x_0,y_0,z_0). Every quotient is monic; its coefficients are polynomials in bounded source/test generators, so all outputs are bounded, even if k and the testing angles approach zero at unrelated rates. Add the unused second circuit root q_2 and the original two hard normals. The resulting eight-root matrix is bounded. CA does not invoke SE by calling epsilon infinitesimal when it is a unit.
6. SE: the two scalar stages and both normal rows
Here epsilon,j_s are positive source infinitesimals; the bounded source generators include tau,E,K,Q,R_1,r,h,h_1, t,j_R,g, and all displayed source-unit inverses. The testing variables u,omega,v are independently infinitesimal. The SE formulas (A7)–(A9) give
$\boxed{f_A,f_{BB},f_{BC},f_{CC},f_0,
\ell_{NA},\ell_{NB},\ell_{NC},\ell_{MB},\ell_{MC},
H_{NN},H_{NM},H_{MM}\in\varepsilon^2\mathcal B.}\tag{A17}$This does not follow merely from their raw boundedness. In particular (A9) is needed to eliminate 1/D_0^2, and the products nu gamma^2/epsilon^2, nu gamma/epsilon must be simplified using (A7) before a source coefficient is considered bounded.
Divide the entire coefficient polynomial by the common pure-source epsilon^2; put hats on the normalized coefficients. After clearing bounded source-unit and D_u denominators, all normalized coefficients are polynomial in u,omega,v over a bounded source coefficient ring. The four quantities
$ t_B=h_1D_uV_0,\quad r_B=(h_1-1)D_uV_0,\quad
Y_B=-h_1V_0,\quad U_B=h_1u^2V_0$are the only scalar expressions depending on omega outside the explicit packet A=epsilon omega v; and each occurs through
$V_0=1-\frac{\varepsilon^2\tau}{R_1}uv\omega.$Consequently every normalized scalar coefficient satisfies hat f_i-hat f_i|_{omega=j_s}\in(epsilon^2) and, modulo epsilon, is even in both u and v. For example, the exact nonzero reductions include
$\overline{\hat f_A}=-\frac{\tau^2r^2Q^2}{K^2}D_uD_v,
\qquad
\overline{\hat f_0}=-\frac{\tau^2Q^2P_\alpha}{K^3Q_c}D_u.
\tag{A18}$Likewise all normalized first-normal coefficients are omega-independent modulo epsilon. Their parity is not required: t_N=-h_1uv, for example, is odd in the test angles, but its coefficient is still independent of omega modulo epsilon.
Let \hat S=k^2\hat f_A+\hat f_{BB}+\hat f_{BC}+\hat f_{CC}+k^2\hat f_0. By (A16), \hat S_c=0. Its omega-dependence starts at epsilon^2, and \bar{\hat S}=s(u^2,v^2) with s(0,0)=0 in the reduced coefficient ring. Thus the first monic division, in the exact order u=k, then omega=j_s, then v=epsilon j_s^2 E, satisfies
$T_u(\hat S)\equiv u\,p_0(u^2,v^2),\quad
T_\omega(\hat S)\in(\varepsilon^2),\quad
T_v(\hat S)\equiv v\,q_0(v^2)\pmod\varepsilon,
\tag{A19}$where the latter two quotients have no source infinitesimal inverses. Use SE7 with V=0, U=T_omega/epsilon; its U has an additional epsilon factor.
Subtract the exact circuit contact-gradient relation, using the full F_0\in J^2 jet and the nonzero contact Jacobian -E^2 epsilon^3j_s^4, which is canceled as a zero identity, not inverted. The four corrected scalar coefficients J_x,J_y,J_z,J_h have exact zero contact values. The original corrected conductor now gives, from the actual congruences (A19),
$\boxed{(T_\omega J_x,T_\omega J_y,T_\omega J_z,T_\omega J_h)
\equiv(0,0,E v\,q_0(v^2),0)\pmod\varepsilon.}\tag{A20}$Thus each second-stage T_omega J_i\in(\varepsilon,v). The two polynomial quotients V_i=[T_omega J_i-(T_omega J_i)|_{v=0}]/v and U_i=(T_omega J_i)|_{v=0}/epsilon exist in the bounded coefficient ring. This is the proved second-stage premise, not an appeal to generic contact vanishing.
For the two normal rows define
$S_N=\frac{k\hat\ell_{NA}+\hat\ell_{NB}+\hat\ell_{NC}}2,
\qquad
S_M=\frac{\hat\ell_{MB}+\hat\ell_{MC}}2.
\tag{A21}$Each vanishes at contact by (A16), and each is omega-independent modulo epsilon, hence its single ordered quotient satisfies T_omega(S_N),T_omega(S_M)\in(\varepsilon). Apply SE7 once to both complete normal rows. Notice the exact factor 1/2 in (A21): the native (A14) writes N L_N+M_B L_M, while the corrected general conductor writes 2NG_N+2M_BG_M.
Combining the scalar second-stage outputs, both normal rows and the unchanged hard quadratic gives a symmetric 8×8 Gram \hat H over \mathcal B on the fixed root order
$\mathcal R=(N,M_B,x_0,y_0,z_0,h_0,q_1,q_2)^T,
\quad\boxed{\mathscr E/\rho=\mathcal R^T(\varepsilon^2\hat H)\mathcal R.}
\tag{A22}$All coefficient operations are monic polynomial divisions, coefficient additions/multiplications, and inversion of explicit pure source or pure testing units. Neither epsilon, j_s, k, the source gap h, rho, nor any mixed source/test element is inverted. Tests u,omega,v may have any relative infinitesimal valuations independent of source valuations.
7. Formal-base input and status
In CA the strict full formal-C1 residue is encoded by the original CA5–CA8 matrix. Its nontrivial (M_B,x_0) determinant is 256/(E^3 delta_c^2)>0. In SE the corresponding source is the actual original SE4 on the unscaled normals; its nontrivial determinant is 64(E^2+2E ell+4E+4ell)/( (E+ell)^2[1+ell(E+ell)] )>0. The soft contrast matrix and the other hard diagonals are strictly positive in the printed formulas. The derivation that the formal negative-beta source legitimately retains all selected positive packets is assigned to the separate formal-base audit. Do not replace it by a physical C1 theorem, because beta_1=-s_n/2<0.
Conditional conclusion. Let G_0 be the verified bounded formal-C1 full Gram with positive-definite real residue. By the algebraic result above, H\in\operatorname{Sym}_8(\mathcal B) and \mathscr E=\rho\mathcal R^TH\mathcal R; \rho>0 is source infinitesimal. Therefore \overline{G_0+\rho H}=\overline{G_0}\succ0. TF1–TF2 in 03_tensor.tex produce a finite tensor sum of squares, independent of all source/test relative valuations. Restore the exact contact-zero TL4 packet, the centered-A positive excess and the original positive formal-C1 packets. This closes only the native thin-R10 local testing chart once the TL3 and formal-base audits are accepted. The remote/all-source joins and a complete finite SDP lift for Q222 do not follow from this note.
8. Verified discrepancies and audit boundary
Must cancel D_0^{-2} first: (A9); otherwise raw boundedness would not prove bounded outputs.
Must divide by epsilon^2 in SE before reducing: (A17)–(A20). The first scalar T_u\equiv u p_0 only modulo epsilon; no stronger claim is used.
Second stage is not generic contact vanishing: it uses the normalized exact omega dependence through V_0 and yields (A20). In general, reducing only modulo epsilon is insufficient for this allocation.
Hard-normal factor of two: the native linear rows are N L_N+M_B L_M, so the conductor inputs are G_N=L_N/2, G_M=L_M/2.
Separate CA and SE: in CA delta_c is a source unit and CC3 is used; in SE epsilon,j_s are infinitesimal and the two-stage SE7 division is used. j_R is never replaced by j_s.
Coefficient support is restricted: M^2+(D_0^2), not (M,D_0)^2, throughout. No mixed D_0 products were invented.
Non-overclaim: the exact TL3 coefficient subtraction and OP4/OP5 stationary identities and the strict negative-beta formal-base decomposition remain independent source-level proof obligations. This note verifies their interfaces and consequences, and checks the whole algebraic bounded-conductor application, but does not independently rederive those large source formulas.
