5434: $(A_0,B_0,C_0)^2+(D_0^2)$. This follows directly from $q_A=wvD_u$,
5435: $q_B|_{N=M_B=0}\in(v)$, and $Y,W$ in (CA5): the centered scalar
5436: $Y-r uW$ lies in $(v,uw)$; its square is in the same raw monomial
5437: square ideal since $\delta,E$ are units. The sole constant state term
5438: comes from $\alpha$, which has the factor $k^6$, and is a bounded
5439: unit multiple of $D_0^2$. Both full first normals belong to
