      R10 Remote State and Source Join



 


Native C2 R10: complete independent-test join, conditional on one thin contact gate
Scope and verdict. This establishes the all-test, all-real-state tensor-SOS assertion for the original strict C2 R10 source, assuming only the supplied R10 Thin Native Gate Proof for the positive-infinitesimal rho=n/e^2 contact chart. The other two rho branches, every remote measurement chart, the unit-W state chart, literal axes and infinities are proved from explicit full-parent identities below. There is no common real-cap requirement and no source/test rate comparison. The result is a conditional sector theorem, not a finished global Q222 lift.

Theorem: exact R10 join
Let K_c and K_t be independently labelled real closed extensions of R. Work in T=K_c tensor_R K_t. Normalize the positive canonical CHSH coefficient to d=1 in K_c. Assume an actual strict native C2 stationary source with lambda=beta=0, m,C,A,D,alpha>0, n>=0, the full native gaps and positive total-scale intersection of R10. Precisely, in the common-II source coordinates,




t,g,j  positive bounded units;  a positive unbounded;
B,h,e,x=aB positive infinitesimals; r=1-e;
the full native physicality inequality n>=0.

Define s=1/a, w=a e, b=a^2 B, so B=s^2 b, x=s b, e=s w, r=1-s w, and rho=n/e^2 (zero if n=0). Throughout, lambda_circ=(m-a^2q)/(ta^2B) denotes the positive BA angular-circuit mass, not the canonical event coefficient lambda_event=0. The BA formulas abbreviate this mass as lambda. Assume the following sole conditional input:
When 0<rho is source-infinitesimal and a test satisfies u,v,omega=(W+uY)/(1+u^2)>0 all testing-infinitesimal with Z=1, the original quarter-parent has a separated tensor SOS. This is the attached TL4/TL6 complete thin-state gate, including both unrestricted state normals and the positive actual-C2 packets.
Then the whole original parent is a finite sum of squares in T for every independent real projective measurement pair and every real homogeneous state. Complex states follow by real/imaginary splitting. The claim does not order rho against u,v or any testing state ratio.

Therefore the numerator is identically zero at b=0 and its cleared numerator has the polynomial factor b. At s=h=0, keeping bounded w,b, the un-divided numerator reduces to tb/2. Polynomial division gives a bounded quotient with residue (lambda_circ)_0=1/2, even when b has zero residue. 5. The literal n=0 branch is exactly physical C1, not a limiting assertion

6. Exhaustive local-state/sign/endpoint join
For the positive-infinitesimal rho leaf, dividing R10.2 by the positive pure-source w gives
b/w = Q_*/K_* - 2r Delta_* rho w/K_*.
Because rho is infinitesimal and w bounded, this is b/w ~ 1/t. Therefore b is a positive unit or infinitesimal, and the provided CA/SE thin gate has exactly its requisite opening-safe native source premises. This establishes its entry before invoking the assigned hypothesis.
The source split is strictly trichotomic in K_c:
rho positive with positive real residue or unbounded: use §3 on every u,v testing-infinitesimal state.
0<rho source-infinitesimal: use §4 if W is a testing-unit, otherwise the supplied thin-contact gate after the reduction below.
rho=0: use §4 if W is a testing-unit, otherwise §5 (physical C1 positive base).
No comparisons of rho with testing infinitesimals occur. The sign-gauge identity, term by term in the original full parent, is
(u,v,W,X,Y,Z) -> (eps u,eta v,W,eta X,eps Y,eps eta Z) for eps,eta in {+-1}.
At u=0 or v=0, the pure testing signed CHSH core is a sum of squares, in literal projective homogeneous form (SJ9--SJ10), and every retained event packet is nonnegative. Its square roots belong to K_t. Thus literal axes need no contact gate.
For u,v>0 testing-infinitesimal, normalize a nonzero testing state by its coordinate of maximum absolute value; now all state coordinates are bounded. The signed core has residue (X^2+Y^2)/2. If X or Y is a testing-unit, this pure-testing core is strictly positive and supplies an immediate separated square, so assume X,Y are infinitesimal. At least one of W,Z is a testing-unit:
W a unit: normalize W=1 and use §4, with arbitrary bounded signed Z and unrestricted other normals.
W infinitesimal, Z a unit: normalize Z=1; set omega=(W+uY)/(1+u^2) and z_rot=(Z-uX)/(1+u^2). Now omega is testing-infinitesimal and z_rot is a positive testing-unit. The exact SJ5 testing-only Schur identity decomposes the core into positive hard squares plus a scalar proportional to u^2v^2(omega^2+z_rot^2)-2uv H omega z_rot with a positive testing denominator H^2-u^2v^2, H=1+u^2+v^2. If omega z_rot<=0, the whole pure-testing core is nonnegative, so the parent is a sum of pure-testing and event squares. Otherwise omega>0, exactly the positive contact chart of the thin gate/physical n=0 CA--SE base.
The zero state is trivial, and for complex states the real matrix parent satisfies F(psi_re+i psi_im)=F(psi_re)+F(psi_im).
Literal projective infinities were treated by BA11--BA15 without dividing a vanishing homogeneous coordinate. The homogeneous original core at any measurement axis is the explicit sum of two pure-testing squares from SJ10, and all event terms are positive. For tests approaching infinities at an arbitrary independent infinitesimal rate, the BA residues were derived with the reciprocal testing coordinates themselves, so no limiting assumption or mixed denominator is hidden.
