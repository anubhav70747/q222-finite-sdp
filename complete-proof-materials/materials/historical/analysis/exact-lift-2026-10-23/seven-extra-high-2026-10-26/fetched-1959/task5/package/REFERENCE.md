1. Current published baseline (read-only): https://github.com/anubhav70747/q222-finite-sdp/tree/8d23998/complete-proof-materials . The full `K364` matrix and global outer SDP are assumed verified; their duality, symmetry, and density proofs are not repeated.
2. Original native C1 coquotients and complete four-state polynomial: `latex/certificates/c1_short.tex` SD1/SD2 and SG2, with an unchanged script copy `source_refs/c1_native.py`.

4. The preceding oriented 18-feature exact coefficient construction, stored unchanged at `source_refs/PREVIOUS_Z18_SCOPE.md`. It remains only a sufficient subcone.
5. A. P. Calderón, *A note on biquadratic forms*, Linear Algebra Appl. 7 (1973), 175–177, https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(73)90052-9 . Only the theorem that a nonnegative 2-by-n real biquadratic form is SOS of bilinear forms is imported for the one-angle contact anchor.
6. C. Hanselka and R. Sinn, *Positive Semidefinite Univariate Matrix Polynomials*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.08261 . Imported only for the standard real-line univariate matrix SOS factorization underlying the exact half-line Gram implication; a polynomial skew globally PSD still must be separately proved.

All imports are separate from H1. A positive matrix at a single Bob contact is not an SOS for all Bob angles; interval/remote strictness does not constitute global K364 membership.
