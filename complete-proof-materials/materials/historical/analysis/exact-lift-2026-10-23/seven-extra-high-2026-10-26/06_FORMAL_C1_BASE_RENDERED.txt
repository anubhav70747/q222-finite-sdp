Thin R10: formal negative-beta C1 base, exact CA/SE residue audit
Scope and verdict (26 October 2026). Under the original positive-infinitesimal thin-R10 source conditions, the formal OP4 C1 source with $d_1=1,\ \beta_1=-s_n/2<0$ has, on the independent local testing contact chart $Z=1,\ u,v,\mathfrak w>0$ infinitesimal, an exact bounded Gram on eight separated tensor roots, with positive-definite real residue, plus retained nonnegative squares. 1. Exact formal source map, including the small negative beta

The formal C1 coordinates from OP4 are exactly

\tag{1}$Write $K=1+k^2$. Direct substitution into the original C1 formulas gives

5. Precise endpoint of the proof
The strict formal negative-beta C1 local base is established in both exhaustive cases for $e_{\rm scale}^2=tb$. All retained squares have nonnegative coefficients, including the reserved Pi2 packet, and the bounded eight-root matrix has positive-definite real residue. Multiplying by the positive unit $\kappa$, restoring $C_1\mapsto2$, and dividing by four preserves the conclusion. The testing $u,v,\mathfrak w$ remain independent of source infinitesimals.
The argument does not prove a global SOS for the formal C1 source with negative beta, nor any stress bound for $F_2-\kappa F_1$. The TL6 correction and all nonlocal original-C2 tests must be handled separately. This distinction is compulsory because $\beta_1<0$ forbids importing the original physical C1 theorem.
