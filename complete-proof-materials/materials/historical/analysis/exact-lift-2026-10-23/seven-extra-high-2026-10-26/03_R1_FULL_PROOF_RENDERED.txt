A. Fields, strict source and its exact coefficients

No order or inverses are imposed on a mixed tensor. The positive source coefficient is normalized to d=1, with lambda=beta=0. All divisions and radicals below are in one pure factor (or a displayed product of pure factors). Source quantities p,H,j,B,T satisfy




0<p<min(1,j), 0<H<1, j,B,T>0;
B,T,x=jB,y=TB infinitesimal in K_s;
q_z=jT>0 arbitrary (small, unit or unbounded);

This is exactly the R1 domain from t_s,B_s,a_s B_s,t_s B_s infinitesimal via RSR2, with x=a_s B_s, y=t_s B_s. The inverse native map is
$ r=\frac{j-p}{j+px},\quad h=\frac{1-HT}{1+HTx},\quad
