Native C1: a strict all-parity Gram for the entire 105-dimensional hard-state block
26 October 2026. Exact analytic lemma, not a full C1 admission proof.
Sources: SG2 and SG3, and the recovered all-zero basis in Q222_C1_ALL_ZERO_SIXTH_ROOT_REDUCTION_2026-10-26.md. An independent finite exact check, with no SDP or sweeps, is in verify_c1_hard_block_interior.py.
Proposition (hard-state Gram is strictly feasible in all four parities)
Let lambda=n=0, d,m,C,A>0, and D,alpha,beta>=0. No stationary-contact assumption or inequalities beyond these signs are needed. Use independent real positive testing angles u,v, and the complete SG2 parent F=psi^T P(u,v) psi. Let H=P[WXY,WXY], the state principal block, which is unchanged by the source-only shear psi=(xi+xi_c Z,Z). Put r=u^(1/6), s=v^(1/6) and let




W = {r^i s^j xi_W : 0<=i,j<=6, i+j>=6},   |W|=28,
X = {r^i s^j xi_X : 0<=i,j<=6, i<=j},     |X|=28,
Y = {r^i s^j xi_Y : 0<=i,j<=6},           |Y|=49,
U = (W,X,Y),                              |U|=105.

Then there exists a real symmetric matrix G_H(c) >> 0 of order 105 such that, coefficientwise,
xi^T H(u,v) xi = U(r,s,xi)^T G_H(c) U(r,s,xi).
The matrix can be chosen block diagonal by simultaneous parity of the two exponents. The positive-definite parity block orders are 36,24,24,21. All coefficients are source-only, the angular-state functions are fixed, and this identity is valid at every independent test. The statement remains true when one or both of D,beta vanish (even though native extreme-source classification need not admit all such weight patterns).
Proof, step 1: exact sum of squares
Write Du=1+u^2, Dv=1+v^2. Directly setting Z=0 in the complete SG2 parent gives




xi^T H xi = 4m(vW-X+uvY)^2
  + 4C[(uW-Y)^2 + v^2(uW-Y)^2]
  + 4A[Y^2 + u^2Y^2 + v^2Y^2 + u^2v^2Y^2]
  + (4D+2d)(uW+uvX-Y)^2
  + (4alpha+2d)[(vY)^2 + (uvY)^2]
  + (4beta+2d)[X^2+(vX)^2].                      (H1)

All displayed coefficients are positive except that D,alpha,beta may vanish; the coefficient on each of the last three groups remains positive because d>0. Every root of (H1) lies in span U, respecting the two Newton support triangles. Consequently H already has at least one semidefinite Gram in U.
Proof, step 2: a forced-zero lemma for positive pseudo-moments
Let A:Sym(105)->V be the coefficient map of U^T G U. If the affine Gram fiber over H does not contain a positive-definite matrix, strict separation of this affine space from the open positive-definite cone produces a nonzero matrix
M=A^* y >=0, satisfying tr(M G_0)=0 for the Gram G_0 >=0 obtained from (H1). (Equivalently, this is the elementary first facial-reduction certificate.)
For any root p displayed with a strictly positive weight in (H1), we have p^T M p=0, and since M>=0, M p=0. The A packet forces the four feature vectors Y,uY,vY,uvY into ker M. The beta/d packet forces X,vX. The C packet and Y,vY then force uW,uvW. The m packet, together with X,uvY, forces vW. Finally, the D/d packet with uW,Y forces uvX. Thus M kills precisely these 10 named Newton vertices:




W vertices:  (6,0), (0,6), (6,6);
X vertices:  (0,0), (0,6), (6,6);
Y vertices:  (0,0), (6,0), (0,6), (6,6).

Here (i,j) labels r^i s^j in the indicated state coordinate. Positive factors of u and v do not change a kernel condition.
The coefficient-Hankel rule implies a second exact null-propagation rule. If (i,j) is in ker M, (k,l) is any allowed root exponent for the same state component, and p=((i+k)/2,(j+l)/2) is integral and allowed, then
M[p,p] = M[(i,j),(k,l)] = 0.
The first equality is precisely coefficient matching (2p=(i,j)+(k,l)); the second follows because the first vector lies in ker M. Since M>=0, M[p,p]=0 implies p in ker M.
Proof, step 3: two rounds fill both triangles
For W, every point in the triangle i+j>=6 is the midpoint of a known vertex and another allowed point, except (4,4). Indeed use vertex (6,0) when i>=3,j<=3, vertex (0,6) when i<=3,j>=3, and vertex (6,6) when i,j>=3,i+j>=9. The exceptions left by these three cases reduce to (4,4); it is the midpoint of (3,3) and (5,5), already null after the first round.
For X, every point in the triangle i<=j is a midpoint from vertex (0,0) if i,j<=3, vertex (0,6) if i<=3,j>=3,j-i>=3, or vertex (6,6) if i,j>=3, except (2,4). That point is the midpoint of (1,3) and (3,5), both null after round one.
For Y, given any (i,j) in the full square [0,6]^2, choose a corner (a,b) by setting a=0 for i<=3 and a=6 otherwise; similarly b=0 for j<=3 and b=6 otherwise. Then (2i-a,2j-b) lies in the square, so (i,j) is the midpoint of a null vertex and an allowed point. One round suffices.

Corollary (one source-independent rational hard-state reservoir)
Let H_* be the WXY polynomial (H1) for the fixed rational tuple (d,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta)=(1,1,1,1,0,0,0); it need not be a quantum-valid full source. The theorem gives some fixed rational positive-definite Gram G_* for H_* in the original source-independent 105-root basis. Rationality follows because the coefficient equations have rational entries and rational points are dense in their rational affine solution space intersected with the open PD cone.
For any source satisfying the proposition choose the pure-source scalar
rho(c) = (1/d+1/m+1/C+1/A)^(-1)>0.
It is strictly smaller than each of d,m,C,A. The difference H_c-rho H_* is manifestly SOS by (H1), because its four corresponding weights are nonnegative, and the additional D,alpha,beta are nonnegative. Therefore
G_H(c) = rho(c) G_* + G_rem(c) >>0, with G_rem(c)>=0,
is an exact all-parity 105-order Gram and G_H(c) >= rho(c) G_*. The fixed base matrix G_* exists by the preceding proof, but its 105x105 rational entries were not extracted. No explicit global source affine Gram selection is claimed: rho is a legal source-side proof coordinate and the eventual SDP still uses the original affine coefficient equations.
This establishes a uniform analytic strict feasibility mechanism for the hard block. It neither assumes contact scalar positivity nor any correlation source/test relation.
What this changes, and what remains open
At an actual strict native C1 stationary contact, the independently established result gamma = psi_c^T P psi_c = E^T S_gamma E supplies S_gamma >>0 on the full 45-dimensional truncated contact-vanishing scalar space, with parity orders (15,11,11,8). The theorem above now supplies G_H >>0 on the full 105-dimensional hard-state space, not just on its 36 even-even part. Both Gram sectors are strictly feasible separately.
Their joint coefficient identities have the unchanged exact form
F = xi^T H xi + 2 Z zeta^T xi + gamma Z^2, where zeta=H xi_c+P[WXY,Z].
Allow Q_pi = [[A_pi,B_pi],[B_pi^T,S_pi]] >=0 with the full parity orders 51,35,35,29. The coefficient equations require, for every angular monomial and every state-pair coefficient,




sum_pi coeff(U_pi^T A_pi U_pi) = xi^T H xi,
sum_pi coeff(U_pi^T B_pi E_pi) = zeta^T xi,
sum_pi coeff(E_pi^T S_pi E_pi) = gamma.                 (FULL)

Here U_pi consists of all WXY monomials allowed by the two Newton triangles, and E_pi is the explicit contact-ideal basis of the corresponding parity. The coupling equations are solvable (the earlier anchor identities); the separate H and gamma equations admit strict PSD solutions (the current lemma and prior scalar lemma). Neither fact establishes that the joint block matrices are PSD. Even though both diagonal sectors admit strict Grams, the actual source- and test-coupling must permit a contraction in the same Gram choices. The example (xi+(r^6-1)Z)^2 from the independent audit proves why arbitrary diagonal strict choices need not extend.
The first unresolved implication is therefore the genuinely coupled one:
For every strict native C1 source, can the three affine coefficient systems (FULL) be satisfied simultaneously with all four matrices Q_pi>=0, freely varying A_pi, B_pi, S_pi? Equivalently, is the full C1 sixth-root maximal Gram margin nonnegative?
No positivity conclusion about this last implication, no C1 admission, and no complete Q222 SDP lift follows from the present lemma. The proof does not reinstate the failed three-channel remainder or the over-restricted C1-36 condition.
Sharpened exact frontier: robust free-coupling contraction (equivalent by closure)
There is a useful stronger formulation of the remaining step which does not freeze either diagonal Gram and does not require proving strict positivity at the limiting unperturbed source.
For a fixed strict native C1 source and its actual contact, work in the 150-dimensional all-zero basis (four parity blocks). Let
R(c) = xi^T H_c xi + gamma_c Z^2.
By the new proposition and the previously established scalar lemma, R has a block-diagonal Gram G_R(c) >> 0 on the entire 150-dimensional basis (WXY 105 plus scalar 45). For epsilon>0 form
F_epsilon = F_c + epsilon R = (1+epsilon)xi^T H xi + 2 Z zeta^T xi + (1+epsilon)gamma Z^2.
Lemma (exact equivalence). The unperturbed source F_c admits a sixth-root Gram in the all-zero basis if and only if for every positive real epsilon, F_epsilon admits a positive-definite Gram in that basis.
Proof: If G >=0 represents F_c, then G+epsilon G_R >>0 represents F_epsilon. Conversely, if G_epsilon >>0 represents F_epsilon for all epsilon>0, take epsilon -> 0. The image of the PSD cone under the fixed coefficient map is closed: integration of the squared linearly independent basis over a fixed positive testing rectangle and the state sphere produces a positive-definite moment matrix; its pairing with G_epsilon bounds tr G_epsilon when F_epsilon has bounded coefficients. A convergent subsequence gives a PSD Gram for F_c. QED.
For each positive epsilon, all diagonal and cross blocks are free. In the four parities write G_pi=[[A_pi,B_pi],[B_pi^T,S_pi]]. The positive-definite version is equivalent to the exact affine coefficient conditions




sum_pi coeff(U_pi^T A_pi U_pi) = (1+epsilon) coeff(xi^T H xi),
sum_pi coeff(U_pi^T B_pi E_pi) = coeff(zeta^T xi),
sum_pi coeff(E_pi^T S_pi E_pi) = (1+epsilon) coeff(gamma),

and the strict simultaneous contractions
A_pi >>0,  S_pi >>0,   ||A_pi^(-1/2) B_pi S_pi^(-1/2)||_op <1 for each parity.
Every inverse or square root here is performed on a source-only finite matrix, not a mixed source/test polynomial. This criterion retains the entire 150-dimensional freedom and is genuinely equivalent (by the lemma), unlike fixing S_0, B_*, or all non-even parity couplings. The unproved statement has been sharpened to:
For every finite strict native C1 contact, and every epsilon>0, there is a free full-parity coefficient-compatible strict contraction of the state and scalar Gram reservoirs for F_epsilon.
The separate interior theorems give strict diagonal Gram fibers, and the earlier anchor formula gives nonempty coupling affine fibers. Their simultaneous strict contraction is not implied by these facts or by pointwise positivity of F_epsilon. Proving it, or finding an exact full-parity dual separator, remains the decisive C1 task.
