Q222 V06: PRIMARY REAL-ALGEBRAIC CRITERION AND BOTH INCLUSIONS
26 October 2026

Primary: Claus Scheiderer, Second-order cone representation for convex sets
in the plane, SIAM J. Appl. Algebra Geom. 5 (2021) 114-139,
arXiv:2004.04196v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.04196

Corollary 3.19 (PDF page index 14 / printed p. 15) is NOT restricted to
plane sets: if K subset R^n is closed, convex and semialgebraic, it is a
spectrahedral shadow iff, for every real closed extension R' of R, every
a in K_{R'}, and every f in (P_K)_{R'}, the point-first tensor evaluation
f^tensor(a) belongs to Sigma(R' tensor_R R')^2.

Theorem 3.10 (PDF page index 11 / printed p. 12) is different: to establish
a specified extension-degree bound d, it requires the sum of squares to
have each square of tensor rank at most d. Corollary 3.19 does not ask for
one d, one number of squares, one polynomial degree, one NPA level, or one
source-independent finite testing library across all field/test/source
instances.

In the paper's Definition 3.7, f^tensor(a) is point first; the Q222
proof writes coefficients first. Flip the two factors in R' tensor_R R'.
The flip is an algebra automorphism preserving sums of squares.

The proof of Corollary 3.19 explicitly yields a real affine variety X,
a map phi:X -> A^n, and a finite-dimensional real linear space
U subset R[X] with K subset phi(X(R)) and phi*(P_K) subset Sigma U^2.
It does not assert an evaluated X or U. Theorem 2.10 connects the
finite SOS space to finite semidefinite extension degree.

Set u_1,...,u_r a basis of U and take the finite real vector space
V=span(1,phi_1,...,phi_n,u_i u_j). A feasible point above x in R^n
is a linear functional L:V->R with L(1)=1, L(phi_i)=x_i, and
[L(u_i u_j)] PSD. Linear dependencies among the displayed generators
are finitely many affine equalities; represent with scalar PSD +/-
constraints or eliminate them. Hence there is one finite real affine
pencil.

(+) For x in K, choose z in X(R) with phi(z)=x and L=ev_z|V.
The moment matrix is the PSD rank-one matrix u(z)u(z)^T.
(-) For any feasible (x,L), and any f in P_K, phi*(f)=sum_j g_j^2,
g_j in U. Thus f(x)=L(phi*f)=sum_j L(g_j^2)>=0.
Closed convex separation implies x in K. This proof does not require
L to be evaluation, a representing measure, or a closed projected PSD
cone. It uses both inclusions, not just a dual-cone description.

Applying this to Q222 requires: Q222 closed convex semialgebraic;
its fixed nine-atom first-order extension Q_K; transferred polar P_Q,K;
and a tensor SOS for every real closed K, every transferred f,q.
These are supplied by the V06 chain under the labelled native atom
hypothesis. Thus a finite real pencil EXISTS, but its entries and size
are not extracted from the source-specific certificates.

Other caution: a polynomially defined Q over rational coefficients
allows an existential algebraic-real pencil after fixing some finite
sizes by first-order transfer. That does not evaluate those sizes.
