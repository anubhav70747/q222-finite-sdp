Exact strict native C2 exclusion from the 36-feature half-power cone
Target: native C2 with lambda=beta=0, the complete real state vector (W,X,Y,Z), arbitrary independent positive testing half-angles u,v, and fixed features$Z_{1/2}=(u^{i/2}v^{j/2}\psi_\ell)_{0\le i,j\le2,\ \ell=0,\ldots,3}$.
Result. An exact strict rational native contact has no PSD Gram in this 36-feature basis. The certificate is a 90-integer dual vector. Its four compressed adjoints have strictly positive rational LDL pivots, whereas its pairing with the entire physical parent is negative. The proof is finite, exact and source/test separated. Execute the companion standard-library Python checker for a complete independent replay.

2. Equivalent compressed Gram problem
Write $u=x^2,v=y^2$. The parent is even in each variable and quadratic in its four state coordinates. An SOS can be sign-averaged into four parity blocks with raw orders $(16,8,8,4)$.
The $WW$ diagonal coefficient is $4mv^2+(4C+4D+2d)u^2+4Cu^2v^2$, whose half Newton polygon requires $i+j\ge2$ for a feature $x^iy^jW$. The $ZZ$ diagonal is $4\alpha+(4\alpha+2d+4m)u^2+4Dv^2$, requiring $i+j\le2$ for $x^iy^jZ$. This is necessary for each SOS root, since a sum of real leading squares cannot cancel. The six forbidden features are $W$ at $(i,j)=(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)$ and $Z$ at $(1,2),(2,1),(2,2)$. The retained parity block orders are $(14,6,6,4)$.
Set $u=u_cU,v=v_cV,\psi_\ell=(\psi_c)_\ell\phi_\ell$, an invertible diagonal source-dependent change used only to construct the separator, not an SDP product auxiliary. Any half-power Gram remains a half-power Gram under this rescaling. The contact is now $U=V=1,\phi=(1,1,1,1)$. Every individual parity block vanishes at this point, so selecting the first lexicographic basis feature $z_p$ as pivot, the kernel-complement roots are $q_e=z_e-z_p$. This gives four orders $(13,5,5,3)$ and loses no feasible positive Gram.

3. Negative pairing with the entire native parent
The original source-parent polynomial is exactly
$\begin{aligned}
F_c={}&4nD_uD_vX^2+4m(vW-X+uvY-uZ)^2+4CD_v(uW-Y)^2+4AD_uD_vY^2\\
&+(4D+2d)(uW+uvX-Y-vZ)^2+(4\alpha+2d)D_u(vY-Z)^2\\
&+2dD_v(X+uZ)^2-2dD_uD_vZ^2,
\end{aligned}$with $D_u=1+u^2,D_v=1+v^2$. Let $b_{AB,\ell k}$ be the coefficients of $\widetilde F(U,V,\phi)=F_c(u_cU,v_cV,\operatorname{diag}(\psi_c)\phi)$. The exact rational contraction is
$\boxed{\langle Y,b\rangle=-\frac{31917121340091771507320448459210586965990611717744093996544823253}{1129383645613305483559298619423160947865086012060000000000}<0.}$The right side comes from all state cross terms as well as all testing monomials, without specializing $U,V$.
If a half-power PSD Gram existed, Newton pruning, sign averaging and contact annihilation would give compressed PSD matrices $G^{(p)}$ matching every coefficient, and hence
$\langle Y,b\rangle=\sum_p\operatorname{tr}(Q^{(p)}G^{(p)})\ge0,$a contradiction. Thus this strict native C2 source is excluded from the full 36-feature cone. The dual can equivalently be pulled back to original coefficients using$Y^{\rm original}_{AB,\ell k}=u_c^Av_c^B(\psi_c)_\ell(\psi_c)_kY_{AB,\ell k}$, giving a rational separator in original fixed-source coordinates.
4. Consequence and boundary
The 36-feature half-power cone is sound, closed and affine in the original source coordinates, but it does not admit the complete strict native C2 family. No admission of its $n=0$ limits follows from this failed cone; other already constructed globally sound cones may cover some native contacts. The next justified fixed basis is the existing sixth-root grid, which retains half-power roots and accommodates cubic Hurwitz circuits; it is a candidate, not a proven minimum-size or uniformly admitting construction.
The first unproved positive implication is still the universal compressed-sixth-root admission $\mu_{C2}(c)\ge0$ for every strict native C2 source, or an alternative fixed finite globally sound affine cone covering all strict native C2 sources missed by previously retained cones. The present exact separator settles the smaller half-power question in the negative, not global representability.
