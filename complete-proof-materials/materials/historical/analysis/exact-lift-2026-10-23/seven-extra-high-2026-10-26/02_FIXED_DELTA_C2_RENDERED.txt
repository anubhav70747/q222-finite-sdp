The fixed-Delta native-C2 certificate: exact results and the unclosed PSD implication
Scope and verdict (26 October 2026). Work with the complete four-state SG2 quarter-parent, not a selected state or angular region. This note proves (i) global soundness and source-cone closedness of the proposed 196-function library, (ii) an exact 134-feature forced Newton support and four contact kernels, and (iii) a sharp anisotropic Motzkin-multiplier obstruction. It does not prove that every strict native C2 source is admitted, nor produce a native source with a universal SOS separator. No full Q222 lift is claimed.
The native source and SG2 formula are reproduced below so that all finite checks can be performed without another proof archive. The separate C1 and TL3 arguments are not used.
1. Literal certificate and both directions of the fixed SOS equivalence

$Z_{ij\ell}=r^is^j\psi_\ell,\quad

The SG2 sign congruences extend this to negative finite angles; polynomial/projective continuity includes compatible axes and all projective endpoints, including the four zeros of Delta. This equivalence concerns membership in this library, not equivalence between matrix positivity and SOS.

3. Exact closedness theorem (not conditional on source positivity)
Let K_Delta be the image of the fixed PSD coefficient system (4) in the finite-dimensional vector space of real source coefficients. The cone K_Delta is closed.
Proof. Choose a strictly positive smooth finite measure mu on [1,2]^2 in the independent variables (r,s) and a rotationally invariant probability measure on the state unit sphere. For every parity block form the fixed Gram moment matrix
$\mathcal M_\sigma=\int Z_\sigma Z_\sigma^T\,d\mu.$It is strictly positive definite: if a linear combination of the monomial-state features vanishes on a positive-measure open product, all of its polynomial coefficients vanish, so the combination is identically zero. Suppose source coefficient vectors c_j converge, and choose PSD preimages Q_\sigma^{(j)} in (4). The integrals \int \Delta P_{c_j}\,d\mu remain bounded because the original polynomial coefficient map is linear and finite dimensional. But
$0\le\sum_\sigma\operatorname{tr}(Q_\sigma^{(j)}\mathcal M_\sigma)
 \le C.$Since each fixed M_sigma is PD, the traces of all positive Q_sigma^(j) are bounded. A subsequence of the tuple of Gram matrices therefore converges to a tuple of PSD matrices. Passing to the limit in the finite affine coefficient equations (5) gives a PSD preimage for the limiting source. This proves closedness even at singular Grams. The argument also works for the full 196 basis, avoiding parameter-dependent compression. QED.
Finite omitted-event consequences. If a finite source is a coefficientwise limit of strict native C2 sources already proved admitted by (4), it too is admitted, including the n=0 opening limits. The approximation of an omitted-event contact by strict ones is a separate geometric input; closedness does not supply the missing admission of strict contacts. The known native opening theorem supplies this approximation on its stated domain.
4. A sharp scalar obstruction to anisotropic Motzkin cancellation
Define the homogeneous Motzkin form
$M(x,y,z)=x^4y^2+x^2y^4+z^6-3x^2y^2z^2.$Lemma (sharp). For A,B>0, (A x^2+B y^2)M is a polynomial sum of real squares if and only if A=B.
Proof. For A=B, scale this exact identity:
$\boxed{(x^2+y^2)M=
 [xy(2z^2-x^2-y^2)]^2
 +[xz(z^2-y^2)]^2
 +[yz(z^2-x^2)]^2.}\tag{11}$For necessity, assume an SOS. Since the left is homogeneous of degree eight, all roots can be taken homogeneous of degree four. Its Newton polygon permits precisely these monomials, grouped by sign parity in (x,y):
$\begin{array}{c|l}
\text{even/even}&x^2y^2\\
\text{odd/odd}&xyz^2,\ x^3y,\ xy^3\\
\text{odd/even}&xz^3,\ xy^2z\\
\text{even/odd}&yz^3,\ x^2yz.
\end{array}\tag{12}$Average the Gram under both sign changes. The polynomial vanishes at (1,1,1), so every averaged parity root vanishes there. The even/even sector has only x^2y^2 and therefore vanishes identically. Write the odd/odd roots asxy(a_i z^2+b_i x^2+c_i y^2).Comparing the coefficients of x^6y^2, x^2y^6 and x^4y^4 gives, respectively,
$ \sum_i b_i^2=A,\qquad \sum_i c_i^2=B,
 \qquad 2\sum_i b_ic_i=A+B.\tag{13}$There is no contribution to these three z-free monomials from any other parity block (the only even/even monomial was eliminated). By Cauchy--Schwarz and AM--GM,
$ A+B=2\sum_i b_ic_i\le2\sqrt{AB}\le A+B.$Equality implies A=B. QED.
The same coefficient comparison can be read as a forced Gram principal minor
$\det\begin{pmatrix}A&(A+B)/2\\(A+B)/2&B\end{pmatrix}
=-\tfrac14(A-B)^2.\tag{14}$Relation to the proposed C2 library. Identity (11) shows why a first factor of the form x^2+y^2 can repair the standard homogeneous Motzkin limit; it is not an obstruction by itself. Equation (14) proves that the repair fails for an arbitrary independently weighted first factor. Because native contact normalization changes Delta as in (10), any proof that replaces (10) by an isotropic factor is incorrect.
This scalar lemma is not a native C2 separator. To turn it into one, it would be necessary to construct a specific family of actual native C2 coefficient tuples and state-linear test substitutions whose exact leading polynomial is the anisotropically weighted form in the lemma, while preserving SOS degree bounds and limits. No such native full-state degeneration is proved here. The result cannot be used to exclude the 196-feature library without that step.
