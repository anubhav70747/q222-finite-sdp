Unbounded adjacent-event lemma. For C,A unbounded, retain real caps C=A=mu and their positive excess squares. The exact whole signed core in the raw state frame B=X+uZ, V=vZ is

This provides independent-test remote coverage whenever Alice has noninfinitesimal half angle, without a lower bound on D or beta.

2. Exact defect and repair in the matched-Bob FM leaf
Source domain S,kappa positive infinitesimal, nu,ell positive bounded units, nu+ell=1, with the native physical conditions, including beta=0; in this domain D/S~nu^2/2 so D=0 has empty intersection. Put x=kappa S, theta=1-h, b1=beta theta, j=sqrt(nu ell).
Use N_x=X+h uZ, N_y=(Y-r uW)/sqrt(Sr(1-r)), T=kappa W, U=uZ, P=uW/sqrt S, Q=sqrt S Z, N=T-jU, G=P-jQ.
The original FM17 prints -(b1+theta/2)(1+v^2) in the coefficient L_U. The correct sign is plus. The exact complete-parent differentiation gives

           + u Z [m theta -(D+1/2)v^2(1+h u^2)

The printed FM18, when based on printed FM17, differs by

This is a nonzero universal four-state polynomial even at strict native sources. At the actual contact, where the physical normal derivative vanishes and N=G=0, the printed L_U(x,b)=-2(beta+1/2)theta(1+b^2)<0. Therefore its claimed monic divisibility in FM19 fails as printed.

4. What remains unresolved in this independent audit
The full C1 infinitesimal-total-scale sector is not yet independently proved by this note. Its trichotomy is a finite ordered-source partition, but this alone does not establish the tensor SOS of every local leaf. In particular, the full two-normal divided-coefficient matrices at the ell-infinitesimal source endpoint (EL5–EL21), the nu-infinitesimal endpoint (NU14–NU21), and the double-infinitesimal kappa,nu source under both bounded and unbounded tau (BN13–BN17 / UN14–UN21) require actual independent coefficient replay if they are to be counted as verified, rather than invoked by section title. Likewise several k-unit and eta-unit source leaves have not been rederived here.
The first exact incorrect implication found is FM17 as printed => FM18 universal parent identity => FM19 monic contact divisor. The sign correction closes this defect in the FM leaf and must propagate to UKR10. No counterexample to tensor-SOS or to global qualitative existence follows from this printing error; the corrected residue is strict.
