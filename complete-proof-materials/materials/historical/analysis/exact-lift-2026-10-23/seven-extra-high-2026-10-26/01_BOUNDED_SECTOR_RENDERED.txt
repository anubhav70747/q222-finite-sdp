Bounded canonical sources: one complete separated tensor-SOS sector
Date: 26 October 2026. Scope: The bounded positive-core canonical sector, conditional only on native contact entry: the source is physical and has an actual finite noncommuting contact in C1 or C2 with the native signs/gaps. This is not the full global existence proof and does not assert anything about unbounded normalized event coefficients, R1/R10, or explicit fixed finite Gram dictionaries.

Theorem
Let K_c,K_t be independently labelled real-closed extensions of R, and T=K_c⊗_R K_t. Let
c=(d,lambda,n,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta)
be a canonical physical source over K_c with d>0, delta_tot = d/(d+lambda+n+m+C+A+D+alpha+beta) a positive bounded unit, and an actual finite noncommuting stationary contact. Assume either:
C1: lambda=n=0, m,C,A,alpha>0, D,beta>=0, D+beta>0;
C2: lambda=beta=0, m,C,A,D,alpha>0, n>=0.
Then, for every independent projective pair of testing measurements and every testing real or complex state, the homogeneous source/test slack is a finite SOS in T. This includes all native permitted literal zero weights and every relative valuation of source and testing openings. No mixed tensor element is inverted or square-rooted.
Proof follows. Divide by the pure-source positive d and write d=1; all eight other weights are bounded. Use the quarter-parent F/4 when referencing WA, WN, PP, RC and CR, and the full parent F in FE/FR/S/CU. The factor four is a pure positive square.
Three mechanisms used in every leaf
Strict bounded Gram. For a bounded matrix Q over B_c⊗B_t, if res(Q)≻0, then z^TQz is tensor-SOS for arbitrary rows z∈T^r (TF1–TF2). A retained positive event packet is itself a separated square. Exact finite monic division by u-u_c, v-v_c, etc., before tensor evaluation produces bounded coefficients and never uses a mixed inverse. Pure-source denominators require a proved nonzero real residue, as verified below in the leaves.
Noncommuting residues. The complete NC1 parent admits the hard/soft polynomial form NC7. A finite positive hard block, strict constrained angular Hessian and exact source-field stationary center give NC9, with a positive-residue matrix on all soft and hard deviations. The relevant source-free proof is NC7–NC26: hard event normals form a matching in the eight-cycle, at most three such normals are state-independent, and the four-event case has an exact radial completion NC17–NC26. Its recursion terminates after at most three additional event activations. This establishes every test with both local measurement pairs noncommuting in residue, without a condition linking coefficient scales to testing scales.
Quartic degeneracies. For a finite commuting-edge contact, substitute X=u²x,Y=ua,Z=ub after normalizing the nonzero event amplitude. The complete parent is exactly 4m+u²K; the three-state Hessian is strict, the first missing angular derivative has strictly positive pivot gamma in FE3, and the higher hard row has positive coefficient FE2. For a finite-ratio zero-corner contact with weak alpha, normalize vY-Z=-1, set (X,Y,u,W,V)=(vx,vy,vt,w,v²), and use the exact identity F=4alpha+v²K. The positive state complement, double squared-angle zero and the next determinant coefficient give the strict four-variable Hessian and gamma>0 in FR2. In both cases a source-field semialgebraic implicit center yields FC1–FC3. The complete first-state-normal rows are obtained by monic differences; at the ratio corner the actual undivided rows are (X-vx_c,Y-vy_c,u-vt_c,v(W-w_c),v²-V_c) (no division by an independently infinitesimal test v). If the source scalar kappa is nonnegative, retain kappa u² or kappa v²; if negative, a source-field stationary center with nonnegative physical value absorbs it. The resulting full Gram has a strictly positive real residue.
Exhaustive source routing and forbidden faces
Every source coefficient is nonnegative bounded, so is either a unit or has zero residue. In the residue Laurent polynomial SG5–SG6, the two forbidden faces are exactly
h|_{lambda=beta=A=0}=-(1+2n)(2D+2C+1)(2alpha+2m+1), and
h|_{lambda=n=m=C=0}=-(2D+1)(2beta+1)(2A+2alpha+1).
The first face’s remaining positive Laurent terms, at U=t²,V=t^{-1}, tend to zero; the other face has just a positive q10/V and one sends V→∞. These are real residue contradictions to source physicality, so C2 A is a unit, and C1 at least one of m,C is a unit. The analogous WA1/WA2 residue tests (including the exact state witness F=16epsilon((1+n+m+A+D)epsilon-1) at u=v=1 when C=alpha=0) force C,A,D to be units whenever alpha is weak, in either family. Angular stationarity WA4 then gives U,V infinitesimal and the bounded source shape equations WA7–WA10. These are source-field facts and do not constrain independent tests.

The regular strong leaf S and weak-alpha CU
S assumes d,C,A,D,alpha units. At finite u=0,v_0!=0, after removing the whole weak m packet, the determinant is U E(V)+U² B(V); its q10=4alpha A t7t3 and q12=4CD t5t2 are units, and B(v_0²)>0 because q21>=4CA t1t4 is a unit. Physicality implies E(V)>=0, hence a zero at v_0² is double. The explicit strictly positive three-state block M0(v0) and FE1–FE3 above finish the complete finite-edge form. Every other finite one-commuting test has positive-definite state residue except the zero ray at a double corner. The complete corner residue Grams from S are, respectively:
(0,0): 2(1+2n+2beta) X² + (4C+4D+2)(u-Y)²+4A Y²+4alpha Z²;
(∞,0): 4C W²+4A Y²+(4D+2)(W+v)²+4alpha Z²+[4(m+beta)+2](p+Z)²;
(0,∞): (4n+4beta+2)X²+4C(u-Y)²+[4(A+alpha)+2]Y²+4D Z²;
(∞,∞): 4C W²+4A Y²+4D(X-p)²+(2+4n)X²+[2+4(m+alpha)](Y-q)².
Each represents the full remainder after retaining the displayed original positive whole squares, and has a trivial square kernel. The row substitutions involve only positive-unit angles or reciprocal homogeneous coordinates. Literal axes are polynomial values.
CU has n,m,C,A,D units and weak alpha. The same complementary faces remain strict except the joint zero-angle corner. In the W=1 chart its full quadratic residue in (X,Y,u,v) is (4n+2)X²+4m(v-X)²+[4(C+D)+2](u-Y)²+4A Y², strictly positive. In the Z=1 chart, its complete quadratic jet Q_w is (source, Eq. CU (2)):
4n X²+4m(vw-X-u)²+4C(uw-Y)²+4A Y²+4D(uw-Y-v)²+2[(uw-Y)²+(X+u)²-2uvw].
If strict, apply the bounded Gram lemma. If singular, its state block is positive and its angular kernel has a finite nonzero ratio; q20=4nAt3t4, q02=4mDt5t6 and q21,q12 are strict, supplying precisely the FR1–FR2 hypotheses. This handles all independent tested u/v rates and includes alpha positive infinitesimal, and the literal measurement axes.
The weak-alpha leaves WA and WN

The two reciprocal leaves PP and RC

The C1 reciprocal leaf CR
Here C,A,alpha are units, m,D weak, and beta is any bounded nonnegative element. Source balances give U=u_c² and P=v_c^{-2} infinitesimal, and D/P², beta U/P² bounded (CR1). The original X,Y source equations, after taking the positive two-by-two source-field Schur complement, establish bounded r,x,y and the critical unit shape equations CR6–CR7. No mixed Schur inverse occurs. Put Q=Z_c², L=PQ, and choose the reciprocal quarter-parent CR9.
At W=1 with u=rbz, X=xbz²,Y=ybz the entire scalar is CR11 (verified directly), and the three contact balances CR13 give the balanced identity CR15:

The exact cubic conductors CR22 control these with infinitesimal Gram coefficients whenever theta is a unit; when delta is a unit, R is kept directly. For unbounded source ratio Q, CR26–CR27 reduce the entire scalar to a strictly positive real-residue two-row Gram on (R,K) plus retained positive squares, and CR19 controls the normals.

C1 literal D=0 or beta=0 is allowed wherever the actual contact exists; they cannot both be zero. This closes CR39 and its all-tests restoration.
Endpoints, independence and scope
The listed frames use only (i) positive pure-source normalization by d, bounded source coquotients whose denominators have positive unit residue, and positive pure-testing coordinates such as 1+u², 1+v²; (ii) polynomial/monic changes of testing roots; and (iii) source-only centers when a quartic point demands one. All listed positive-residue matrices act on the complete scalar, normal and quadratic state expansion. Any zero row at a literal endpoint is harmless: a positive-definite coefficient matrix remains SOS on an arbitrary, possibly dependent, row vector. The two testing half-angle sign gauges are exact congruences; real and imaginary parts of the state add as two nonnegative contributions. Source and testing infinitesimals were never identified.
Thus the bounded canonical-source theorem is proved at the native contact-entry interface. What it does not prove is the exhaustive global SG/EN source decomposition, any unbounded normalised source (R1/R10 included), the separated SOS for all other leaves, Scheiderer's full global hypothesis, or a printed exact full-Q222 affine pencil.
