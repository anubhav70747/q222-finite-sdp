# Native C2 finite-c admission near both open c-ends: a fixed 16-root analytic Gram

**Theorem (fixed sixth-root subgrid, 26 October 2026).** Set
\(\varepsilon=c^{-2}\), \(b=a^2t+\eta\varepsilon\), \(r=\rho\varepsilon\), with **fixed strictly positive real** face parameters
\[
 a,t,\eta,\rho>0,\qquad 0<\rho<t,\quad 0<t-\rho<1,
 \quad 0<\eta<1,\quad a^2t+\eta>1.\tag{1}
\]
For every point of (1) there is \(\varepsilon_0(a,t,\eta,\rho)>0\) such that, for all \(0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0\), the **exact native C2 full four-state parent** admits a positive semidefinite Gram in the following **fixed 16 rows**, a subgrid of \(Z_6\):
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal Z_{\rm even}&=(UW,UVX,VZ,VW,UZ,UVY,UVW,Z,UX,VX,UY,VY,X,Y),\\
 \mathcal Z_{\rm odd}&=(\sqrt{UV}\,W,\sqrt{UV}\,Z).
\end{split}\tag{2}
\]
The source normalization of \(U,V,W,X,Y,Z\) is given in Step 1. No new testing power, testing denominator, or state normalization is used. The construction is **rational in the source and \(\varepsilon\)** once one positive source-only Gram gauge is chosen. Its two finite kernel constraints are exact, not limits.

The assertion is **uniform on every compact** \(K\) contained in (1): one positive Gram gauge and one \(\varepsilon_K>0\) work on all of \(K\). The exact native C2 source inversion in Step 8 transfers the theorem to the corresponding whole open \(c\to0\) end. This is a local **explicit-lift admission** result, not an assertion that every native C2 source or Q222 lies in the sixth-root lift.

## 1. Exact full parent and native normalization

Write \(\mathcal P=F/4\). Define the *rescaled parent*, with \(\widetilde F=4\widetilde{\mathcal P}\), by the **identity**
\[
\begin{split}
\widetilde F={}&4n(U^2+\varepsilon)(V^2+\varepsilon)X^2
 +4A(U^2+\varepsilon)(V^2+\varepsilon)Y^2\\
&+4m(VW+UVY-UZ-\varepsilon X)^2
 +(4D+2)(UW+UVX-VZ-\varepsilon Y)^2\\
&+(4\alpha+2)(U^2+\varepsilon)(VY-Z)^2
 +\frac{4C}{\varepsilon}(V^2+\varepsilon)(UW-\varepsilon Y)^2\\
&+2(V^2+\varepsilon)(2UXZ+\varepsilon X^2-Z^2).
\end{split}\tag{3}
\]
This equals \(\varepsilon F(cU,cV;W,X/c,Y/c,Z)\). All weights \(n,m,C,A,D,\alpha\) are the **actual native rational weights**, not independent free coefficients. Their exact six rational functions are printed and constructed in `map_core.py` under `native_weights`. They have analytic finite limits \(n_0,m_0,0,A_0,D_0,\alpha_0\), with \(C=C_2\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^3)\), all strictly positive except the displayed zero. Their denominators stay nonzero for (1).

The actual source contact of (3) is
\((U_c,V_c;W_c,X_c,Y_c,Z_c)=(1,a;b/a,-\rho,\eta/a,1)\).
Make the invertible **source-only** substitutions
\[
 V_{\rm old}=aV,\quad W_{\rm old}=\frac ba W,
 \quad X_{\rm old}=-\rho X,\quad Y_{\rm old}=\frac\eta aY,\quad Z_{\rm old}=Z.\tag{4}
\]
The contact then has \(U=V=W=X=Y=Z=1\) for **every** \(\varepsilon\). The coefficient vector \(f(\varepsilon)\in\mathbb R^{78}\) is defined by expanding all ten symmetric state coefficients of the normalized \(\widetilde{\mathcal P}\) in the 78 monomials generated by (2). In particular this retains both entire state-normal rows and every state cross term.

## 2. The two *exact* contact kernels and fixed coefficient map

In (2), split the fourteen even rows into \(\mathcal A=\{0,1,2,3,4,5,12,13\}\) and \(\mathcal B=\{6,7,8,9,10,11\}\). Set
\[
 K_A=\mathbf1_{\mathcal A},\qquad K_B=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B};\tag{5}
\]
these vectors have zeros outside their named groups. The odd contact kernel is \((1,1)\). Choose the twelve **fixed** even difference rows
\[
 d_i=z_i-z_5\ (i=0,1,2,3,4,12,13),\qquad
 d_j=z_j-z_{11}\ (j=6,7,8,9,10),\tag{6}
\]
and the odd row \(h=\sqrt{UV}(W-Z)\). Let \(D=(d_1,\ldots,d_{12})^T\). The Gram target is \(D^TH(\varepsilon)D+g(\varepsilon)h^2\), with \(H\in\mathrm{Sym}_{12}\), \(g\in\mathbb R\). It automatically annihilates (5) and the odd contact kernel **for every \(\varepsilon\)**.

Form the completely explicit coefficient matrix
\[
 \mathfrak M:\ \mathrm{Sym}_{12}\oplus\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R^{78},\qquad
 (H,g)\mapsto\operatorname{coeff}(D^THD+gh^2).
\tag{7}
\]
Rows and columns are generated combinatorially from (2) and (6); its size is \(78\times79\) and its **exact rational rank is 66**. A fixed rational right inverse \(\mathcal R\) on its 66-dimensional image is obtained by choosing the lexicographically first 66 independent columns, then 66 independent rows, and inverting that **constant rational** minor. Every entry of this map and its pivots is regenerated from integer monomial exponents; no stored matrix or binary intermediate is needed.

There are 12 rational left annihilators of (7). Six vanish on the symbolic whole parent (3) **before** substituting any source weight. For the other six, substitute the exact native rational weights, multiply by the common explicit source denominator
\(2\varepsilon a^2H_*\rho g_*(a^2t)\eta[a^2(1+\rho-t)](1-\eta)\),
where \(H_*\) and \(g_*=a^2t+\eta-1+(\eta+\rho)\varepsilon\) are computed from the native denominators. The resulting six multivariate numerators are **identically zero polynomials**. `verify_finite_c.py` constructs these polynomials coefficient by coefficient and checks literal zero, rather than checking source samples. Therefore
\[
 \boxed{f(\varepsilon)\in\operatorname{im}\mathfrak M\quad
 \text{for all admissible sufficiently small }\varepsilon.}\tag{8}
\]
In particular \(f_0=f(0)\), \(f_1=f'(0)\) also lie in the image.

## 3. Endpoint Gram with a *free small* source-only gauge

The exact endpoint identities give the four squared forms and the sixth-root-compatible 8-root Gram. Put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 p_2=\frac{2(1+\rho)(a^2t+\eta)}{a^2t(t+1)(a^2t+2\eta-1)},\\
 q_2=\frac{2(1+\rho)(t-\rho)}{t(t+1)(2\rho+1-t)},\qquad
 \gamma_{\max}=\frac{4ap_2q_2}{q_2+a^2p_2}>0.
\end{gathered}\tag{9}
\]
For **any** \(0<\gamma<\gamma_{\max}\), the normalized six-row endpoint Gram \(Q_\gamma\), explicitly constructed by `verify_finite_c.py` as
\[
 Q_\gamma=\frac14\Big[c_Xe_Xe_X^T+c_Ye_Ye_Y^T
 +p_2u_2u_2^T+q_2u_1u_1^T
 -\frac\gamma2(s_1s_2^T+s_2s_1^T)\Big],\tag{10}
\]
has kernel \(\mathbb R\mathbf1_6\) and is positive definite on its five-dimensional contact complement. All six explicitly normalized vectors \(e_X,e_Y,u_1,u_2,s_1,s_2\) are in the checker; \(c_X,c_Y>0\) are the known closed-form endpoint weights. This positivity follows from the exact endpoint LDL with pivots \(\gamma a t^2\), \(q_2-\gamma a/4\), and \(p_2-\gamma/(4a)-\gamma^2/[16(q_2-\gamma a/4)]\). The remaining two are the strictly positive \(c_X,c_Y\). The odd square has coefficient \(\gamma a^3t^2/4>0\).

Set \(H^{(0)}_{ij}=(Q_\gamma)_{ij}\) for the first five difference rows, with all other even entries zero; take \(g^{(0)}=\gamma a^3t^2/4\). Exact full-parent coefficient matching is
\[
 \mathfrak M(H^{(0)},g^{(0)})=f_0,\tag{11}
\]
verified symbolically for arbitrary \(\gamma\). **Unlike fixing \(\gamma=\gamma_{\max}/2\), we deliberately allow the gauge to approach zero.**

## 4. Positive source-uniform first-order *tree* and the last two rows

Label the six charged rows \((P,Z,UX,VX,UY,VY)=(z_6,\ldots,z_{11})\). Write \(T=a^2t+\eta\). Allocate the six exact first-order forced diagonal masses by the following **five positive edges** \(w_{ij}(z_i-z_j)^2\):
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{edge}&\text{positive source-only weight}\\\hline
P-Z&\dfrac{(1+\rho)(T+\rho-t)}{2(t+1)}\\[1mm]
P-VX&\dfrac{a^2\rho t(1+\rho)}{2(t+1)}\\[1mm]
P-UY&\dfrac{\eta t(1+\rho)}{2(t+1)}\\[1mm]
Z-UX&\dfrac{\rho(t-\rho)}{2(t+1)}\\[1mm]
Z-VY&\dfrac{\eta(1+\rho)T}{2(1-\eta)(t+1)}.
\end{array}\tag{12}
\]
Indeed \(T+\rho-t=(T-1)+(1+\rho-t)>0\); every other numerator and denominator is manifestly positive by (1). These edges form a **connected spanning tree** on all six vertices. Its five-row charge-difference Gram \(L\succ0\) has
\[
 \det L=\prod_{e\in\text{tree}}w_e>0,\tag{13}
\]
and every charge degree matches the *exact source* coefficient of its squared feature in \(f_1\). No auxiliary fractional feature is required.

Let \(Q_0=Q_{\gamma=0}\), a semidefinite endpoint Gram of rank four on the five differences. Define the pure-source old-to-last couplings by
\[
 B_{iX}=\frac{\rho}{a^2t}(Q_0)_{i,3},\qquad
 B_{iY}=-\frac{\eta}{a^2t}(Q_0)_{i,0},
 \qquad i=0,\ldots,4.\tag{14}
\]
The two last rows are the difference roots \(X-UVY,Y-UVY\); their mutual first-order coefficient is **zero**. Also set every charge-to-last first-order coefficient to zero. All source factors in (14) are pure-source rational functions; no test or contact ratio has been inverted.

These prescriptions fix 38 of the 79 first-order Gram entries. The remaining 41 entries are the fifteen old/old coefficients, the twenty-five old/charge coefficients, and the odd scalar. Their static coefficient map has **rank 37**. Its 41 left annihilators all vanish on
\(f_1-\mathfrak M(H_{\rm fixed}^{(1)})\) for the **entire native source domain (1)**. The checker verifies these as exact rational identities after substituting the full-parent source coefficients and their first derivatives. Choose lexicographically the first 37 independent columns and 37 independent rows; invert the constant rational minor, put the remaining four free coordinates to zero, and obtain an **exact source-rational** \((H^{(1)},g^{(1)})\) satisfying
\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak M(H^{(1)},g^{(1)})=f_1}.\tag{15}
\]
Its charged diagonal five-block is \(L\succ0\); its two last-state rows and all their actual cross coefficients are matched, not simply evaluated at contact.

## 5. The last surviving \(2\times2\) Schur implication is strictly positive

Let \(A_\gamma\) be the old five-by-five block of \(H^{(0)}\), let \(B\) be the \(5\times2\) matrix (14), and define the exact source-only matrix
\[
 S_\gamma=\operatorname{diag}(T_X,T_Y)-B^TA_\gamma^{-1}B,
 \quad\begin{cases}
 T_X=\rho^2(n_0+m_0+\tfrac12),\\
 T_Y=\eta^2a^{-2}(A_0+D_0+\tfrac12).
 \end{cases}\tag{16}
\]
This is the **entire** remaining leading tail Schur complement, rather than two separate scalar pivot tests. The matrix \(A_\gamma\succ0\) for any \(0<\gamma<\gamma_{\max}\).

At \(\gamma=0\), \(A_0\) has exactly one extra kernel, \(x=(1,1,1,0,0)^T\), and the derivative has strictly positive kernel pivot
\[
 x^T\partial_\gamma A_\gamma\,x\big|_{\gamma=0}=a^3t^2/4>0.\tag{17}
\]
Both columns of \(B\) lie in \(\operatorname{ran}A_0\): (14) makes each a scalar multiple of a **column of the original rank-four Gram**. Write \(A_0\) in orthogonal coordinates as \(\operatorname{diag}(A_4,0)\), \(A_4\succ0\). Because the kernel pivot in (17) is positive, elementary two-block Schur elimination proves that for \(B\in\operatorname{ran}A_0\)
\[
 \lim_{\gamma\downarrow0}B^TA_\gamma^{-1}B=B^TA_0^+B.
\tag{18}
\]
Here \(A_0^+\) is used only as the **real source-side limiting inverse**, not as a testing/tensor coefficient. The two columns occupy complementary \(X\)- and \(Y\)-blocks of \(A_0\); hence their limiting cross cost is exactly zero. The two diagonal costs equal \((\rho/(a^2t))^2(Q_0)_{33}\) and \((\eta/(a^2t))^2(Q_0)_{00}\). Direct reduction of the original limit weights gives the **exact positive residual**
\[
 \boxed{\lim_{\gamma\downarrow0}S_\gamma
 =\begin{pmatrix}
 \dfrac{\rho t(1+\rho)}{2(t+1)}&0\\
 0&\dfrac{\eta t(1+\rho)}{2(t+1)}
 \end{pmatrix}\succ0.}\tag{19}
\]
Every equation in (17) and both equalities giving (19) are independently checked as rational identities by `verify_finite_c.py`. Consequently, **for every strict face point**, one can choose a sufficiently small \(\gamma>0\) with \(\gamma<\gamma_{\max}\) such that \(S_\gamma\succ0\). An exact terminating, **source-only** choice is \(\gamma_j=\gamma_{\max}/2^{j+2}\), \(j=0,1,\ldots\), choosing the first index for which the two principal-minor inequalities \((S_{\gamma_j})_{11}>0\) and \(\det S_{\gamma_j}>0\) hold. Termination is proved by (19). This tests only source-algebraic rational inequalities; no testing-dependent condition or numerical/SDP search occurs.

## 6. **All-orders** exact matrix and complete PSD proof

By (8), \(f(\varepsilon)\), \(f_0\), and \(f_1\) lie in \(\operatorname{im}\mathfrak M\). Since the six exact source weights have analytic rational expansions at \(\varepsilon=0\), the coefficient vector
\[
 r_2(\varepsilon)=\frac{f(\varepsilon)-f_0-\varepsilon f_1}{\varepsilon^2}
\tag{20}
\]
is a **bounded rational vector** for \(0<\varepsilon\ll1\), with a removable source-only singularity at zero. Let \(\mathcal R\) be the fixed rational right inverse from Step 2 and define the entire Gram, with no unassigned entries, by
\[
 \boxed{(H(\varepsilon),g(\varepsilon))
 =(H^{(0)},g^{(0)})
 +\varepsilon(H^{(1)},g^{(1)})
 +\varepsilon^2\mathcal R\bigl(r_2(\varepsilon)\bigr).}\tag{21}
\]
Because \(\mathfrak M\mathcal R=I\) **on its exact image**, (11), (15), and (20) give
\[
 \boxed{\widetilde{\mathcal P}(U,V;W,X,Y,Z)
 =D(U,V;\psi)^TH(\varepsilon)D(U,V;\psi)
 +g(\varepsilon)UV(W-Z)^2}\tag{22}
\]
*coefficientwise for all independent positive \(U,V\) and all four unrestricted states*, for every admissible \(\varepsilon\) where the native weights are defined. This equation is **not truncated at second order**. Both separate Gram kernels (5) are preserved automatically, for every \(\varepsilon\).

The last-state \(2\times2\) block of \(H(\varepsilon)\) has **exactly**
\[
 \varepsilon^2\operatorname{diag}\left(
 \rho^2(n(\varepsilon)+m(\varepsilon)+\tfrac12),
 \frac{\eta^2}{a^2}(A(\varepsilon)+C(\varepsilon)+D(\varepsilon)+\tfrac12)
 \right);\tag{23}
\]
its off-diagonal entry is identically zero. These entries are forced by the constant-angular \(X^2,Y^2,XY\) coefficients of (3), so the arbitrary right inverse does **not** affect them. By construction, the charge-to-tail block has zero terms through first order. With the root order \((\text{old 5},\text{charge 5},\text{tail 2})\), the full bounded rational Gram thus has the grade structure
\[
 H(\varepsilon)=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 A_\gamma+O(\varepsilon)&\varepsilon B_{01}+O(\varepsilon^2)&\varepsilon B+O(\varepsilon^2)\\
 \varepsilon B_{01}^T+O(\varepsilon^2)&\varepsilon L+O(\varepsilon^2)&O(\varepsilon^2)\\
 \varepsilon B^T+O(\varepsilon^2)&O(\varepsilon^2)&\varepsilon^2T_0+O(\varepsilon^3)
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 g(\varepsilon)=\gamma a^3t^2/4+O(\varepsilon),\tag{24}
\]
where \(A_\gamma\succ0\), \(L\succ0\), and \(T_0=\operatorname{diag}(T_X,T_Y)\). First eliminate the old five-dimensional block. Its Schur complement has charged block \(\varepsilon L+O(\varepsilon^2)\), charge/tail coupling \(O(\varepsilon^2)\), and tail block
\[
 \varepsilon^2 S_\gamma+O(\varepsilon^3).\tag{25}
\]
The charged block is positive and its inverse is \(O(\varepsilon^{-1})\). Eliminating it changes the tail by only \(O(\varepsilon^3)\). Since \(S_\gamma\succ0\), the last tail block is positive for **all sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\)**, including its determinant. The separate odd block remains positive since its constant term is positive. Hence \(H(\varepsilon)\succ0,g(\varepsilon)>0\), proving (22) a legitimate **exact PSD certificate**. No ordinary continuity on the reduced endpoint support has been substituted for this *full graded Schur calculation*.

For each compact \(K\subset(1)\), the positive coefficients and source denominators have uniform strict margins. Equation (19) and source continuity let one select a **single** sufficiently small \(\gamma_K>0\) working for all \(K\); the three fixed graded positive blocks then have uniform lower eigenvalue bounds, while all rational remainder matrices are uniformly bounded. Choosing \(\varepsilon_K>0\) from the finite Schur inequalities makes the entire proof uniform over \(K\). No assertion of a single numerical \(\varepsilon_0\) independent of the face parameters is needed or made.

## 7. Fixed-source Gram is valid in the *original* sixth-root dictionary

The transformations \(u=cU,v=cV\), the source-state scalings in (3)–(4), and multiplication by \(\varepsilon=c^{-2}\) are all **invertible pure-source** operations. Every row in (2) becomes a pure-source multiple of \(u^{i/6}v^{j/6}\psi_l\) with allowed \(0\le i,j\le6\). Thus (22), after dividing by \(\varepsilon>0\), is an exact PSD Gram in the original fixed \(Z_6\), without an extra localizing factor, degree increase, state slice, or testing denominator.

## 8. Exact source inversion transports the theorem to the other end

In the original native parameters, define
\[
 c'=\frac1{ac},\quad a'=a,\quad b'=\frac{a^2}{b},
 \quad r'=\frac{a^2c^2r}{b},\quad t'=\frac{1-c^2(b-a^2t)}b.
\tag{26}
\]
Direct substitution in the **six original native rational weights** proves the exact event permutation
\((n,m,C,A,D,\alpha)'=(n,D,\alpha,A,m,C)\). Independently, the complete parent has the exact homogeneous identity
\[
 u^2v^2 F_{\sigma c}(1/v,1/u;Z,X,Y,W)
 =F_c(u,v;W,X,Y,Z).\tag{27}
\]
Both identities are verified *symbolically* in `verify_inversion.py`, including the original state normal terms. On the sixth-root grid, multiplying \(uv\) after reciprocal substitution takes the indices \((i,j)\) to \((6-j,6-i)\), an exact permutation of \(Z_6\). As \(\varepsilon\to0\), the transformed face coordinates obey
\[
 (b',r',t')\longrightarrow
 \left(\frac1t,\frac\rho t,\frac{1-\eta}{a^2t}\right),\tag{28}
\]
which bijectively satisfies the open zero-end inequalities \(0<r'<1,0<t'<1,b'+r'>1,b'>a^2t'\). Hence the admitted infinity-end neighborhoods transfer exactly to the admitted zero-end neighborhoods. This is the only use of source inversion.
