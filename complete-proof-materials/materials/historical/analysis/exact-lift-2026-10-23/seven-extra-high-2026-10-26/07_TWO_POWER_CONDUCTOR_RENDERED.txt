Theorem A: filtered two-stage conductor
Let $K_c,K_t$ be separately labelled real-closed extensions of $\mathbb R$. Let $\mathcal A\subset B_c$ be a domain of bounded source elements containing $\varepsilon,j,E,k=\varepsilon j$ and closed under the unit-denominator operations below, with $\varepsilon,j,E>0$. Work in $R=\mathcal A[u,\omega,v]$ (or clear finitely many positive, bounded testing-only denominators beforehand). Set
$ v_0=\varepsilon j^2E,\quad J=(u-k,\omega-j,v-v_0),\quad
 A=\varepsilon\omega v,\ B=v,\ C=Eu\omega,\ D_0=\varepsilon^2j^3E,$$ x=B-C,\quad y=A-kC,\quad z=uB-kC,\quad h_0=kC-D_0.$Let $q_1,q_2$ be the separated circuit roots with squared monomials $A,uB,kC,D_0$; equivalently
$ q_1=\sqrt\varepsilon\sqrt{u\omega}\,(v-v_0),\qquad
 q_2=\varepsilon\sqrt{jE}\sqrt{uv}\,(\omega-j).$The prefactors in these formulas are products of pure-source and pure-testing radicals. In particular the square roots are elements of the separated tensor ring.
Consider the complete normal-quadratic expression
$ \mathscr F=F_0+2NG_N+2M G_M+aN^2+2bNM+cM^2,$$ F_0=f_AA^2+f_{BB}B^2+f_{BC}BC+f_{CC}C^2+\varpi D_0^2,
 \quad G_T=g_{TA}A+g_{TB}B+g_{TC}C\quad(T=N,M).$Suppose exactly that (i) $f_A,f_{BB},f_{BC},f_{CC},\varpi\in\varepsilon^2 R$; (ii) $g_{T\bullet}\in\varepsilon R$; (iii) $F_0\in J^2$, $G_N,G_M\in J$; and (iv) $a,b,c\in R$ have bounded evaluations. These are hypotheses about formal bounded coquotients, not just small values after evaluating at one testing point.
Conclusion. There is a finite explicit $H\in\mathrm{Sym}_8(B_c\otimes_{\mathbb R}B_t)$ with
$ \mathscr F=\mathscr R^TH\mathscr R,
 \qquad \mathscr R=(N,M,x,y,z,h_0,q_1,q_2)^T.$Both monic conductor stages may set $V=0$; no angular parity condition is necessary. The statement holds whether $\varepsilon$ is a positive unit or an infinitesimal, provided the stated bounded coquotients exist. No inverse of $\varepsilon,j,E$, a contact difference, or a mixed source/testing element appears in $H$.
Proof and hand-reproducible algorithm
For a polynomial $P$, perform ordered monic subtraction:
$ \begin{aligned}
 T_u(P)&=\frac{P(u,\omega,v)-P(k,\omega,v)}{u-k},\\
 T_\omega(P)&=\frac{P(k,\omega,v)-P(k,j,v)}{\omega-j},\\
 T_v(P)&=\frac{P(k,j,v)-P(k,j,v_0)}{v-v_0}.
 \end{aligned}$The displayed quotients are polynomial divided differences, computable by $(X^n-Y^n)/(X-Y)=\sum_{a=0}^{n-1}X^{n-1-a}Y^a$, without inversion. If $P_c=0$ and $T_\omega(P)=\varepsilon U$, put
$ \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal L_x(P)&=-uT_u(P)-\varepsilon\omega U-Ek\omega T_v(P),\\
 \mathcal L_y(P)&=U+Ej T_v(P),\\
 \mathcal L_z(P)&=T_u(P)+E\omega T_v(P).
 \end{aligned}}\tag{A1}$Then the exact conductor identities give
$ CP=x\mathcal L_x(P)+y\mathcal L_y(P)+z\mathcal L_z(P).\tag{A2}$Indeed $C(u-k)=z-ux$, $\varepsilon C(\omega-j)=y-\varepsilon\omega x$, and $C(v-v_0)=E(\omega z+jy-k\omega x)$.
First scalar division. Define
$ S=k^2f_A+f_{BB}+f_{BC}+f_{CC}+k^2\varpi,
 \quad Q_2=f_Ay^2+f_{BB}x^2+\varpi h_0^2.$Direct substitution $A=y+kC,\ B=x+C,\ D_0=kC-h_0$ gives
$ F_0=Q_2+C\{x(2f_{BB}+f_{BC})+2kf_Ay-2k\varpi h_0\}+C^2S.\tag{A3}$At contact $C_c=\varepsilon j^2E\ne0$; $F_{0,c}=0$ forces $S_c=0$ by cancellation in the source domain. Since $S=\varepsilon^2S_2$, all its monic quotients have $\varepsilon^2$ as a formal factor. Hence $U_S=T_\omega(S)/\varepsilon\in\varepsilon R$, so (A1) applies with $V=0$. It yields
$ \mathcal L_x(S)\in\varepsilon^2R,\qquad
 \mathcal L_y(S)\in\varepsilon R,\qquad
 \mathcal L_z(S)\in\varepsilon^2R.\tag{A4}$Put
$ \begin{aligned}
 H_x&=2f_{BB}+f_{BC}+\mathcal L_x(S),\\
 H_y&=2kf_A+\mathcal L_y(S),\\
 H_z&=\mathcal L_z(S),\\
 H_h&=-2k\varpi,
 \end{aligned}
 \quad \lambda=2k\varpi_c,
 \quad J_i=H_i-\lambda(-2k,1,1,-1)_i.$Thus, before using contact derivatives,
$ H_x\in\varepsilon^2R,\quad H_y\in\varepsilon R,
 \quad H_z\in\varepsilon^2R,\quad H_h\in\varepsilon^3R,
 \quad\lambda\in\varepsilon^3R,$and consequently
$ \boxed{(J_x,J_y,J_z,J_h)\in
 \varepsilon^2R\times\varepsilon R\times\varepsilon^2R\times\varepsilon^3R.}\tag{A5}$The exact circuit relation is
$ r_*=y+z-h_0-2kx\in J^2,\qquad
 kCr_*=q_1^2-yz=q_2^2+zh_0.\tag{A6}$Because $F_0\in J^2$ and $Q_2\in J^2$, the first derivative of (A3) after the first division gives $\sum H_{i,c}\nabla r_{i,c}=0$. Since $r_*\in J^2$, the coefficient vector $(-2k,1,1,-1)$ is an exact kernel vector of those gradients. Its $h_0$ entry is $-1$, while $H_{h,c}=-2k\varpi_c=-\lambda$. Therefore $\sum J_{i,c}\nabla r_{i,c}=0$ and $J_{h,c}=0$. Finally,
$ \det \frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,\omega,v)}\bigg|_c
 =-E^2\varepsilon^3j^4\ne0.$Invertibility over the source field proves $J_{x,c}=J_{y,c}=J_{z,c}=0$. This uses no inverse of the infinitesimal determinant in any Gram coefficient.
Second scalar division, with no parity. Every $J_i\in\varepsilon R$, so each monic quotient $T_\omega(J_i)\in\varepsilon R$. Choose simply
$ \boxed{U_i=T_\omega(J_i)/\varepsilon\in R,\qquad V_i=0.}\tag{A7}$The quotient is extracted from its known formal $\varepsilon$ factor; it is bounded. Apply (A1) to each $J_i$, writing $\ell_{ix}=\mathcal L_x(J_i)$, $\ell_{iy}=\mathcal L_y(J_i)$, $\ell_{iz}=\mathcal L_z(J_i)$. Equations (A3),(A6) yield the complete scalar identity
$ \boxed{F_0=Q_2+2\varpi_c(q_1^2-yz)
 +\sum_{i=x,y,z,h_0}r_i(x\ell_{ix}+y\ell_{iy}+z\ell_{iz}).}\tag{A8}$Both normals. For each $T=N,M$ set $S_T=kg_{TA}+g_{TB}+g_{TC}\in\varepsilon R$. From $G_T=g_{TB}x+g_{TA}y+CS_T$ and $G_T\in J$ it follows that $(S_T)_c=0$. Thus $T_\omega(S_T)\in\varepsilon R$. With $U_T=T_\omega(S_T)/\varepsilon$ and $V_T=0$, (A1) gives
$ \boxed{G_T=\gamma_{Tx}x+\gamma_{Ty}y+\gamma_{Tz}z,\quad
 \gamma_T=(g_{TB}+\mathcal L_x(S_T),\ g_{TA}+\mathcal L_y(S_T),\ \mathcal L_z(S_T),0,0,0)^T.}\tag{A9}$These are the full first-normal rows, not contact restrictions.
The full matrix. In the soft basis $(x,y,z,h_0,q_1,q_2)$, let $e_i$ be coordinate vectors and $\widetilde\ell_i=(\ell_{ix},\ell_{iy},\ell_{iz},0,0,0)^T$. Define
$ \begin{aligned}
 H_s={}&f_{BB}e_xe_x^T+f_Ae_ye_y^T+\varpi e_he_h^T
 +\varpi_c(e_{q_1}e_{q_1}^T+e_{q_2}e_{q_2}^T)\\
 &-\frac{\varpi_c}{2}(e_ye_z^T+e_ze_y^T)
 +\frac{\varpi_c}{2}(e_ze_h^T+e_he_z^T)\\
 &+\frac12\sum_{i=x,y,z,h_0}(e_i\widetilde\ell_i^T+\widetilde\ell_ie_i^T),\\
 H={}&\begin{pmatrix}
 a&b&\gamma_N^T\\ b&c&\gamma_M^T\\
 \gamma_N&\gamma_M&H_s
 \end{pmatrix}.\tag{A10}
 \end{aligned}$The equal circuit split follows from $q_1^2-q_2^2=z(y+h_0)$. Substitution of (A8),(A9) proves $\mathscr F=\mathscr R^TH\mathscr R$. Every operation uses sums/products, formal factor extraction, or positive unit inverses, so every entry is bounded. QED.
Theorem B: application to the entire native TL6 correction
Retain the exact original R10.2 conditions and the exact TL3 coefficient identities. Distinguish source $w_s=ae$ and $j_R$ from test $\omega$ and SE contact $j$. Source $s=1/a$, $b_s=a^2B$, $x_s=sb_s=k^2$, $w_s=ae$, $e=sw_s$; $t,g,j_R$ are positive units and $s,B,h,e,x_s$ infinitesimal, while $\rho=n/e^2>0$ is source-infinitesimal.

Full TL6 stress, including both normals and the hard block. Put $D_u=1+u^2$, $D_v=1+v^2$, $A_t=v\omega$, $X=N+pD_uA_t-u$,

Use uniformly the SE-normalized soft frame

One frame for CA and SE, with an essential base distinction
The unit-scale CA monomials are $(\omega v,\delta v,\delta Eu\omega,\delta^2k^3E)$, with $\delta=1/\varepsilon$. Equation (B5) says exactly
$ (A,B,C,D_0)_{\rm CA}=\delta(A,B,C,D_0)_{\rm SE}.$Every CA soft root $x,y,z,h_0,q_1,q_2$ is therefore $\delta$ times the corresponding SE root. The original normals $N,M_B$ are unchanged, not $(N,\delta M_B)$. When $\varepsilon$ is a positive unit, $\delta$ and $\delta^{-1}$ are bounded source units, so the two full eight-root frames are related by the invertible bounded congruence
$ D=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,\delta,\delta,\delta,\delta,\delta,\delta),
 \quad \mathscr R_{\rm CA}=D\mathscr R_{\rm SE},
 \quad G_{\rm SE}=D^TG_{\rm CA}D.$This unifies the entire correction matrix algorithm for positive-unit and infinitesimal $\varepsilon$. When $\varepsilon$ is infinitesimal, $\delta$ is unbounded and this CA-to-SE congruence is not an allowed bounded-residue transfer; one must use the independently established SE formal-base residue. When $\varepsilon$ is a unit, the already established CA base-residue positivity transfers through the bounded congruence. Thus base positivity still has two checks; the correction conductor does not.
Finally $\rho>0$ is source-infinitesimal, so conditional on the separately checked formal base $\overline{G_0}\succ0$, the local actual Gram has $\overline{G_0+\rho H}=\overline{G_0}\succ0$. Restore the positive TL4 row, retained centered-$A$ packet and original positive event packets. This proves only the prescribed local thin-R10 tensor-SOS chart, conditional on the imported TL3 and base identities, with source/testing variables independent throughout.
