7. Sharpness and logical boundaries
Why first-stage w-dependence must have an ε² factor for this algorithm. Take
$ F_0=\varepsilon C^2(w-j)^2$with only f_CC=ε(w-j)² nonzero. This lies in (A,B,C)^2∩J²; all raw coefficients reduce modulo ε to even, w-independent polynomials. Its first T_w(S)=ε(w-j) is divisible by ε but not ε². Applying the prescribed first conductor gives H_y=w-j, and consequently the second T_w(J_y)=1\notin(ε,v). The polynomial itself is an elementary square; this counterexample refutes only the automatic second-stage inference from the weaker hypotheses. It shows exactly why the stronger divisibility must be established on the actual source coefficients.
Minimal second-stage test. For any contact-vanishing J_i in the algorithm, T_w(J_i)∈(ε,v) is equivalent to the formal polynomial J_i(0,w,0) being independent of w (with the other source letters retained). This is the minimal direct test when the convenient S1 parity/ε² conditions are unavailable. Either proof gives the bounded conductor.
Why the raw support matters. In the CC system, Cr_*∈(A,B,C,D_0)^2∩J², but is not in the square of its five soft roots by the weight-42/x-divisibility test printed at group_01.tex:1470–1478. Hence the unrestricted replacement (A,B,C,D_0)^2 is false. The present theorem uses only (6), corresponding to (A,B,C)^2+(D_0^2), and never assumes the invalid larger ideal inclusion. Because the CC and SE normalizations scale x by ε, CC coefficient boundedness cannot simply be transferred to SE.
Boundedness is an independent hypothesis. Polynomial division proves exact membership before specialization. To obtain a matrix over B_c⊗_ℝ B_t, the native application must verify that every raw coefficient and formal coquotient has bounded value after legal unit denominator clearing. In particular, an expression with a factor 1/ε is not accepted solely because its complete parent is bounded. The sufficient hypothesis here is explicitly polynomial, or a separately verified bounded coquotient. No order on the tensor product and no mixed Cholesky factor is used.

Check S0, S1, N1, B1 on the literal quarter-parent stress \mathscr E/\rho_{thin}. Formulas (9), (11), (13), (16)–(17), (24), (26)–(28) then produce its eight-root matrix exactly.
