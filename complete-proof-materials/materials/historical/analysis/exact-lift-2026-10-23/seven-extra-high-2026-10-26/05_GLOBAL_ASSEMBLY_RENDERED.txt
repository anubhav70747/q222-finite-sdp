1. Exact criterion, without a uniformity strengthening
Corollary 3.19 of arXiv:2004.04196v2: if Q is closed, convex and semialgebraic, it is a spectrahedral shadow if and only if for every real closed extension K/R, every f in (P_Q)_K and q in Q_K, the point-first tensor evaluation sum q_nu tensor f_nu (include the constant) is SOS in K tensor_R K. Corollary 3.19 asks only for finitely many squares for each independently chosen triple K,f,q. One does not need beforehand a uniform number of squares, word list, matrix size, or cap, and no two embeddings into a common field are used.
The proof of Corollary 3.19 uses the constructible/real-spectrum compactness arguments of Lemmas 3.15–3.17, followed by Theorem 2.10. 6. From Corollary 3.19 to one finite affine pencil: explicit logical construction and both inclusions
The published Corollary 3.19 and its proof supply one affine real variety X, morphism phi:X->A^8, and a fixed finite-dimensional U subset R[X] with Q subset phi(X(R)) and phi^* P subset Sigma U^2. Add 1 to U if needed and choose a fixed basis u_1,...,u_N. Let V=span_R{1,phi^*x_1,...,phi^*x_8,u_i u_j:1<=i,j<=N} subset R[X], a finite-dimensional real vector space. Introduce a real linear functional L:V->R as finitely many auxiliary variables, and imposeL(1)=1, L(phi^*x_nu)=q_nu, M(L)=[L(u_i u_j)]_{i,j=1}^N psd.All relations in V are fixed finite real linear relations. Choosing coordinates on the affine slice yields a single fixed block pencil A_0 + sum_nu q_nu A_nu + sum_j y_j B_j psd (with linear equalities either eliminated or encoded as scalar PSD blocks). No contact or field parameters appear in the matrix coefficients.
Forward inclusion: given q in Q, choose x in X(R) with phi(x)=q. Evaluation L(g)=g(x) satisfies constraints; M=u(x)u(x)^t psd.
Reverse inclusion: if a functional L is feasible, take any f in P. The published finite-library property gives phi^*f=sum_k (sum_i a_ki u_i)^2, hence f(q)=L(phi^*f)=sum_k a_k^t M a_k>=0. The intersection of all supporting half-spaces {f>=0} is exactly closed convex Q, by finite-dimensional separation, so q in Q. Thus both inclusions hold, without assuming PSD moment matrices correspond to measures or particular source/test points.
The construction is a proof of existence, not an evaluated pencil: the corollary does not specify a computable X, basis U, N, or coefficient entries from the local certificates. It therefore does not justify an explicit small lift, a claim sxdeg=2, or a particular NPA hierarchy level. Because the finite defining formula for Q has rational data, once particular finite sizes exist, elementary transfer to the real algebraic numbers supplies algebraic entries of some representing pencil. This still does not evaluate the matrices.
