      R10 Thin Native Gate Proof



 


Q222: the native thin-R10 bounded-matrix gate
Scope. This note proves the local statement asserted in TL6–TL10 of the recovered October R21 source. It is not a global source-cover or a finite-SDP-lift theorem. The matrix factors below are indefinite bounded corrections; positivity is recovered by adding them, with the infinitesimal source factor $\rho$, to a strictly positive-residue formal-C1 base. All formulas use the quarter parent $F/4$. We distinguish the R10 source variables $w_s=ae$, $j_R$ from the independent testing variable $\omega=\mathfrak w$ and the SE source coordinate $j_s$.
Statement
Let $K_c,K_t\supset\mathbb R$ be independently labelled real-closed fields. Take an actual native weak-C2 R10 source with the complete conditions of R10.2, including positive-unit $t,g,j_R$, positive infinitesimal $s=1/a,B,h,e,x=aB$, and $0<\rho=n/e^2\in\operatorname{Inf}(K_c)$. Retain the original gaps. On the double-infinitesimal testing chart $Z=1$, $u,v,\omega>0$ infinitesimal, use the actual CA/SE hard normals $N,M_B$. Let $\mathscr E$ be exactly TL6 after retaining the entire positive contact-zero TL4 square and the positive centered-$A$ excess packet.
Then for each of the two source-scale alternatives $tb$ a positive unit or infinitesimal, with the corresponding actual eight-root frame $\mathcal R=(x_0,y_0,z_0,h_0,q_1,q_2,N,M_B)^T$, there exists a symmetric matrix $H\in\operatorname{Sym}_8(B_c\otimes_{\mathbb R}B_t)$ such that
$\boxed{\mathscr E/\rho=\mathcal R^T H\mathcal R.}\tag{G}$The matrix is constructed by the finite algorithm in §4 below; it uses no division by a source infinitesimal, by a contact difference, or by a mixed source/testing expression. Independently, the exact CA or SE formal-C1 remainder has a positive-definite real-residue Gram even though its native $\beta_1=-s_n/2<0$. Therefore the thin local actual-C2 quarter-parent has a separated tensor SOS for all independent source/test relative rates in this chart.
Exact inputs. Native coefficient differences TL3, original R10.2, formal-contact OP4/OP5, the polynomial-state inverse TL7, and the elementary contact identities CC1–CC6 / SE5–SE7. The proof below verifies their native application; it never substitutes contact evaluation for a full-state identity. The algebraic source packets and CA/SE residues used are printed in the recovered group_01.tex, group_03.tex, and group_07.tex.

5. Strict real residue of the formal negative-beta C1 base

$ 0<s_n=O(\rho w_s),\quad

Thus the formal base's selected packets are actually nonnegative despite its negative raw beta. The C1 source normalization to $C_1=2$, whenever used, is a positive unit, and $\kappa$ and the quarter-parent factor $1/4$ likewise preserve positive residue.
