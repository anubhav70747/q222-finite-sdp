This is a stronger formulation than merely saying positive-definite Grams continue locally: every possible failure is witnessed by a nonzero PSD dual moment functional satisfying
$\boxed{
A_\sigma^*y\succeq0,\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^*y)=1,\qquad

Such a witness is a zero-cost pseudo-contact. It is not automatically an actual additional quantum contact.

A general truncated PSD moment functional need not be a positive combination of such evaluations. Excluding those nonrepresentable functionals is the mathematical content missing from a contact-only positivity argument.
