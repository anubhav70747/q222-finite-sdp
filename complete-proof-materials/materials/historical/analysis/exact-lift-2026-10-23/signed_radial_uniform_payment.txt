UNIFORM PAYMENT OF THE R1 SIGNED RADIAL MASS

Domain and notation. Use the actual RSR4--6/15--21 dictionary in
latex/certificates/c2_short.tex, with delta=2^-10, 0<B,jB,T<=delta,
0<p<min(j,1), 0<H<1, n>=0. Put x=jB,y=TB, J=j+1,K=J-p,
kap=-kc>0, h0=mu+1/2, Me=n h0 j^2/(n+h0), Sigma=Me+fc/y.
All denominators below are pure source. This concerns only radial
terms, not the completion of either state normal.

1. Exact averaged identity.

Set theta=Me/Sigma. Write A27=(1+U^2)Psi-R_*^2 and
B29=2(Psi+V^2 Q_*^2+U^2 B_*^2)-R_*^2, the separated SOS in
RSR27/29, with U=u/sqrt(x),V=v/sqrt(y). Then

 fc aPsi Psi+qc aQ Q_*^2+Me*y aE B_*^2-kap R_*^2
     =kap[(1-theta)A27+theta B29],                       (1)
 aPsi=kap(1+theta)/fc+kap*u^2/(Sigma*y*x),
 aQ=2kap*theta*v^2/(qc*y),
 aE=2kap*u^2/(Sigma*y*x).

Indeed (1-theta)/fc=1/(Sigma*y), while theta=Me/Sigma;
these identities match all four coefficients, without cancelling Me.
At n=0, theta=Me=0, Sigma=fc/y>0: (1) is literally kap A27.
The SOS identities are used in the nonnegative angle chart; their
coefficient bounds below involve only u^2,v^2 and apply after the fixed
angle/state sign gauges as well. No independent state bound is used.

2. Uniform source bounds, including arbitrary j and jT.

Let f0=1-delta-delta^3. The previous exact derivation in
full_r1_budget_independent.txt gives
 P>=J, E>=1-delta, L>=f0 K, f0 K<=F<=2K,
 p^2<=(45/2)Sigma.
For completeness, L>=K-T-BT^2 follows directly from RSR5.
The regrouped F1,F2 there give F1<=4K,F2<=3delta K, proving
F<=K(1+4delta+3delta^3)<2K. From RSR18, dropping only negative
terms gives
 Kcal/J<=p+2B+TB(2+delta)+T^2B^2<=p+3B.                 (2)
RSR19 also gives Kcal>=p(1-p)(1-BJ)>0, so kap>0.

Define Gq=(1+x)(1+Hy)(K+Hjy)/(1-HT)-xF. Exactly
 qc=Tx Gq/(2P), Gq>=(1-2delta)K>0.
Consequently, throughout the full source domain,
 kap/(qc*y)=(1+x)Kcal/(E Gq)
 <=(1+delta)(2+6delta)/[(1-delta)(1-2delta)]<3.           (3)

If p>=1/2, then j>=1/2 and Sigma>=1/90. Equations (2) and
F>=f0K give the explicit bounds
 kap/fc=T(1+x)Kcal/(E*j*F)
 <=T(1+delta)(2+12delta)/[(1-delta)f0]<3T,
 kap/(Sigma*y*x)=T(1+x)Kcal/(2EP Sigma)
 <=45T(1+delta)(1+3delta)/(1-delta)<48T.                 (4)
Thus aPsi<=6T+48T*u^2, aQ<=6v^2, aE<=96T*u^2.
Every bound is uniform as n->0, j-p->0, H->0 or1, or j,jT grow.

If p<1/2, no averaging is needed:
 kap/rc=T Kcal/[(1-p)(1+Hy)L]
 <=T(2p+6delta)/[(1-p)f0]<3T.                           (5)

3. Whole signed square and fixed gates.

RSR24 gives Z_*(V^2-1)=-VQ_*-R_*, and exactly
 2kap V^2 Q_*^2+2kap R_*^2-kap Z_*^2(V^2-1)^2
     =kap(VQ_*-R_*)^2.
For p>=1/2 combine this with twice (1). The whole negative kc term
therefore costs fractions at most
 12T+96T*u^2 on fc Psi,
 18v^2 on qc Q_*^2,
 192T*u^2 on Me*y B_*^2.
On |u|<=2^-12, |v|<=2^-25 these are, respectively,
 <1/64, <2^-45, <2^-26.
For p<1/2, (3),(5) instead cost <1/128 of rc R_*^2 and <2^-47
of qc Q_*^2. Thus the three old RSR25--29 source cases are replaced
for this signed mass by two numerical p intervals.

These are coefficient bounds, not pointwise positivity in a tensor.
For example, write 2aPsi=c0+c2*u^2 with source-only c0,c2>=0,
bPsi=c0+c2*2^-24<1/64. Then exactly
 1-2aPsi=(1-bPsi)+c2*2^-24(1-2^24 u^2).
The Q and current fractions have the same identity with the fixed
gates 1-2^50 v^2 and 1-2^24 u^2. Constants in the p<1/2 case
are source-only positive residues. Thus all residual scalar factors
have explicit fixed-gate decompositions; no adaptive real threshold
is needed for this allocation. Other radial purchases and all normal
couplings still require their own simultaneous budget.
