Result
------
The announced parent identities and affine pencil construction are correct,
provided the retained positive core square d*J^2/2 is explicit. This term
is present in the corrected integrated note. No other algebraic defect
was found. The independent exact calculation produced the claimed 36
Alice coefficient records and 15 Bob coefficient records.

Full-parent restoration
------------------------
The check starts from the original fully homogeneous four-state parent,
not a contact-state or state-normal restriction. On the two unit circles,
its compact value P=F_h/4 equals the stated compact source expression.
The displayed residual G is exact after retaining
  lambda E_lambda^2 + beta L_A^2 + nX^2 + mM^2 + C(cE)^2
       +(eE,J) S_tau (eE,J)^T + dJ^2/2.
Omitting the last term leaves a discrepancy of exactly dJ^2/2.
Every state coordinate W,X,Y,Z is independent throughout the check.

Why unrestricted auxiliary coefficients are sound
-------------------------------------------------
Write V=cK, ell=cY, Y0=eY and g^2=b(B-b), zeta^2=1-b.
The three auxiliary matrix coefficients evaluate to the following zero
testing identities:
  tau: 2(eE-J)bV/B + 2{(bV)^2+(gV)^2}/B^2 = 0,
  p:   b{aR ell-U ell-b^2 ell^2-bVY0} = 0,
  q:   b^2 ell^2{a^2+b^2-1}/2 = 0.
They use only J-eE=V, allocation identity (2), and the circle/gate
equations. Thus H_B^T Q20 H_B=P for arbitrary independent tau,p,q;
q=p^2/d is not imposed or used for the identity. All twelve coefficient
identities were checked separately. The source-only assignments
p=d*h/(1+h), q=d*h^2/(1+h)^2 are sufficient PSD witnesses, not constraints
on the affine pencil.

Sufficient PSD conditions and zero cases
----------------------------------------
With nonnegative source coordinates, S_tau>=0, rho=tau-d/2>=0 and
4A*rho>=d^2 P_B^2, the source witness decomposition makes Q20 PSD.
The first two normal blocks are PSD by
  d*q=p^2,
  alpha*(d/2-p)-p^2/4
     =d{2alpha(1-h^2)-d*h^2}/[4(1+h)^2]=0.
The remaining diagonal terms are nonnegative. H_+,H_0,Q_* are PSD because
P_B>=B/2, using 0<=h<=1. This includes d=0, alpha=0 and the branch
d=alpha=0 with h=0; n,lambda,beta may vanish without any division.
Other zero-source specializations require the displayed PSD conditions;
they are not all admitted automatically.

The Bob identity (5) and all nine Bob coefficient identities also hold
for the full homogeneous parent. Its source witness condition is
S_(d/(2delta^2))>=0. Its matrix is affine in the original nine sources;
tau0=2^51*d introduces no auxiliary or nonlinear source constraint.

Testing endpoints and limits of the result
-------------------------------------------
The exact identities hold on the closed algebraic covers, with arbitrary
state signs and both signs of a,c,e. There is no inverse of a testing or
state coordinate. Alice includes b=0 (u=infinity), b=B, and every Bob
endpoint. Bob includes |c|=delta, c=+/-1,e=0 (v=infinity), and every
Alice endpoint including u=0,infinity. Zero gate values are literal.

The uncovered cap remains b>B and |c|<delta; in particular u=v=0 is
not covered. The native contact has cE=L/sqrt((1+z)(1+y))>0 while P=0,
so a globally positive decomposition retaining C(cE)^2 cannot certify
that contact. Neither pencil nor their chart union is a full canonical
certificate. Source-envelope estimates were assigned to the other
reviewer and were not duplicated here. No global lift or contact-cap
completion is inferred from these local identities.

The exact SymPy checker passed in approximately 1.3 seconds. It used no
