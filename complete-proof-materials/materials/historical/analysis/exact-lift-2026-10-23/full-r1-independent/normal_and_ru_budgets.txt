RADIAL AND NORMAL PURCHASE BUDGETS ON THE FULL QUANTITATIVE R1 BOX

Scope. This independently verifies the source estimates and purchase
bounds in equations1--3/5 of pro_full_r1_completion_reply.txt. It does
not verify the core matrix, full-parent contraction, support export,
closedness or complementary testing coverage. All source coefficients
are the actual RSR4--6/15--21 rational dictionary, not independent values.
Let d=2^-10, x=jB,y=TB, 0<B,x,T<=d, 0<p<min(j,1), 0<H<1,n>=0.
Write J=j+1,K=J-p, eta=B+x, h0=mu+1/2, Me=n h0j^2/(n+h0),
Sigma=Me+fc/y, theta=Me/Sigma. No bound on j or jT is imposed.

1. Source estimates and the phi cancellation.

Put f0=1-d-d^3, l0=1+d+d^2+d^3 and
 R0=1+2d+2d^2+4d^3+d^4(1+2d).
The exact termwise estimates previously proved give
 J<=P<=R0 J, 1-d<=E<=1+d^2,
 f0 K<=L<=l0 K, f0 K<=F<=2K,
 Gq>=(1-2d)K, qc=Tx Gq/(2P),
 fc>=Tx^2/8, qc>=Tx/8, rc>=TjB^2(1-p)/8.
Also c:=BTCH=B L/(2P)<B, and
 alpha<=B^2 J*(1+d^2)l0/[2(1-d)]<=B(B+x)=B eta.
These estimates hold down to H->0,1 and n=0.

Let Dp=1-p+p^2>=3/4 and phi=p^2/Dp. Exactly
 (1-phi)/(1-p)=1/Dp<=4/3,
 phi/Sigma<=128/3, phi/j<=4p/3<=4/3.
The second bound uses p^2<=32Sigma (independently strengthened to
(45/2)Sigma in full_r1_budget_independent.txt). Thus all cancellations
are source-only and retain literal n=0; no inverse of Me is used.

2. Jalpha and Ru, including their interaction.

The exact identity Jalpha=j alpha b=-(alpha/B)Ru-alpha uR gives
equation1 by 2a^2+2b^2-(a+b)^2=(a-b)^2. There is no sign defect:
 (1+U^2)Psi=Bstar^2+Rstar^2+U^2Q^2+U^2(w-UVZstar)^2,
so either Bstar^2 or Rstar^2 may be subtracted in the RFN identity.
Its costs per Jalpha^2 are bounded respectively by
 2eta^2 on Ru^2,
 (64/3)eta^2 u^2 on rc Rstar^2,
 8B eta^2+(32/3)eta^2 u^2 on fc Psi.
These follow directly from alpha<=B eta and the three phi inequalities.

For the Ru purchase, the printed coefficients rPsi,rQ,rE,rR have
coefficientwise absolute majorants (s=u^2)
 rPsi*:32B+(256/3)s+(512/3)(eta+s)^2,
 rQ*:64Bs+(256/3)s^2,
 rE*:(1280/3)(eta+s)^2,
 rR*:(128/3)s.                                          (A)
For example, xy/fc<=8/j, y/qc<=8/j and x^2y/fc<=8B.
Apply phi/j<=4/3 to the s and s^2 terms, phi/Sigma<=128/3
to all harmonic terms, and (1-phi)/(1-p)<=4/3 to rR.
For rE, replace (s-x)^2 by its coefficient majorant (s+x)^2;
the latter minus the former is 4xs, a positive polynomial square.
This proves exactly equation3, rather than merely pointwise bounds.

Funding 16Jalpha^2 adds 32(1-phi)(alpha/B)^2 Ru^2. Consequently
the effective Ru multiplier is 16+32(1-phi)(alpha/B)^2
<=16+32eta^2<17. This interaction is included in every total below.

3. Opposite-normal helper O and the signed mass.

O=sqrt(j)T v(alpha-cu^2)Rstar. Replace its multiplier by
 Cbar=2jT^2v^2(alpha^2+c^2u^4).
This replacement is exact up to the separated square
 Cbar Rstar^2-O^2=jT^2v^2(alpha+cu^2)^2 Rstar^2.
Average the direct Rstar^2 purchase with weight1-phi and the
theta-harmonic purchase with weight phi. Their scalar fractions are
 bPsi=Cbar*phi[(1+theta)/fc+s/(Sigma*y*x)],
 bQ=2Cbar*phi*theta*v^2/(qc*y),
 bE=2Cbar*phi*s/(Sigma*y*x), bR=Cbar*(1-phi)/rc.
Using alpha<=B eta,c<=B gives the coefficient majorants
 bPsi<=(128/3)T v^2(1+2s)(eta^2+s^2),
 bQ<=32v^4(eta^2+s^2),
 bE<=(512/3)T s v^2(eta^2+s^2),
 bR<=(64/3)T v^2(eta^2+s^2).                            (B)
They involve no moving testing root; O uses only v and u^2v.

The same phi-direct/harmonic average pays the entire negative kc
square. From Kcal/J<=p+3B, the exact ratios give
 phi*kap/fc<=3T, phi*kap/(Sigma*y*x)<=24T,
 kap/(qc*y)<=3, (1-phi)*kap/rc<=3T.
Use Zstar(V^2-1)=-VQ-Rstar and
 2kap V^2Q^2+2kap Rstar^2-kap Zstar^2(V^2-1)^2
   =kap(VQ-Rstar)^2.
The resulting four losses are bounded by
 12T+48Ts, 18v^2, 96Ts, 6T, respectively.                 (C)
These uniform bounds remove the previous p split for this purchase.
