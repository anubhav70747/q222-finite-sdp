Exact full-source symmetry used for contact-family transport

For c=(d,lambda,n,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta), define
Tc=(d,beta,n,D,alpha,A,m,C,lambda).
For the published full canonical polynomial F,
u^2 v^2 F_c(1/v,1/u; Z,X,Y,W)
= F_{Tc}(u,v; W,X,Y,Z).
The map is an involution and preserves nonnegative source coordinates.

This was checked by independently rebuilding all nine coefficient
polynomials, substituting simultaneously, and solving the unique linear
source map. All coefficients agree exactly; no source face was imposed.

A direct proof transports the lambda/beta, m/D, C/alpha terms in pairs.
The n and A terms stay fixed. For the d coefficient, use the exact identity
TD^2+Du(vY-Z)^2+Dv(X+uZ)^2-DuDv Z^2
= Tm^2+Dv(uW-Y)^2+Du(W+vX)^2-DuDv W^2,
where TD=uW+uvX-Y-vZ and Tm=vW-X+uvY-uZ.
It follows by expansion, and proves the d coefficient is unchanged.

Homogenizing replaces the multiplied reciprocal identity by a literal
identity under (a0,a1;b0,b1)->(b1,b0;a1,a0), W<->Z.
Thus zero and infinite angles are included by the same polynomial identity.

This establishes validity transport, not membership in a prescribed
certificate cone. A testing family using fractional powers only of Bob's
angle generally transports to functions involving Alice's angle.
A source reduction may therefore require adjoining its transported testing
family; one cannot assume the original 40 functions already contain it.

Status: exact symmetry proved. No global adequacy conclusion.
The Pro task is currently preparing a purported separated counterexample
at a symmetry image; its actual source and dual are not yet recovered.
