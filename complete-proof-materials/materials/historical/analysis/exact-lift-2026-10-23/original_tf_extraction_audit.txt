Original existence route: bounded extraction audit, 23 October 2026

Conclusion. The constructive identities give finite local root templates,
but their present TF1/TF2 lifting does not specify one finite global testing
basis. Chapter 03 explicitly allows root number/tensor ranks to depend on
K,f,q (lines 194--197); the final appeal to Corollary 3.19 is the step that
asserts a finite representation. This is an existence-to-extraction gap,
not an objection to the stated pointwise tensor-SOS argument.

The exact bottleneck is 03_tensor.tex, TF3--TF4 (lines 49--74). For a
particular bounded tensor it selects a pure-factor decomposition, real
reserve allocations epsilon_j and real M_j>|y_j|. Its testing factors are
sqrt(M_j+y_j), sqrt(M_j-y_j), or, when roles are interchanged,
sqrt(epsilon_j/M_j+y_j), sqrt(epsilon_j/M_j-y_j). Neither the y_j library
nor these gate parameters are fixed across the source/testing types. TF2
then invokes this operation for every scalar coefficient of TF6. Finitely
many displayed local matrices therefore do not themselves supply finitely
many testing functions after this last step. Allowing M to move inside
sqrt(M+t) generally gives an infinite-dimensional function family; this
observation is only about literal extraction from this recipe, not a
no-go theorem for a different fixed-family certificate.

Several apparent parameters are NOT intrinsic span obstructions. The real
whitening C and TF6 delta only mix/weight an existing frame and may be
absorbed into source Gram coefficients. Source radicals, real event caps
and finite penalty constants likewise remain source coefficients when
whole event squares are retained. For example, c2_short.tex OA7--OA9
(lines 5215--5278) chooses a real allocation using u_0 and rho_0, but its
varying real matrix entries do not require new testing functions. Pure
testing normalization in TF8 is also harmless once its factors belong to
a fixed finite chart library: add their inverse square roots to that
library and retain separate literal-zero charts. An arbitrary TF8 unit
has not, however, been assigned such a common library merely by naming it.

The conductor calculation in 06_mechanisms.tex CC1--CC6 is favorable:
monic polynomial division and separated monomial radicals preserve a finite
testing support. It introduces no moving testing branch point. The loss
of literal extraction is visible when the C1 bounded-z proof finishes its
displayed twelve-root frame by the unspecialized bounded-Gram fact
(c1_short.tex lines 7672--7687), rather than fixed numerical gates.

One concrete sufficient uniformization lemma would close this gap. On each
of finitely many source-independent algebraic testing covers, put every
local frame in K_c-span(H), for one finite testing list H, and prove an
identity valid throughout the chart
 Q=Q0(c) tensor 1 + sum_nu A_nu(c) tensor p_nu(t),
where the p_nu form one fixed finite library with |p_nu|<=1. Find source-only
alpha_nu with alpha_nu I +/- A_nu PSD and Q0-sum alpha_nu I PSD. Then exactly
 Q=(Q0-sum alpha_nu I) tensor 1
   +sum_nu [(alpha_nu I+A_nu)/2 tensor (1+p_nu)
            +(alpha_nu I-A_nu)/2 tensor (1-p_nu)].
TF9 now gives the fixed testing span
 H + sum_nu sqrt(1+p_nu)H + sum_nu sqrt(1-p_nu)H,
plus finitely listed retained squares and fixed normalization factors.
No mixed inverse or point-dependent gate remains. The missing requirement
is uniform chart identities/admission for these fixed lists, rather than
strict residue at each individual pair of types. TF1/TF2 alone does not
prove this requirement.

Chapter 07 (lines 78--96) supplies all-state local assembly and nine-atom
bounds, not that uniformization. Once an atomic fixed family is obtained,
TF10 is finite bookkeeping: the fixed nine-atom testing cover introduces
only sqrt(w_i)*h(atom_i); source conic coefficients remain pure source.
Finite angle/state sign charts and the field flip likewise cause no new
infinite family. The full source/testing symmetries must transport the
testing functions and normalizations as well as the source coefficients:
a Bob-only fractional family need not remain the same span after reciprocal
Alice--Bob exchange. Once a fixed family exists, a finite symmetry orbit can
be accommodated by a finite union of transported families. If their targets
use different fixed nonnegative pure-testing multipliers, a common product
multiplier gives a common target by multiplying each root by the square
roots of the other factors, provided that product is positive on a dense
testing set. This does not prove closure of the particular 40-function span
and does not remove the unfixed TF4 gates.
Thus the actionable extraction task is to replace the
generic strict-residue gates by one finite fixed-chart gate library and
source-only Gram allocation, then propagate it through the existing
nine-atom assembly. This audit does not address case coverage or any
ramified completion cone. Originals were preserved; no numeric work.

Independent exact check of the moving-threshold issue. For any distinct
positive constants M_1,...,M_n, the functions sqrt(M_i+t) are linearly
independent on every interval about zero on which they are defined.
If sum a_i sqrt(M_i+t)=0, take derivatives k=0,...,n-1 at zero and
divide by the nonzero falling-factorial coefficient (1/2)_k.
The resulting equations are sum (a_i sqrt(M_i))(1/M_i)^k=0.
Their coefficient matrix is Vandermonde with distinct nodes 1/M_i,
so every a_i vanishes. A symbolic gate with a freely varying threshold
therefore cannot be counted as one fixed testing function.
