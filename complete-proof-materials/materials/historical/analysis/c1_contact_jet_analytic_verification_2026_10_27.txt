1. Zero geometry and the rank-six contact anchor

For strict C1, the positive SG6 terms include exponent directions in both
angular coordinates. At fixed V=v_*^2>0 the Laurent polynomial has a
strictly convex dependence on log U, with a stationary zero at U=k^2.
There is therefore no other positive U zero. The Alice sign congruence
gives precisely the other sign u=-k. SG3's PD three-state block makes the
state kernel one-dimensional at either finite nonzero angle. The u=0
and Alice-infinity endpoint matrices are strictly positive for v_*>0
under the stated positive weights. Hence the two projective zeros used
in section3 are indeed the only ones; this is not inferred from Hessian
positivity alone or from a single sampled contact.

Write z=(w,y), h=k, x+=(z,hz), x-=(w,-y;-hw,hy).
Their Alice factors(1,h),(1,-h) and state factors z,(w,-y) are independent
because h>0 and w,y are both nonzero. Consequently the span N of x+,x-
contains no further rank-one Segre point. The source-rational projector
Pi_R onto Nperp thus has exactly these two zeros on RP1 x RP3.

SG18--SG19 give a strictly positive Hessian in(W,X,Y,u,v) with Z=1.
Restriction to fixed v and passage to local projective coordinates give
a positive quadratic lower bound for Dv_*F near each zero. Q_ref is
smooth, nonnegative and vanishes there, so Q_ref=O(distance^2). Away
from the two zeros the normalized Segre variety is compact and F>0.
These facts rigorously give a sufficiently small source-dependent real
epsilon>0 with
  Dv_*F-epsilon Q_ref>=0
globally in the homogeneous Alice pair and all states. Compactness is
used at one real source, not as a compact cover of the native domain.

With Calderon's2-by-n nonnegative-biquadratic SOS theorem explicitly
imported, the remainder has a PSD bilinear Gram annihilating x+,x-.
Adding epsilon Pi_R gives kernel exactly N, rank6, and positivity on its
six-dimensional complement. Alice-sign averaging preserves this strict
complement and produces the required4+4 parity blocks, each with a
one-dimensional kernel. Thus the new nondegenerate anchor is stronger
than the predecessor's isolated possibly degenerate PSD anchor.

2. Exact source-only contact skew

The coefficient identity fixes the diagonal state subblocks and the
symmetric cross part, leaving only the cross-parity skew T. The contact
equations reduce to
  T y=p, T^T w=q, w^T p=y^T q.
The compatibility follows from the actual state and Alice stationarity;
it is not an identity for arbitrary independent event weights.
The printed affine line
  T=p y^T/||y||^2+w q^T/||w||^2
    -(w^T p)w y^T/(||w||^2||y||^2)+tau w_perp y_perp^T
is the complete solution. Its denominators are strictly positive source
norms. Rank-six existence makes the simultaneous strict complement-PD
inequalities a nonempty open one-variable semialgebraic set. Finite
root isolation/selection gives a source-algebraic tau; this is not a
globally rational closed formula or a numerical SDP conclusion.

3. Derivative freedom and two-sided PSD

At x=x+, the physical tangent space
  E={(dotpsi,h dotpsi+dotu z)}
has dimension5 and E intersect N=R x. Thus
  L=Eperp intersect Nperp=(E+N)perp
has dimension2. The coefficient-neutral correction satisfies
  J(U)x=(h S(U)z,-S(U)z),
and its image is ALL Eperp, of dimension3: the cross-skew map has only
the one-dimensional kernel R w_perp y_perp^T. Both source subvectors
are nonzero throughout the stated domain, so there is no rank loss.

U0=w y^T/(||w||^2||y||^2) changes the two raw soft derivatives by
+2h and-2h. Bob stationarity makes their sum zero, hence one source-only
coefficient kills both. The remaining independent directions
U1=w_perp y^T and U2=w y_perp^T give a basis l1,l2 of L. Their Gram
  G_ij=l_i^T Msharp l_j
is PD because Msharp is PD on Nperp. The correction -G^(-1)b makes
Msharp b_opt orthogonal to L, hence in E+N, and therefore b_opt=M e
for some e in E. This proves that the full-range Schur minimization
agrees with the physically permitted Segre-tangent minimization; it
does not minimize over spurious independent state normals.

The quadratic Taylor coefficient along an actual Segre path is
  e^T M e+2 e^T b_opt+x^T H2 x.
Terms involving the second derivative of the Segre embedding vanish
because Mx=0. The full strict native Hessian, after minimizing the
other four projective state/Alice variables for a fixed unit Bob
displacement, gives
  kappa=x^T H2 x-b_opt^T Msharp b_opt>0.
Radial state scaling contributes only the already removed x null line.

Parity eliminates cross terms between the two soft modes, so their
second-order coefficients sum to kappa. Adding delta U0 to the quadratic
skew coefficient changes them by+2h delta,-2h delta. The printed delta
makes both equal kappa/2>0. Complementary3x3 blocks stay PD. Their exact
Schur expansions are delta_v^2(kappa/2+O(delta_v)), proving positivity
on BOTH sides of v_* for sufficiently small nonzero delta_v. This is
an actual singular-matrix Schur proof; continuity of the contact Gram
alone would not establish it.
