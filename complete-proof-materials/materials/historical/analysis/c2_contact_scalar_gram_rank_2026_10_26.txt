it is sum_e q_e exp(e dot (x,y)) e e^T. The actual contact gives a
stationary zero, so it is the unique zero in squared-angle space.
This remains true at n=0, D=0 or beta=0, since neither displayed
positive coefficient has these quantities as a factor; the t_i remain
strictly positive from d>0.

For uv!=0, if gamma vanishes then positivity makes psi_c a kernel
vector of P(u,v). The determinant must vanish, so

gamma. At compatible angles the next argument also excludes zeros.

2. The angular Hessian is strictly positive.

The positive log-Hessian of L makes Hess(det P) positive definite at
the contact: the prefactor is positive and L and its first derivatives
vanish there. The coordinate map from (u,v) to (log u^2,log v^2)
has invertible derivative at the contact.
Let H3 be the positive WXY principal block, with off-column beta3.
For the FIXED contact state psi_c=(xi_c,1), near contact one has the

the leading two-by-two block of G_gamma is positive definite. This
pointwise identity is used ONLY to prove a source-only rank statement;
it is not division inside the source/testing tensor ring.

4. Every possible Gram kernel is excluded.

Suppose G_gamma w=0 for a nonzero real w=(x,y,zeta).
If xyzeta!=0, (s,t',s t') is proportional to w at
s=zeta/y,t'=zeta/x. This gives a SECOND finite zero of gamma,
contradicting part 1.
If zeta=0, the strict two-by-two block from part 2 excludes w.
The remaining cases have zeta!=0 and x=0 or y=0. If x=0,y!=0,
put s=zeta/y and let t' tend to infinity. Then

contradicting strict positivity at the v-projective endpoint. The
case y=0,x!=0 is the corresponding u-endpoint. If x=y=0, the
u,v joint-projective leading coefficient is F0=0, again contradicting
part 3. Thus ker G_gamma={0}, proving rank three.
