CLOSEDNESS OF THE HYBRID CERTIFICATE CONE

This is an independent elementary refinement of the closed-sum lemma posted
by web task7 while that run was still active. It proves closure and compact
normalization, not admission of the remaining native contacts.

For s=(A0,A1,B0,B1) in {+1,-1}^4, write
chi(s)=(A0,A1,B0,B1,A0B0,A0B1,A1B0,A1B1).
Let f(q)=t+b.q be nonnegative at these16 deterministic behaviors.
Averaging over the16 assignments gives
        t=E_s f(chi(s)),     b_i=E_s chi_i(s) f(chi(s)).
Indeed, the eight displayed nonconstant monomials and the constant1 are
orthonormal under the uniform sign measure: a product of two distinct
monomials contains an independent sign to an odd power.

Since f(chi(s))>=0 and |chi_i(s)|=1, it follows that
        t>=0,              |b_i|<=t, i=1,...,8.             (1)
Thus t=0 forces f=0, and t=1 defines a compact section of every closed cone
of such slacks. No canonical nonnegative source coordinates are required.

Let K1,...,Kr be finitely many closed cones consisting of slacks nonnegative
at these deterministic behaviors. Suppose sum_i f_ij converges.
The constants obey 0<=t_ij<=sum_i t_ij, and (1) bounds all other coefficients.
A common subsequence of the finitely many summands converges, with limits
in Ki. Their sum is the required limit. Hence K1+...+Kr is closed.
For quantum certificate cones global soundness implies the required
deterministic positivity. Closure alone does not establish global soundness.

The fixed NPA SOS cone itself has a particularly short closedness proof.
Choose distinct reduced words w1,...,wN in the universal group generated
by the four observable involutions, commuting between parties. The
identity coefficient in wi^* wj is delta_ij. Therefore the identity
coefficient of W^* G W is trG, for real symmetric or complex Hermitian G.
Exact matching to an affine Bell slack tI+sum_i b_i chi_i, with all other
word coefficients zero, gives
                         trG=t.                          (2)
If a sequence of NPA-certified slacks converges, (2) bounds the PSD Grams
in this one fixed finite-dimensional space. A convergent subsequence
preserves PSD and every coefficient equation, proving the cone is closed.
Redundant or algebraically identical word entries must first be removed;
the argument uses universal coefficient matching, not evaluation at a
single state or at one qubit strategy.

Consequently, for a finite hybrid of this fixed NPA cone and other closed,
globally sound cones, no closure is needed around their sum. Coverage of
every extreme ray of the full valid-slack cone is equivalent to equality
with it: its t=1 section is compact by (1), and finite-dimensional convex
geometry generates that section from its extreme points. In this assertion
each added cone must be a subcone of the same full valid-slack cone.
Canonical extreme-ray tests require their separate classification and
cannot silently be replaced by full-polar extreme-ray tests.

If f is an extreme ray and is a sum of sound summands in the same cone,
every nonzero summand is proportional to f, so some summand cone contains
f. This direct-coverage condition remains necessary. Once coverage is
proved, the polar of the sum is the intersection of the duals; joining
their already attained finite PSD representations yields the global lift.
This argument does not prove any of the missing coverage or dual-attainment
hypotheses for a newly proposed block.
