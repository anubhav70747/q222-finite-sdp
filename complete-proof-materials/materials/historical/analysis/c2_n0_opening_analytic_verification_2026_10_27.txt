VERDICT

The conditional neighborhood theorem is valid as stated. Its quantifiers
are essential: for EVERY genuine n=0 wall point having a Gram positive
on ALL FOUR larger wall contact complements, the supplied certificate
has a source-only continuation admitting every sufficiently nearby
actual native source on the n>=0 side. It is not a theorem that every
wall point has the required strict-complement Gram, nor that every
strict C2 source is in this neighborhood union. The smaller18-feature
wall certificates alone do not meet its hypothesis.

1. Native domain, normalization and strict-side existence

The complete SG2 parent and native weight formulas match the established
C2 d=1,lambda=beta=0 convention and EN14--EN16 contact. A wall point
requires0<r<1, a,b,c,t>0, h,g,j,k=1-c^2h,B=b-a^2c^2r>0,
Delta,N_*>0, N_n=0 and all five retained weights positive. For nearby
strict sources replace N_n=0 by N_n>0. These restrictions cannot be
dropped merely because a point has formal positive parameters.

The substitutions u=cU, v=acV and
  psi=diag(b/a,-cr,ch/a,1)Phi
are invertible throughout that domain. The negative X scale is an
ordinary invertible source congruence and causes no PSD problem. Every
normalized feature takes value1 at contact; half-power angular factors
change only source coefficients by positive source radicals. There is
no testing denominator or mixed-field inverse.

The actual native full state/angular stationarity gives all six
normalized first derivatives zero by the chain rule. Four state
derivatives also imply the value condition by degree-two state Euler
homogeneity, so no seventh independent contact condition is missing.

The strict side is locally nonempty at every wall point in the stated
domain, not just at the delivered example. Delta is independent of t,
and
  partial n/partial t = a^2 c^2(b+r)(1+c^2r)/(2r Delta)>0.
Increasing t sufficiently little opens n>0 while preserving the other
strict gaps and retained weights. Native stationarity and the existing
stationary-source physicality theorem apply to this branch.

2. The universal coefficient right inverse is genuinely fixed

The four difference columns have lengths12,4,4,3. Their independent
symmetric Gram coordinates number78+10+10+6=104. Because each block
has a fixed parity, their products are polynomials with the specified
72 integer-exponent quadratic-state keys. The product map correctly
uses factor2 for off-diagonal independent Gram coordinates.

I independently reconstructed M and J from the serialized feature
blocks and upper-entry positions, rather than relying on a native
parameter specialization. Exact arithmetic confirmed:
  M is72x104; its selected66x66 minor has determinant2^54;
  J is6x72; its selected6x6 minor has determinant2;
  JM=0;
  M[:,pc] minor_inverse M[pr,:]=M;
  every selected column is in the first78 integer-block coordinates;
  the maximum absolute row sum of the inverse is141.
Thus rankM=66, rankJ=6 and imM=kerJ hold everywhere: no rational-function
generic-rank exception remains. The constructed E is a right inverse
on this kernel, with corrections confined to the integer block. This
is stronger than the earlier generic18-feature wall reduction.

3. Carrier and full residual identity

The normalized carrier is the correct source-rescaled version of the
prior two-square opening:
  C_hat=4n c^4r^2[(-UX+qVX+(1-q)VW)^2
                 +2qUV(W-X)^2], q=a/(1+a).
Its roots vanish at the normalized contact, hence every carrier first
state/angular derivative vanishes. It removes the entire forced
U^2X^2 coefficient and cancels its UVX^2 coefficient internally.
The formal residual support check retains all four state components
and all mixed terms, including the negative WX transfers; it does not
infer positivity from individual coefficient signs.

The carrier is an ambient SOS on the positive canonical testing chart,
not a separate Bell source. Here that distinction is explicitly stated
and harmless: only the combined full-parent identity is used for source
admission. For every actual stationary source Jr_theta=0, hence
  x(theta)=x0+E(r_theta-r0)
matches EVERY residual coefficient. The last three normalized matrices
are exactly unchanged. No positivity or Gram dominance assumption is
used to establish this coefficient identity.

4. PSD estimate and local quantifiers

For a supplied wall certificate Q_b^0>0,
  mu_b=1/||(Q_b^0)^(-1)||_infinity
is a valid positive lower eigenvalue bound: the inverse is symmetric,
and its spectral radius is at most its maximum absolute row sum.
An upper-coordinate correction is bounded by C_b epsilon entrywise;
after symmetric unpacking its operator norm is at most d_b C_b epsilon.
There is no additional missing off-diagonal factor in this bound.

Since C=(141,0,0,0),
  ||r_theta-r0||_infinity < mu0/(2*12*141)
implies Q0(theta)>=mu0 I/2, while Q1,Q2,Q3 remain PD. Native weights,
normalization and q are continuous at the wall point because all their
denominators stay nonzero. The coefficient-radius condition therefore
defines a genuine relative source neighborhood. Its radius depends on
the wall point and its chosen Gram; it is not a common Euclidean native
parameter radius or merely a condition that n is small.

The wall assumption is exactly strict positivity on each whole larger
contact complement. A generic singular PSD wall Gram or a Gram on the
smaller18-row support cannot replace it. For an arbitrary supplied
strict wall Gram, the continuation is rational in the moving source
data relative to that fixed certificate; the theorem does not supply
a universal rational selection of the initial wall Gram.

5. Feature counts and conclusion

All residual roots remain in the27 larger wall features. The carrier
adds only UX, so28 fixed half-power features suffice before the Dv
multiplier. I independently checked their union with their V-shifted
copies has49 distinct features. Their Alice exponents are<=1 and Bob
exponents<=2, in half-integer steps, hence all lie in the original
364-row sixth-root grid. Undoing contact normalization changes only
source coefficients. The physical multiplier uses v=acV, so its shifted
roots acquire only the source factor ac, with no dictionary change.

The combined identity therefore gives actual K364 membership on each
neighborhood. SG2 sign congruences and homogeneous continuation apply
to the complete parent, not necessarily to each carrier separately.
The advertised single fixed28-feature cone for the union of these
neighborhoods is consistent with these source-only rescalings.

The delivered rational seed is a finite instance of the hypothesis and
continuation; it is not the basis for the universal conditional step.
I inspected its stated native and radius checks but did not rerun that
seed search/LDL replay in this analytical pass. The source-independent
map/minor/right-inverse/count checks above were independently executed.

L19 establishes a legitimate conditional wall-to-strict opening, with
an actual fixed surjectivity map and norm bound. It does not reverse
L15 without the additional strict wall-Gram hypothesis. Whole-wall
strict-complement PSD selection, coverage beyond these neighborhoods,
arbitrary strict H2, and H1 remain open. No claim of unconditional
exactness of the global pencil follows.
