1. Physical set and transferred formulas
---------------------------------------

to projections, with the initial state in the common input subspace. Jordan's
two-projection decomposition then gives blocks of dimension at most two. The
block-diagonal observables see only diagonal state blocks, so a correlation is
a convex combination of two-qubit correlations. In each block local basis
choices put both observables in a real plane. A complex vector r+is has the
same expectations of real symmetric Bell matrices as the sum of the real
vector contributions r and s, with their squared norms as weights. Spectral
decomposition handles mixed states. Degenerate one-dimensional blocks are
included by two-dimensional extensions and deterministic state support.

The polynomial map Phi on S1 x S1 x S3 therefore generates exactly Q222.
Caratheodory in eight real coordinates gives at most nine atoms. Conversely a
finite direct sum realizes each finite convex combination. Compactness of the
parameter space and the nine-atom simplex gives compactness of Q222, and the
fixed finite polynomial formula gives semialgebraicity. This formula, rather
than an assertion about arbitrary Hilbert spaces over a field, defines Q_K.
Validity of an affine function, extremality of a finite-dimensional source
base, and nine-term convex decomposition are fixed first-order formulas.
Their transfer to any real closed extension is legitimate.
