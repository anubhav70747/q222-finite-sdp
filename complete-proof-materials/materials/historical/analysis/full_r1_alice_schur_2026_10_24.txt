EXACT TWO-NORMAL SCHUR ELIMINATION ON THE REMAINING R1 STRIP

Outcome. The exact elimination is valid, and its normal determinant has
a positive polynomial decomposition on the whole native source box.
However, separating its positive Schur-square component is incompatible
with any one finite source-independent testing family. This obstruction
already occurs on v=0 at actual positive native sources and on the
outer Alice interval. It rules out literal Schur-square separation as
the finite-certificate construction; it does not rule out a different
full-parent Gram. The remaining R1 strip is NOT certified here.

Inputs read: published uniform-contact parent, native dictionary and
source bounds; analysis/full_r1_annulus_independent.txt. No old party
swap, numerical source sweep, SDP or public edit is used.

1. Exact entire-parent elimination.

Keep the native source box B,T,x=jB<=delta=2^-10,
0<p<min(j,1),0<H<1,n>=0, d=1,lambda=beta=0. All source
coefficients below are the actual dictionary values. Put Du=1+u^2,
Dv=1+v^2 and use the existing rotated coordinates. Change only the
state coordinates by

 t=N+p v w,               r=-M-Hv zeta,
 W=w-u r, X=t-u zeta, Y=r+u w, Z=zeta+u t.

The exact quarter-parent numerator T=Dv*S_c is

 n Dv X^2+m Du(vw-t)^2+C Du Dv r^2+A Dv Y^2
 +D Du(r+v zeta)^2+alpha(vY-Z)^2
 +[Du(r+v zeta)^2+(vY-Z)^2+Du Dv t^2-Dv Z^2]/2.

Let q=alpha+1/2. Direct coefficient extraction gives

 T = (N,M)H(N,M)^t+2 LN*N+2 LM*M+F,
 F = T evaluated at N=M=0,

 H=[[q u^2+RN, q u v],[q u v,q v^2+RM]],
 RN=m Du+(n+1/2)Dv,
 RM=Du(C Dv+D+1/2)+A Dv.

The complete linear coefficients are

 LN=vw[n p Dv-m(1-p)Du-q u^2(1-p)+(p/2)Dv]
       +u zeta[alpha(1+Hv^2)-n Dv+(H-1)v^2/2],

 LM=v zeta{Du[H C Dv-(1-H)(D+1/2)]
                    +H A Dv+q(1+Hv^2)}
       -u w[A Dv+q(1-p)v^2].

No scalar restriction or event square is removed in this formula.
In particular

 Delta=det H=RN*RM+q u^2 RM+q v^2 RN>0.             (1)

This is a strict positive polynomial decomposition even at n=0;
RN>=Dv/2 and RM>=Du/2. For ell=(LN,LM)^t and z=(N,M)^t,

 T=(z+H^-1 ell)^t H(z+H^-1 ell)+F-ell^t H^-1 ell, (2)

or, with no inverse,

 Delta*T=(Hz+ell)^t adj(H)(Hz+ell)
                  +Delta*F-ell^t adj(H)ell.        (3)

The final state form in(2) remains to be certified. Positivity of H
alone does not prove positivity of T on the strip.

For Alice infinity set a=u/sqrt(Du),b=1/sqrt(Du). Rescale N=b*Nbar,
M=b*Mbar before taking b=0. The continuous normal block is b^2 H:

 [[q a^2+m+(n+1/2)Dv b^2, q a b v],
  [q a b v, C Dv+D+1/2+(A Dv+q v^2)b^2]].

At b=0 it is diag(q+m,C Dv+D+1/2), strictly positive. Thus normal
elimination has no singularity at the projective Alice endpoint.
This does not by itself settle the complementary state form there.

2. A precise obstruction to using the separated Schur square.

At v=0 the exact parent is the direct sum

 (AN+BN u^2)N^2+2(alpha-n)u*N*zeta+(n u^2+alpha)zeta^2
 +(AM+BM u^2)M^2-2A u*M*w+A u^2 w^2,

 AN=n+m+1/2, BN=m+alpha+1/2,
 AM=C+D+A+1/2, BM=C+D+1/2.

The N-completed-square component of(2) therefore contains, on the
source-independent test-state substitution W=Y=0,X=-u,Z=1,

             g_c(s)=(n-alpha)^2*s/(AN+BN*s),
             s=u^2>=(9/256)^2.                   (4)

The M square vanishes at this substitution. The state substitution is
independent of all native parameters: at v=0 it is exactly N=M=w=0,
zeta=1, despite the source-dependent normal coordinates used off v=0.

Use actual native sources j=1,H=1/2,B=T=2^-20, with
p in[1/4,1/2]. Their physicality numerator is uniformly positive:
expanding NN-p and taking its absolute coefficient majorant on this
interval gives a number less than8*2^-20, so NN>p-8*2^-20>0.
All other native signs and denominators follow from the published
source box. The ratio r_c=AN/BN is a nonconstant rational function
of p. Two exact values are

 p=1/4:
 r=6447599759597134890401793/5641653824874866551728812,

 p=1/2:
 r=4835700972619905530593281/3626777458848285570629633.

They are unequal. The factor n-alpha is nonzero at both points and
hence is not the zero rational function. Discarding its finitely many
possible zeros leaves infinitely many distinct positive ratios r.

Consequently the family(4) has infinite-dimensional real linear span,
even when restricted to any open real interval s>(9/256)^2. Indeed
choose any number of distinct ratios r_i. After nonzero source-only
scaling, the functions are s/(s+r_i). A linear relation on that real
interval is a rational identity; multiplying by product_i(s+r_i)
and setting s=-r_i forces each coefficient to vanish.

If the separated positive component in(2) had one finite fixed testing
family f_1,...,f_k with source-only Gram coefficients, its restriction
to the above source-independent states would lie in the finite span
of the k(k+1)/2 products f_i f_j. This contradicts the infinite-span
result. The obstruction allows arbitrary source-only Gram dependence,
not merely affine dependence, and arbitrary fixed testing functions.
It is therefore not cured by a different basis for the same separated
Schur component, by changing a pointwise Cholesky factor, or by adding
finitely many fixed functions to that component.

3. Scope and the needed next step.

There is no obstruction to the full parent at v=0. It has the explicit
polynomial SOS

 (C+D+1/2)Du*M^2+A(-M+uw)^2
 +(m+1/2)Du*N^2+n(N-u*zeta)^2+alpha(zeta+uN)^2.

Thus the rational terms introduced in the Schur split cancel between
its two positive pieces, while their sum already has a finite fixed
SOS. One cannot demand a finite fixed certificate for each separated
piece and infer that this certifies the full family.

On the outer strip, x/u^2<=64/81 and
u^2-x>=(17/81)u^2. This genuine contact separation does not remove
the moving poles in(4); they lie at negative s and persist on every
positive real test interval. A successful proof must retain the
cancellation before selecting fixed positive pieces, for example via
a source-only block completion or a polynomial Gram that reallocates
the retained squares. Equation(3) is only a multiplied identity;
Delta>0 is not a tensor-ring inverse or multiplier-removal theorem.

The exact helper analysis/full_r1_alice_schur_2026_10_24.py/.json
checks the entire-parent coefficients, positive determinant formula,
generic fraction-free identity, v=0 decomposition, physicality bound
and both rational ratio values. It does not claim strip admission.
The bounded attempt ends at this precise obstruction to literal Schur
separation; it is not a counterexample to another fixed full-parent
certificate, qualitative Q222 existence, or a global lift.
