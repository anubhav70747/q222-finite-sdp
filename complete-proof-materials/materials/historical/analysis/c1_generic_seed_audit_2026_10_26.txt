3. Precisely justified generic extension.

Choose five independent strict native contact coordinates
 p=(k,ell,S,r,theta),
 psi_c=(S,k(theta-1),kSr,1), (u_c,v_c)=(k,ell).
Here eta=ell/(kS), zeta=S ell/k, and NC1--NC4 give rational source
coefficients. The strict parameter domain imposes positivity of these
coordinates, 0<r,theta<1, all native gaps, and D,beta>0. It is an open
five-dimensional semialgebraic domain before the optional k<=1 orientation.

Let T(p) be the seven-root contact action and multiply it by
(1+k^2)(1+ell^2) to obtain a polynomial matrix T_hat(p). Set
 Delta(p)=det(T_hat[:,1:4]) and, on Delta!=0,
 N(p)=[-T_hat[:,1:4]^{-1} T_hat[:,5:7]; I3].
The same universal six-word prescription gives a rational matrix M(p),
whose entries have denominators dividing Delta^2. Hence
 H(p)=Delta(p)^12 det M(p)
is a polynomial. At the seed, Delta=216 and det M=7360/243, so neither
Delta nor H is the zero polynomial. Their common nonvanishing set is
open and dense in EVERY nonempty open component of the strict parameter
domain. At every such point the rank-four compression and invertible
six-word system prove nonmembership in 1+AB.

This rational-polynomial argument does not require an uncomputed
generic five-parameter determinant or an appeal to connectedness of an
analytic graph. If k<=1 is enforced, k<1 is the open interior; the seed
at k=1 has strictly positive other gaps and can be approached from
k<1. The same conclusion is relatively dense in the oriented chart.

Scope of “generic”: open dense in the strict five-dimensional native
CONTACT PARAMETER domain (and consequently dense in its continuous
source image), not an open subset of the ambient nine source coordinates.
No statement is established on the selected exceptional algebraic sets
Delta=0 or H=0, on literal D=0/beta=0 parameter faces, or on degenerating
contacts. Other pivots/equations might exclude such points, but were not
examined. A vanishing selected determinant is not an admission certificate.
