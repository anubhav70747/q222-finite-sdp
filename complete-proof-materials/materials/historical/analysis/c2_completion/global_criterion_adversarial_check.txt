Finding: no invalid final implication found, conditional on the actual
universal LC/source decomposition inputs established elsewhere.

1. The set in the corollary must be closed, convex and semialgebraic over
R. FA1's finite polynomial expectation model on nine copies of the compact
real S1 x S1 x S3, with simplex weights, has these properties and equals
the full finite-dimensional complex quantum set by simultaneous effect
dilation, Jordan block decomposition, real/imaginary splitting and direct
sum realization. Its real compactness gives closedness. One does not need
topological compactness over a non-Archimedean extension.

2. The corollary quantifies over EVERY real closed extension K of R,
EVERY affine f in the transferred valid-affine cone, and EVERY point of
the transferred set. FA2 identifies the cone by the same quantified
nonnegativity condition and elementary transfer. FA9 has the stronger
two-labelled-field assertion. Choosing two labelled copies of the same
arbitrary K gives precisely K tensor_R K. The factor flip is a ring
isomorphism preserving squares, so point-first/coefficient-first order
is harmless. An assertion only over R or one chosen extension would be
insufficient; that is not the assertion used here.

3. FA12 is exact for AFFINE evaluations. For f=sum a_j f_j, nonnegative
source weights need not sum to one. For q=sum w_i Phi(z_i), testing
weights DO sum to one, preserving f's constant term. Expanding bilinearly
then gives sum a_j tensor w_i times each elementary slack. Their square
roots are pure source/testing. Zero weights give zero roots and are never
inverted. Field-dependent finite choices are allowed by the universal
pointwise SOS criterion; no pre-evaluated common atlas or square count is
an additional hypothesis of Corollary 3.19.

4. FA11 divides only by positive s in Kc and positive Delta_A Delta_B
norm(psi)^2 in Kt. Projective angle pairs are nonzero, and a nonzero state
has positive squared norm. Their inverses and square roots therefore
exist in their separate real closed fields. Literal affine infinity is
handled by nonzero homogeneous pairs; zero state is evaluated separately.
There is no cancellation or square root of a mixed tensor element.

5. FA10 factors two PSD matrices inside their respective real closed
fields, allowing singular factors. The entrywise pairing expands to the
displayed squares; dual linear equations cancel auxiliary terms. This
step requires the explicit attained closed-face dual witnesses and the
strict-point/closed-image argument, checked by the lead; the final
assembly does not replace attainment by a formal nonnegative pairing.

6. Corollary 3.19 asks for finite SOS with no COMMON tensor-rank bound.
Its proof supplies a finite-dimensional algebraic space U from these
universal hypotheses and concludes finite semidefinite extension degree.
Consequently FA13's finite real affine pencil exists. Multiple PSD blocks
can be combined diagonally, and affine projection equations can be
retained as equalities or represented by opposite scalar blocks. This
does not compute N,m or any matrix entry and does not establish an SOC
lift or extension degree two. E1-E3 are later constructive tasks rather
than missing hypotheses for the qualitative implication.

Adversarial conclusion: the remaining possible failure point would have
to be an unproved local/source-generation/closed-face premise, not a
quantifier, convex-weight, pure-factor, ambient-dimension or extraction
mismatch at the Corollary 3.19 interface. No such interface mismatch was
found in this short check.
