FIXED NPA CONE AND THE NATIVE CONTACT SOURCES
25 October 2026. Bounded analytical result; no numerical membership tests.

1. Conventions and the fixed cone

Use S=A0 B0-A0 B1+A1 B0+A1 B1, H=I-S/2, and

  f_c=d H+4 sum_i a_i p_i,
  c=(d,lambda,n,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta).

The eight events, in that order after d, are
 (++|01),(+-|00),(+-|11),(-+|10),(-+|00),(-+|11),(--|01),(+-|10).
These are exactly latex/chapters/04_geometry.tex, SG1, lines16-29.
At its deterministic CHSH-facet neighbour v_i, f_c(v_i)=4 a_i.
At the chosen PR point, f_c(PR)=-d.

Fix w=(I,A0,A1,B0,B1,A0B0,A0B1,A1B0,A1B1). Let N be the
SOS cone f=sum_ij G_ij w_i^* w_j, G>=0, modulo Ax^2=By^2=I
and [Ax,By]=0. This is the dual SOS cone of the NPA 1+AB level.
Every coefficient identity, including vanishing higher-word coefficients,
is part of this definition. Real Bell coefficients permit real symmetric G.
The identity coefficient is tr(G), since the nine reduced group words are
distinct and w_i^* w_j=I only when i=j. Thus bounded target coefficients
bound PSD Gram preimages; this cone is closed. No Gram-limit gap is hidden.

An event projector P=(I+aAx)(I+bBy)/4 satisfies P^2=P=P^*, so its
probability slack has a root in span(w). Also

  2 sqrt(2) I-S = [(A0-(B0-B1)/sqrt(2))^2
                        +(A1-(B0+B1)/sqrt(2))^2]/sqrt(2).

The one-sided tilted slacks
  T_t=sqrt(8+2t^2) I-t A0-S, 0<=t<2,
and their physical relabellings have exact SOSs in this same fixed word
space: Bamps--Pironio, arXiv:1504.06960v1, Sec.II, especially II.2.3.
This imported family is not a classification of the full cone N.

2. Exactly what these familiar SOS families remove

At EVERY deterministic strategy v, t A0(v)+S(v)<=t+2. Consequently

  T_t(v)>=sqrt(8+2t^2)-(2+t)
          =(2-t)^2/[sqrt(8+2t^2)+2+t]>0  (0<=t<2).

The inequality remains strict after every physical relabelling. If valid
slacks sum to a slack tight at v, each nonzero summand must be tight there.
Therefore a native source cannot contain any positive multiple of a
subcritical tilted slack, including the t=0 CHSH slack, in an exact sound
decomposition. This argument holds in the full Bell polar, independently
of canonical coordinates or extremality.

Native C1 sources have lambda=n=0, and native C2 sources have
lambda=beta=0 (SG10, lines417-427). Both have d>0. Their deterministic
zeros are, in the ordering (A0,A1,B0,B1),

  v_lambda=( 1, 1, 1, 1),
  v_n     =( 1,-1,-1,-1),
  v_beta  =(-1, 1,-1, 1).

At t=2 the tilted slack 4I-2A0-S is nonsignalling-valid: check the sixteen
deterministic vertices and the eight PR vertices (A0=0, S<=4). It is
therefore a nonnegative event combination by SG1's vertex/Farkas argument.
Limits do not repair the exclusion. Normalize every generator by its
positive constant Bell coefficient. Events, finitely many relabellings,
and t in the compact interval [0,2] give a compact set in a fixed affine
slice, whose conic hull is closed. A native d>0 source cannot be solely
an event combination, since it is negative at PR. Hence NONE of the
native C1/C2 sources belongs to the cone generated by these familiar
event/CHSH/tilted families, even with closure. This does NOT say it lies
outside the full cone N; other nine-word SOSs are the relevant question.

3. Exact seven-word membership problem for each native family

The three deterministic evaluation vectors in the nine-word ordering are

  ell=(1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1,1),
  nu =(1, 1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1, 1,1),
  be =(1,-1, 1,-1, 1, 1,-1,-1,1).

If G>=0 and a native slack has zero expectation at these points, then
G ell=G nu=0 for C1, and G ell=G be=0 for C2. A fixed rational basis of
each corresponding seven-dimensional coefficient subspace gives the
following source-independent operator lists:

  h1=(A0-I, A1B0-I, A1B1-I, B0-A1, B1-A1,
                                      A0B0-A1, A0B1-A1),
  h2=(A1-I, B1-I, A0B0-I, A1B1-I, B0-A0,
                                      A0B1-A0, A1B0-A0).

Precisely, write h_sigma=U_sigma^T w. The columns of U1 are
 e1-e0,e7-e0,e8-e0,e3-e2,e4-e2,e5-e2,e6-e2;
the columns of U2 are
 e2-e0,e4-e0,e5-e0,e8-e0,e3-e1,e6-e1,e7-e1
(indices0,...,8). Their independence and the two vanishing evaluations
are immediate. They span the complete respective kernels. Hence

  f_c in N  iff  f_c=sum_ab R_ab h_sigma,a^* h_sigma,b
                 in the universal algebra, for some R>=0 in S^7.

This equivalence is exact on the indicated canonical coordinate face,
including all literal additional event-zero faces. It has no varying
testing functions, normalization poles, or source-dependent branch points.
Coefficient matching must be done in the universal algebra, not merely
on the native two-qubit contact or its determinant.

For reproducibility, the target's nine Bell coefficients are
  f0=d+lambda+n+m+C+A+D+alpha+beta;
  A0: lambda+n-A-alpha; A1: m-C-D+beta;
  B0: -n+C+A-beta; B1: lambda-m+D-alpha;
  E00: -d/2-n-A; E01: d/2+lambda+alpha;
  E10: -d/2-C-beta; E11: -d/2-m-D.
All other reduced universal-word coefficients are zero.

At an additional physical contact (A_c,B_c,psi_c), define T_c as the
4-by-9 matrix whose columns are w_i(A_c,B_c)psi_c. Tightness further
forces R (T_c U_sigma)^T=0. This is source-only contact algebra, not a
moving testing frame. Its supporting root dimension is 7-rank(T_c U_sigma),
and is at most3 IF rank4 is established. That rank assertion must be
checked on the chosen native domain; it is not assumed at zero faces.

4. The precise hard-source map and the hybrid obstruction

For an actual native source parameter domain D_sigma, define
 E_sigma={theta in D_sigma: the seven-by-seven coefficient system above
                              has a PSD solution R},
 H_sigma=D_sigma\E_sigma.
The finite event symmetries in SG8-SG10 reduce canonical coercive extreme
sources to C1/C2 and the stated third omissions. The parameter partition
E_sigma/H_sigma is exact and semialgebraic (using rational/algebraic native
formulas and positive denominator conditions). No explicit analytic
description of E_sigma, nor proof that it exhausts either family, follows
from the familiar tilted certificates. Producing such a description is the
first useful NPA source-map obligation. A finite cell decomposition exists
in principle, but is not an evaluated admission proof or an extracted lift.

For an extreme ray c of the canonical valid cone C_can, any finite sum
c=sum c_i with c_i in sound canonical cones contained in C_can has every
nonzero c_i collinear with c. If finitely many such summand cones are
closed and contained in R_+^9, their sum is closed: each summand of a
convergent sum is coordinatewise bounded, and subsequence limits give
the required decomposition. Thus summing unrelated easier canonical
inequalities cannot patch a missing extreme ray, even by taking closure.
This canonical extremality argument cannot simply be applied to arbitrary
full-polar summands, whose coordinates relative to one PR chart need not
be nonnegative. The tight-at-a-strategy argument in section2 applies
without that restriction and proves the familiar-family exclusion above.

Conclusion: keep the single fixed NPA 1+AB cone globally, retain the exact
closed-face cones SG12-SG14 separately, and ask which native C1/C2 sources
solve the explicit seven-word Gram problem. No native source can currently
be removed merely by citing the event/CHSH/one-sided-tilted SOS families.
Any remaining hard certificate cone must contain each unadmitted extreme
native ray itself, rather than relying on sums of unrelated easy sources.
