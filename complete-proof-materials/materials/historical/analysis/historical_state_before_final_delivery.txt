MATHEMATICAL ASSEMBLY

The recovered argument seeks the universal separated evaluation
  f0 tensor 1 + sum_(nu=1)^8 f_nu tensor q_nu in Sigma(Kc tensor_R Kt)^2
for every two labelled real closed extensions Kc,Kt, every valid affine support
f over Kc, and every quantum point q over Kt.

The reduction has four parts:
1. Represent Q222 by convex combinations of at most nine real two-qubit atoms;
   this also establishes compactness, convexity and semialgebraicity.
2. Decompose every source into event sources, two explicit closed faces, and
   physical canonical contact families C1/C2. SE proves the finite-contact
   reduction; EN proves entry into the actual native source coordinates.
3. Prove exact tensor SOS of each full homogeneous parent, including independent
   testing angles/states, coefficient boundaries, and positive infinitesimal
   openings. LC assembles its positive-total and weak-total case theorems.
4. Restore positive pure-field factors and finite convex/conic weights. Apply
   Scheiderer's Corollary 3.19 to two labelled copies of any one extension.

The primary criterion is in arXiv:2004.04196v2, printed page 15:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.04196
It applies to closed convex semialgebraic sets in arbitrary dimension. Despite
the title of that paper, this corollary is not restricted to planar sets.
It gives finite SDP existence without an evaluated pencil or a rank-two claim.
The final implication is correct conditional on the universal local certificates.
