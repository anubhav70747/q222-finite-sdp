Checked 26 October 2026 UTC.

Calderon, A note on biquadratic forms, Linear Algebra Appl. 7 (1973), 175-177:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0024379573900529
The indexed publisher abstract confirms the nonnegative 2-by-n biquadratic
sum-of-bilinear-squares statement. Direct publisher access returned403;
the full original three-page proof was not retrieved. Applying this theorem
still requires the genuine parent to be nonnegative for the complete
homogeneous Alice pair and every state at the fixed Bob contact.

Hanselka and Sinn, Positive Semidefinite Univariate Matrix Polynomials:
https://arxiv.org/html/1707.08261v1
Corollary2.8 states polynomial matrix SOS factorization for every real
symmetric univariate PSD matrix, including the identically singular case.
Its proof explicitly reduces the singular case to a nondegenerate block.
No nonzero-determinant hypothesis is needed for this cited corollary.

Independent degree argument for this application: each diagonal of
H(t^2)=Q(t)^T Q(t) is a sum of real polynomial squares of degree at most8.
Therefore every entry of Q has degree at most4. Averaging the factorizations
at t and -t removes odd cross terms. With v=t^2 this yields a matrix SOS
plus v times a matrix SOS, with row degrees2 and1 respectively. Multiplying
those rows by (psi,u psi) gives the stated fixed40-feature span.

These imports prove the contact-anchor existence and the conditional
half-line-to-Gram step only. Neither proves H(v) PSD for all v>=0,
universal C1 admission, or the global reverse inclusion.
