4. Why merely using u0=1/16 with these allocations fails.

This is a failure of the current payment, not an obstruction to any
other full-parent Gram. Take the actual sources j=1,H=1/2, first let
B=T tend to0 with p fixed in(0,1), then let p tend to1. The physical
numerator tends to p>0, so n>=0 holds for sufficiently small positive
B,T. The other coefficients remain positive by the native theorem.
In this sequential limit Me tends to3/4, theta tends to1, phi tends
to1, P/F tends to2, and the effective radial multiplier tends to16.
At v=0, the exact Psi loss consequently tends to

                 512s+(256/3)s^2.

At s=1/256 this exceeds2, whereas only3/4 of the Psi mass is
available. Therefore the same fixed16Ru purchase cannot certify
that larger cap by a nonnegative residual coefficient; sufficiently
nearby actual sources already violate this necessary residual sign.
This neither disproves Bell validity nor excludes reducing/rebalancing
the Ru allocation, combining residuals, or another fixed Gram.
