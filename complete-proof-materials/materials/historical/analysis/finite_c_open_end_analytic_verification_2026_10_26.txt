1. EXACT DOMAIN
The theorem fixes positive real a,t,eta,rho with rho<t,
0<t-rho<1, eta<1 and T=a^2t+eta>1, then uses
b=a^2t+eta epsilon, r=rho epsilon, epsilon=c^-2.
These are strict face interiors, not arbitrary source-dependent
infinitesimal face parameters. For sufficiently small epsilon the native
gaps h=eta epsilon, g=T-1+(eta+rho)epsilon,
j=1+rho-t, 1-c^2h=1-eta and
b-a^2c^2r=a^2(t-rho)+eta epsilon have the required signs.
The full rescaled parent is source-normalized without a state slice;
map_core.py:8-29 and proof:20-43 retain all four state coordinates.
Its C/epsilon coefficient is analytic because C starts at epsilon^2.

2. SMALL GAUGE AND ENDPOINT BLOCK
Proof:79-97 gives p2,q2>0: a^2t+2eta-1=(T-1)+eta>0 and
2rho+1-t=rho+[1-(t-rho)]>0. gamma_max is strictly positive.
For 0<gamma<gamma_max the listed last LDL condition is equivalent to

3. RANGE CONDITION AND SCHUR LIMIT
verify_finite_c.py:57-61 shows Q0 splits into the first-three and
last-three feature blocks. Its first-five principal block A0 has one
extra kernel x=(1,1,1,0,0). The actual tail coupling B is a multiple
of principal columns 3 and 0 of A0 (proof:118-124; checker:95-98).
Thus both columns are in ran A0; this is stronger than merely small B.
The two columns belong to complementary blocks, so Q0[0,3]=0 makes
their limiting cross cost zero. The principal-column quadratic costs
are exactly the indicated Q0[3,3] and Q0[0,0] entries.

The limit at proof:145-160 is justified. In range/kernel coordinates,
A_gamma=[A4+O(gamma), O(gamma); O(gamma), gamma d+O(gamma^2)]
with d>0. Its kernel Schur term is gamma d+O(gamma^2); the range
inverse tends to A4^-1 and the B vectors have zero kernel component.
Hence B^T A_gamma^-1 B -> B^T A0^+ B; no 1/gamma pole survives.
The supplied exact source reductions give the full residual matrix
diag(rho t(1+rho)/(2(t+1)), eta t(1+rho)/(2(t+1)))>0.
Consequently small positive gamma makes the full 2x2 tail Schur matrix
positive, not just its two original diagonal terms. The proposed
dyadic source-only search terminates by this positive limit.

4. FIRST-ORDER TREE AND ACTUAL TAIL COUPLINGS
All five tree weights at proof:103-110 are positive; the only less
immediate numerator T+rho-t=(T-1)+[1-(t-rho)] is positive.
The five edges form a connected tree, so its reduced Laplacian is
positive definite (its quadratic form vanishes only on constants,
removed by the chosen differences). Its determinant is the edge-weight
product. The first-order free solve leaves this charge block fixed,
fixes old/tail B, and forces charge/tail and tail/tail first orders zero.
Thus the 37-rank compatibility solve needs no positivity from its free
old/old or old/charge entries. Those entries are harmless at the later
graded Schur orders. Their exact compatibility is an algebraic checker
obligation rather than a consequence of tree positivity alone.

5. ALL-ORDERS RIGHT INVERSE AND FORCED TAIL
The coefficient map is constant rational on the fixed 12 differences
plus odd square (two_kernel.py). A fixed rational inverse on its image
maps the analytic rational Taylor remainder r2 to an analytic bounded
matrix; it cannot introduce a small epsilon denominator. An isolated
zero denominator in the Taylor quotient is removable by its exact
value/first derivative subtraction. The construction is exact for the
whole coefficient vector, not a truncated equality.

The last-state X and Y rows are X-UVY and Y-UVY. No other difference
root has constant-angular X or Y. Consequently their diagonal and mixed
Gram entries are uniquely the full parent coefficients of constant X^2,
Y^2,XY. This verifies the analytic force-of-coefficients reason for
proof:186-193; an arbitrary image right inverse cannot alter those
entries. The tail is epsilon^2 T0+O(epsilon^3), with exactly zero mixed
entry, while charge/tail begins at epsilon^2. Note that the literal code
ordering is old5,tail2,charge5; the proof's displayed block ordering is
the corresponding fixed permutation, not a different frame.

6. FULL GRADED SCHUR
For fixed admissible face parameters and a chosen small gamma,
the old block is uniformly positive for sufficiently small epsilon.
Eliminating it produces charge epsilon L+O(epsilon^2), charge/tail
O(epsilon^2), and tail epsilon^2 S_gamma+O(epsilon^3).
The charge inverse is O(epsilon^-1), so its full 2x2 tail correction
is O(epsilon^3). Since S_gamma is positive definite, the whole remaining
tail is positive definite, including its determinant. The odd scalar
has a positive constant term. Hence H(epsilon)>0 and g(epsilon)>0
for all sufficiently small positive epsilon; endpoint support continuity
alone was not substituted for this argument.

7. COMPACT UNIFORMITY
On a compact set strictly inside the face domain, all source denominators,
A4/kernel pivots, the explicit limiting S0 margins and gamma_max have
uniform bounds away from zero. The preceding range/kernel Schur formulas
give uniform convergence S_gamma->S0. Thus a single sufficiently small
positive gamma works throughout the compact set. All rational coefficient
maps and removable Taylor remainders are uniformly bounded there. The
three positive blocks and odd scalar then admit one sufficiently small
epsilon_K. Pointwise continuity alone would not justify the single gauge;
the fixed-rank block form and positive explicit margins supply it.

8. OTHER END AND FIXED GRID
The source inversion and entire-parent permutation are separate exact
identity checks. The limiting coordinate map is bijective on the strict
face interiors: from zero-end (a,b',r',t') recover
t=1/b', rho=r'/b', eta=1-a^2 t'/b'. Its strict inequalities are exactly
rho<t, t-rho<1, eta in (0,1), T>1. The reciprocal parent transform maps
sixth-root indices (i,j) to (6-j,6-i), preserving the fixed finite grid.
The present 16 feature set is also preserved up to source/state scaling
and permutation (UVX<->X, UVY<->Y, UVW<->Z, UW<->UZ, VW<->VZ).

To interpret 'whole open zero end', transformed face coordinates may vary
analytically with epsilon rather than stay exactly fixed. The proved
compact uniformity handles these paths in a small compact neighborhood
of each strict limiting face point. It does not cover approach to the
boundary of that open face while epsilon vanishes at an uncontrolled rate.
