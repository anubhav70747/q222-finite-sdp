1. VALID IMPLICATIONS AND WHY CONSTRUCTION IS A SEPARATE QUESTION

(a) Physical reduction. C:67--90 supplies a complete compact rational atom
model Q222 = conv Phi(Z): simultaneous binary-observable dilation, Jordan
blocks, realification, real pure-state decomposition and nine-atom
Caratheodory reduction. This proves compactness, convexity and rational
semialgebraicity. It does not imply semidefinite representability.

(b) Scheiderer's exact criterion. A:182--205 correctly retains all real
closed extensions and both independently varying arguments. Fresh primary
verification: C. Scheiderer, Second-order cone representation for convex sets
in the plane, arXiv:2004.04196v2, Theorem 3.10, printed pp. 12--15, and
Corollary 3.19, printed p. 15 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.04196).
The latter gives a finite lift from universal tensor SOS, without a prescribed
rank bound. Its proof obtains a finite function space; evaluated matrices or
a user-supplied finite atlas are not extra hypotheses. Proposition 3.5 only
handles strictly positive residue, leaving zero-residue contacts to prove.

(c) Global geometry. The star/polar route A:64--129, its corrected visible
facet argument B:R1, the normalized support cone A:134--178 and semialgebraic
extreme-point choice A:192--196 form a valid route to a smaller criterion.
In the support-cone proof, compactness of normalized E and e(x*)=1 make its
conic hull closed (with the finite-dimensional Caratheodory bound understood).
The asserted finite conic representation is first order and transfers.
Once the universal tensor premise for each star is actually established,
finite convex hull and polarity give the full quantum body, A:2205--2215.
These conclusions are not impeded by the absence of printed affine matrices.

(d) Coefficient descent. C:123--159 is valid conditionally on existence of
a fixed finite block pattern. Compress the auxiliary span to dimension at
most the ambient symmetric matrix space. Existence/equality of the lift is
then a rational first-order sentence. Real-closed-field transfer to the real
algebraic numbers, followed by elementarity back to R, gives algebraic
coefficients. Enumerating single-block orders with exact decisions terminates
under an independently proved finite-lift premise. This does not prove that
premise, and an unrestricted lift does not ensure an order-two search works.

(e) Fixed features from supplied local identities. D:359--440 proves the
finite-rectangle assembly and moment-pencil separation argument. The algebra
is sound under its explicitly listed hypotheses. For existence, neither
evaluating a basis nor printing product coefficient tables is essential;
choosing a finite basis suffices. Conversely, compactness is only useful once
the local identities really cover the required incidence pairs and retain
all clearing and coordinate maps. A cover of ordinary real points by residue
formulae is not automatically a cover of all infinitesimal lifts.

2. DECISIVE UNRESOLVED IMPLICATION: RESIDUE CHILD VERSUS EXACT CHILD

A:2083--2091 makes common-node incidence the bridge from arbitrary independent
arguments to the local modules. The proof's simultaneous-projective case
uses leading cofactor packets A:2101--2132. At A:2134 it derives coordinate
vanishing at the RESIDUE point. A:2134--2135 then invokes a proper common
coordinate child and repeats the coefficient filtration. A:2141--2159 proves
termination only for children imposing EXACT coefficient zeros or EXACT
proper stratum equations. A:2173--2186 invokes that termination to close the
universal tensor claim.

There is a missing bridge. Vanishing of a nonnegative product in the residue
field implies residue vanishing of the required factor, not exact vanishing
in R. For a positive infinitesimal epsilon, bar(epsilon)=0 but epsilon != 0.
An earlier positive packet annihilates a residue state; its actual amplitude
need not be zero. Deleting its coefficient, restricting its amplitude to
zero, or treating its residue face as an exact child changes the actual
tensor unless an exact reconstruction/decomposition is proved.

This distinction survives extremality. For example, on the compact convex
semialgebraic set K={(s,t): 0<=s<=1, s^2<=t<=1}, the point
(epsilon,epsilon^2) is extreme over a real closed extension. Both coordinates
have zero residue but are nonzero, and the exact tangent
t-2 epsilon s+epsilon^2 vanishes there. Thus exact supporting contact plus
extremality does not generally upgrade a residue face to an exact face.
This example refutes that general inference; it is not a Q222 counterexample.

The text presents Bell-specific leading cofactor identities, but supplies no
identity transporting the full tensor, including its nonzero infinitesimal
coefficients, to the asserted exact common child. If "child" means only a
residue stratum, the termination argument A:2143--2155 no longer proves a
strict decrease of the exact invariant. Normalizing to a later coefficient
layer is not the deletion of a nonzero original coefficient. Finiteness of
the set in A:2156--2158 only proves termination after each arrow has been
shown valid and strictly decreasing.

A further local occurrence is A:1925--1936: the reciprocal unit-or-child
firewall declares the z-, P-, or R-residue-zero cases earlier positive EVENT
packets, then uses positive residues in A:2042. Positivity/earlier order can
identify an initial face. It does not by itself furnish an exact SOS for the
remaining full tensor or a strictly smaller exact problem. This is the
unproved all-rates collar step, irrespective of the printed firewall label.

The August 31 overlay repairs visible facets, chart definitions, gauge

4. WHY DROPPING THE ORDER-TWO CLAIM DOES NOT CLOSE THE PROOF

An incorrect separation-rank estimate might cease to matter for plain SDP
existence if the same displayed roots were still finite elements of R tensor R.
That relaxation is legitimate. Here, however, the unproved steps concern
whether the actual universal tensor has any SOS certificate supplied by the
argument. Residue restriction discards data; a missing restoration map fails
to identify the tensor being certified. Allowing larger finite ranks does
neither operation for free.

The local diagonal implication, with genuine diagonal-square membership and
positive tangential quadratic class, is supported by Scheiderer's 2025
Proposition 3.11/Lemma 3.12 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.17121), and D:89--195
gives an explicit denominator-free local construction with these hypotheses.
It does not prove that all physical contacts satisfy those hypotheses.
In particular, a positive eliminated scalar Hessian does not itself supply
the full independently evaluated tensor identity. Uncontrolled mixed
inversion is also not a harmless enlargement of finite root rank: D:174--190
shows why 1/(1-xy) cannot have finite separation rank on an open rectangle.

R:284--285 explicitly limits its strictness result to the actual previous
pencils and leaves other enlarged constructions and arbitrary finite-lift
existence untouched. It is not an obstruction theorem for all SDP lifts.

5. SMALLEST SUFFICIENT MISSING LEMMA FOR THE EXISTENCE TARGET

An all-rates zero-contact lemma would suffice, without a block bound:

For every real closed R/R_real, every normalized physical extreme point
x of the canonical star, and every pure real strategy p whose slack has
zero multiplication residue, the ACTUAL tensor
  1 tensor 1 - sum_i x_i tensor Phi_i(p)
(up to the existing pure-side positive normalizations) is a finite sum of
squares in R tensor_{R_real} R.

It is enough to prove this locally at each real zero-incidence pair, allowing
arbitrary independent infinitesimal rates. A practical proof must retain
positive infinitesimal coefficients, give exact centering/restoration to the
full parent tensor, and use only identities living in the tensor product.
For every proposed child transition it must either:
  (i) prove an exact zero and a genuine decrease of the exact invariant; or
  (ii) exhibit an exact SOS decomposition retaining the transverse terms and
       reducing a stated residual problem under a proved well-founded measure.
The B0 case additionally needs its actual congruence, clearing and complete
parent identity. Root separation ranks and number of squares may be arbitrary
finite integers for this existence lemma.

Positive-residue Proposition 3.5 then handles all remaining pairs, and
Corollary 3.19 plus the valid global geometric reductions yields a finite
SDP lift. Its compactness/finite-function-space step supplies finiteness
nonconstructively. No evaluated pencil is needed to finish that theorem.

Audit boundary: this is a bounded source-and-implication audit, not a claim
that every displayed high-degree identity has been freshly expanded by hand.
The identified missing implications suffice to prevent validation of the
supplied global existence proof, independently of such expansions.
