1. C2 r->0 face: universal identities versus finite PSD

The limiting W,Y,Z form and its contact/value/first-derivative identities
are symbolic in c,a,b,t. Its physical face domain is
  Phi={c,a>0,0<t<1,h=b-a^2t>0,
       g0=(1+c^2)b-(1+a^2c^2t)>0,1-c^2h>0}.
The N0 decomposition and positive Delta0 coefficients justify positive
remaining limiting weights there. The elementary five-form decomposition
and two polynomial relations are identities before imposing native weights.

The integer-power no-go is genuinely universal on Phi: Newton pruning
and symmetry averaging isolate uY in a singleton character block; its
sole diagonal must equal4A>0, while the nonzero contact u_cY_c forces
that diagonal to vanish for a PSD Gram. This excludes an integer-power
polynomial SOS for this WYZ limiting form. It does not prove that a
half-power Gram exists everywhere on Phi or that every possible useful
library must contain exactly the displayed half-power rows.

Half-power PSD is certified at the9 stored rational face points only.
The reported210 numerical points are additional numerical evidence,
not a universal proof and not a sweep performed for this review.
'The limit needs exactly the half-power features' should therefore be
read as universal integer-power insufficiency plus half-power success
at the certified points, not universal half-power sufficiency.

This is a3-state limiting form. The original C2 n coefficient diverges
like n0/r, and the X contact amplitude tends to zero; n0 here is not
the literal n=0 wall. The new face certificates alone do not control
the full X normal or prove full-source admission for small positive r.
The separately proved strict open-end theorem retains its own scope.

domain is the subset with0<r<1, c,a,b>0, t_w>0 and all native positive

k=1-c^2h, are exact rational identities on this graph. Their denominators
are positive on that domain. The five retained weights are then positive.

The C1 beta wall graph and D wall graph are likewise rational identities

linear-zeta equation is interpreted on the actual D=0 branch with its

The coefficient matching identity with100 independent Gram entries,

five retained source weights, with no PSD claim. The18 rows comprise

Both are subspaces of fullZ6, so their certified PSD sources enter K364
by multiplying the Gram with Dv through the two standard embeddings.

The symbolic contact equations are18 linear equations in the28 free
Gram variables. Rank12=augmented rank12 OVER Q(c,a,b,r) proves rational
consistency and dimension16 GENERICALLY. It does not by itself prove
rank12 or consistency at every physical specialization: a pivot minor
or a rational denominator can vanish at an exceptional wall point.
The checker does not exhibit a globally nonvanishing rank12 minor or
an everywhere regular16-parameter contact parameterization. Accordingly
'whole wall is one explicit16-parameter family' should be qualified as
a generic rational-function family pending this domain control.

This distinction need not obstruct the constructive route: prove PSD
on a dense physical open set with the fixed library, then the established
coefficient-space closedness can cover exceptional physical limits.
It would establish whole-wall PSD without establishing constant family
dimension at every point. At present even the generic universal PSD
selection remains unproved.

The22 rational data18 points give exact PSD witnesses in this smaller

4. Consequences for the actual admission work

The n=0 C2 source has lambda=n=beta=0 and is the C1 beta=0 triple face
in the same canonical source coordinates. It is a boundary case, not
the strict six-event C2 family required by H2. L15 runs from universal
strict admission in a fixed closed cone to allowed third-omission
walls; finite wall admissions do not reverse that implication. Nor do
the new point checkers themselves supply a uniform neighborhood theorem
or a Schur estimate for opening the missing event.

Useful next constraints are therefore concrete:
  - use the exact graph substitutions and short positive wall weights;
  - retain the forced zero vZ/uvZ rows and all contact-kernel equations;
  - use the new18-row n=0 affine family as a boundary candidate, choosing
    its16 free coefficients by source-algebraic formulas with proved PSD,
    including exceptional parameter values or a closedness argument;
  - if opening into strict sources, prove the new-feature/normal Schur
    estimates over the actual domain rather than infer them from samples;
  - keep full H1/H2 as domain-wide sufficient Gram/SOS obligations in
    the fixed K364 cone. The r0 integer-power obstruction justifies
    retaining fractional freedom; it is not an obstruction to K364.

For a strict C2 construction, one further exact constraint follows
immediately: coeff(u^2 X^2)=4n, whose unique half-grid diagonal is uX.
The new18-row wall library omits uX; it must be restored when n>0,
with coupling to the other rows to enforce the moving contact kernel.
Simply adding the positive n-event squares to a PSD wall Gram cannot
work at a strict contact where X_c=-cr is nonzero. The universal
coefficient-family data are useful as a boundary condition for that
coupled full-K364 construction, not as a claimed globally sufficient
19-feature half-power ansatz.
