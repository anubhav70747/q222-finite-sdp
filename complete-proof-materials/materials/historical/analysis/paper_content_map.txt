Q222 paper: private content and dependency map, 22 October 2026

Status: completed proof review and manuscript; the complete abstract was accepted.
The original author's earlier instruction was to close the global proof first.

Principal claim
The full eight-coordinate finite-dimensional tensor-product quantum set for
two parties, two binary measurements each is a spectrahedral shadow. There
exist finite N,m and real symmetric matrices A0,A1,...,A8,B1,...,Bm such that
Q222={q: exists y, A0+sum q_i A_i+sum y_j B_j >=0}. The proof supplies
qualitative existence. It does not evaluate these matrices or N,m.

Consequences
One fixed finite lifted feasible set represents all marginals and correlators,
so every affine Bell objective has the same exact lifted optimization domain.
The matrices can be chosen with real algebraic entries: Q222 has a fixed
polynomial definition over Q; for the finite N,m supplied by existence, equality
with a projected pencil is a first-order statement over Q about finitely many
matrix coefficients. Transfer from R to the real algebraic numbers supplies
coefficients there. Transfer back to R proves equality for all real q.
This consequence does not evaluate a pencil or assert efficient extraction.

Reader progression
1. Introduce the actual Bell experiment and its eight expectations. Explain
   that a finite SDP representation means linear constraints and positivity
   of a fixed finite matrix after auxiliary variables have been introduced.
   State the full theorem with the original eight coordinates retained.
2. Reduce the physical set to a fixed polynomial two-qubit image and nine
   convex weights. This proves compactness/semialgebraicity and makes later
   field extensions precise. Define Bell slack f=bound-Bell functional.
3. Explain why ordinary pointwise nonnegativity is insufficient for the
   finite-representation question. State the tensor criterion, define its
   two copies of a real closed field and exact tensor evaluation. Give its
   quantifiers before using independent infinitesimal coefficients/tests.
4. Derive the common event-square parent and source reduction. Show the
   determinant's nonnegative outer coefficients and how logarithmic convexity
   supplies actual regular contacts. Reduce extreme sources to the two
   explicit omission families plus the two closed source faces.
5. Explain the local SOS mechanism by proving the positive-residue/strict-
   Gram lemma and the restricted monomial contact lift. At noncommuting
   residues, entanglement makes the vanishing events a matching; adding a
   normal constraint can happen at most four times. At commuting residues,
   retain full normal coefficients and resolve exact source-scale branches.
   Present the source partition and name the operative lemmas without replacing
   them by historical certificate labels. Full derivations must accompany it.
6. Restore pure-source and pure-testing factors, then finite source/testing
   convex combinations. This yields the universal tensor criterion and hence
   the global finite lift. Prove the algebraic-coefficient corollary.
7. Conclude with the precise structural consequence and the remaining concrete
   task of evaluating a useful pencil. Do not claim fixed-level NPA exactness,
   second-order-cone representability, an explicit size, or computational speed.

Notation
Q222: full quantum set; q: its eight-coordinate point.
f: valid affine Bell slack; F: its homogeneous evaluated polynomial.
Kc,Kt: separately labelled real closed fields over R; T=Kc tensor_R Kt.
u,v: testing half-angles; actual contact parameters carry c subscript.
W,X,Y,Z: homogeneous real state coordinates.
d: nonnegative signed-core coefficient, distinct from matrix/block dimension.
lambda,n,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta: nonnegative losing-event weights.
delta_tot: signed-core coefficient divided by total coefficient sum, kept
invariant under every normalization. Matrix dimension should be N, not d.
Literal zero, positive infinitesimal and positive bounded unit are distinct.

Preservation outside the active main theorem
The supplied extraction package's evaluated tilted-CHSH projection and general
conditional assembly are preserved in source/ and its source inventory.
They are not silently promoted to an evaluated lift of the full quantum set.
An explicit feature basis/pencil is a separate constructive output.
