Inversion
---------
The exact source involution is c'=1/(ac), a'=a, b'=a^2/b,
r'=a^2 c^2 r/b, t'=(1-c^2(b-a^2 t))/b. verify_inversion.py:18-29 proves
(n,m,C,A,D,alpha) -> (n,D,alpha,A,m,C) and the unrestricted full-parent identity

The Z6 grid is preserved since uv(1/v)^(i/6)(1/u)^(j/6)
=u^((6-j)/6)v^((6-i)/6), the index permutation (i,j)->(6-j,6-i).
At the transformed zero end, (b',r',t') tends to
(1/t,rho/t,(1-eta)/(a^2 t)). Its strict inequalities

are equivalent to the original face inequalities, with inverse
t=1/b', rho=r'/b', eta=1-a^2 t'/b'. Thus the checked inversion reaches the
corresponding open end without changing the fixed angular grid.
