NORMALIZED TARGETS CANNOT HAVE A FINITE POSITIVE-TEMPLATE COVER

Result. The proposed literal polyhedral route is impossible for ALL
contact-normalized native C1/C2 targets. Already a positive C2 subfamily
has conical limits whose state matrices, at the one normalized testing
pair s=t=1, run through infinitely many distinct rank-one PSD rays.
A finite conic hull of fixed PSD Gram templates can have only finitely
many such rays. This is NOT an obstruction to L6 or to a finite fixed
testing basis with variable PSD coefficient matrices.

Sources inspected: complete canonical parent in gram_lift.py26-30;
uniform-contact/source_dictionary_exact.tex (actual stationary R1
dictionary); the verified native contact/state formulas. Public source
snapshot at read31789f0d95de4ff1ab42c3d824150e45fd6e92dc.
No numerical SDP, sweep, large appendix replay or public edit.

1. An actual native C2 family.

Fix B=T=epsilon=2^-12,H=1/2, let j range over[2,3] and
let3/4<=p<1. Then B,T,x=jB<=delta=2^-10 and p<min(j,1).
The actual native physicality numerator NN is positive uniformly.
From the displayed polynomial dictionary, on this parameter box,

 |NN-p| <=23epsilon+22epsilon^2+50epsilon^3
                           +9epsilon^4(1+epsilon)<24epsilon<3/4.

Thus n=NN/[2(j-p)P]>0. All remaining native weights and denominators
are positive by the existing source bounds. In particular this is
not an independently selected positive event vector.

Set x=jB,y=TB,qz=jT. The contact is

 uc=sqrt(x),vc=sqrt(y),
 psic=(1+Hjy, -(j-p)sqrt(y),
                  (1-HT)sqrt(x),(1+pB)sqrt(qz)).

All four state coordinates are nonzero, including as p approaches1,
because j>=2. The complete stationary parent vanishes at this contact.
Normalize exactly as proposed:

 Fhat_jp(s,t,eta)=F_c(uc*s,vc*t,diag(psic)*eta).

The new contact is s=t=1,eta=(1,1,1,1). No testing/source variables
are identified in this definition; the contact quantities are coefficients.

2. The complete-parent conical limit.

The native weight m=mu/(1-p) diverges to positive infinity as p->1.
Its numerator mu=jp(1+y)(1+x)(1+Hy)/(2P) has a finite positive limit.
All other source weights remain finite: j-p>=1, P>=j+1, E>=1-B,
1-HT>0,H=1/2 and T=B=epsilon are fixed away from zero. The
contact-normalizing factors also have finite nonzero limits.

The m-event term of the FULL parent is
4m(vW-X+uvY-uZ)^2. Its normalized root divided by sqrt(y) is exactly

 L_jp=(1+Hjy)t*etaW+(j-p)etaX
          +jB(1-HT)st*etaY-j(1+pB)s*etaZ.

Every other complete-parent term has bounded coefficients. Therefore
coefficientwise in the fixed finite polynomial space,

           Fhat_jp/(4my) --> g_j=L_j^2,

 L_j=(1+Hjy)t*etaW+(j-1)etaX
          +jB(1-HT)st*etaY-j(1+B)s*etaZ.             (1)

This is a full-parent limit, not a scalar restriction or a discarded
normal calculation. The limiting root still vanishes at the normalized
contact: its four coefficients sum to zero since y=BT.

3. Why finitely many fixed positive templates cannot contain the family.

Suppose fixed PSD Gram polynomials P1,...,Pr generate a cone containing
every Fhat_jp. Their conic hull is closed. A direct proof uses a fixed
positive testing-box/state-sphere integral I: for every nonzero positive
template I(Pi)>0, so convergent coefficient vectors bound every positive
template coefficient. Subsequence limits retain a conic representation.
Thus this finite cone also contains every g_j in(1).

Evaluate all forms at the fixed normalized test s=t=1. Each template
becomes a fixed PSD4x4 state matrix Mi. The limit g_j becomes

                         ell_j*ell_j^t,

 ell_j=(1+Hjy, j-1, jB(1-HT), -j(1+B)).

These are infinitely many distinct rank-one rays. For j!=k the
minor formed by the W and Z coordinates is exactly

 ell_j,W*ell_k,Z-ell_k,W*ell_j,Z=(j-k)(1+B)!=0.       (2)

A sum of PSD matrices has rank one only when every positively used
nonzero summand has its range in that same one-dimensional space:
test every vector orthogonal to ell_j and use nonnegativity. Hence
each represented rank-one ray requires a nonzero fixed Mi of that
same ray. A finite list cannot cover all the distinct directions(2).
Templates whose evaluation is zero cannot supply the nonzero target.
This contradiction proves the obstruction.

Consequently there is no polyhedral cone of normalized coefficients
containing all these actual native targets whose finitely many extreme
generators each have a fixed PSD sixth-root Gram. Adding finitely many
polyhedral cases does not help: the union of their generator lists is
still finite. Stationarity plus coefficient-sign or circuit inequalities
cannot produce the proposed finite positive-generator cover of the
entire family without relaxing at least one of these requirements.

4. The precise limitation, and a useful surviving block.

The obstruction is to a POLYHEDRAL positive-template reduction, not to
the finite sixth-root certificate cone. In fact the limiting family
already has an extremely small fixed-function representation. Define
the two fixed corner-basis roots

 L0=t*etaW-etaX,
 L1=Hy*t*etaW+etaX+B(1-HT)st*etaY-(1+B)s*etaZ.

Both vanish at the normalized contact, and

       g_j=(L0+jL1)^2
          =(L0,L1)[[1,j],[j,j^2]](L0,L1)^t.

The coefficient matrix is PSD rank one for every j, but these matrices
cannot themselves be generated by finitely many fixed PSD matrices.
One variable2x2 PSD coefficient block handles them perfectly. All
functions here lie already in Z1, hence in Z6.

Thus a viable structural reduction must retain genuinely semidefinite
coefficient blocks (or permit additional source-dependent congruences
whose expansion returns such blocks), rather than only nonnegative
scalar combinations of finitely many fixed positive polynomials. This
does not provide the complete native Gram, and does not exclude a
polyhedral cover after restricting away from degenerating openings.

The small exact helper normalized_template_route_checks.py/.json checks
the native sign majorant, m-divergence identity, complete-parent m
coefficient, normalized limiting root, direction minor and fixed2x2
representation. It uses no optimization. The bounded route stops at
this concrete obstruction; no universal admission or global lift is claimed.
