C2 a -> 0 ENDPOINT: EXACT CONSTRAINTS AND SCOPE

Conclusion. The corner coefficient matrices, C2 relative determinants,
leading-face identity and product-state identity are correct. On C2,
the matrices really are principal submatrices of EVERY PSD Z_k Gram,
not merely one choice of coefficient allocation. This needs the beta=0
support-pruning argument below, which the corner script does not itself
prove. The stronger claim about a uniform margin AFTER removing the
moving contact kernel does not follow from these matrices alone.

1. Why the entries are fixed, at every positive integer k.

Write features u^(i/k)v^(j/k) psi_l, 0<=i,j<=k. For psi=(0,0,0,Z),
the exact diagonal state polynomial is
 F/Z^2=4alpha+(K+4beta)u^2+4Dv^2+4beta u^2v^2,
 K=4m+4alpha+2d.
On C2, beta=0. The Newton polygon is contained in
conv{(0,0),(2,0),(0,2)}. Therefore every Z-feature with i+j>k has a
zero row in ANY PSD Gram. Proof: factor G as a sum of squares and
restrict the state to Z. A monomial outside half this polygon would
give a noncancelling highest-weight square term in a separating
direction. Its coefficient vector must vanish; PSD then kills its
whole Gram row. This is a polynomial argument in u^(1/k),v^(1/k).
It does not assume a parity-block-diagonal Gram.

The diagonal corner monomials have unique pairs. For the YZ cross
monomial u^2 v YZ, both features have i=k. Pruning leaves only j=0
for the Z-feature, forcing j=k for Y. Likewise uv^2 XZ forces j=k
in both features, then i=0 for Z and i=k for X. Hence the literal
Gram blocks on (uZ,uvY) and (uvX,vZ), respectively, are
 B1=[[K,-K],[-K,K+4A]],
 B2=[[4D+2d+4n,-4D],[-4D,4D]].
The printed generic coefficient-extraction formulas include beta and
lambda, and are algebraically correct. However, beta>0 removes the
triangular Z-pruning, allowing other feature pairs to contribute to
these cross coefficients. Thus “fixed for every Gram” is proved here
on beta=0, particularly C2, not on arbitrary nine-coordinate sources.

For positive diagonal entries the relative determinants are exactly
 rho1=det(B1)/(B1_11 B1_22)=4A/(K+4A),
 rho2=det(B2)/(B2_11 B2_22)=(d+2n)/(2D+d+2n).
They are invariant under diagonal congruences of the two coordinates.
The diagonally normalized blocks have least eigenvalue
1-sqrt(1-rho_i). The identities are necessary constraints on a Gram,
not sufficient admission certificates.

2. Native limiting scope and the margin qualification.

Fix c,b,t,r with z=c^2, 0<r<1, b,t,c>0, and strict limiting gaps
 g0=(1+z)b-1+r>0, j0=1+zr-t>0, 1-zb>0.
Let Delta0 be the printed native Delta at a=0 and Ac=1+zr. Then
 A/a^2 -> A1=Ac^2(b+r)/(2b Delta0)>0,
 a^2D -> D0=(1+z)(b+r)b Ac/(2j0 Delta0)>0,
 n -> n0=((b+r)Ac^2-r Delta0)/(2r Delta0),
 m -> (1-r)Ac^2/(2g0 Delta0),
 alpha -> z^2(1+z)b(b+r)^2/(2(1-zb)Delta0).
Thus rho1,rho2 are positive constants times a^2 along actual native
paths with n>=0; n0>0 ensures that condition for sufficiently small a.
The three rational points P1--P3 have n0 and all limiting gaps strictly
positive, checked exactly. Their reported asymptotic coefficients are
consistent with these formulas. This is not a statement about every
simultaneous approach to all source walls. In the d=1 normalization
C,D diverge, so no finite full source exists at a=0. Projective
normalization produces additional zero coefficients, not only A=0.

CRITICAL QUALIFICATION: small rho bounds a principal block's
correlation eigenvalue, but does NOT alone bound the smallest positive
eigenvalue after deleting the full moving contact kernel. For example
 G_epsilon=I-ww^T/(w^Tw), w=(1,1,epsilon),
has nonzero eigenvalues exactly 1,1, while its first two-coordinate
block has determinant epsilon^2/(2+epsilon^2). Kernel projection can
account for the entire small direction. Consequently the wording
“no diagonal rescaling can make the reduced margin uniform” needs
an additional quantitative comparison to the contact kernel. It is
not established by check_a0_corners.py. The exact shear identities are
 K(uZ-uvY)^2+4A(uvY)^2,
 4D(uvX-vZ)^2+(2d+4n)(uvX)^2;
they show no sign obstruction, but do not complete the remaining Gram.

3. A stronger exact endpoint constraint to send to the native-C2 task.

On the u-projective face the FULL polynomial is
 [u^2]F=4C(1+v^2)W^2+4n(1+v^2)X^2
       +K(vY-Z)^2+4A(1+v^2)Y^2+(4D+2d)(W+vX)^2.
In particular, on W=X=0,Z=vY, its value is exactly
 4A(1+v^2)Y^2.
After Z-pruning, the face moment vectors of this curve span a fixed
(k+1)-dimensional subspace S_k: the k+1 Y-features and the Z-feature
are related by Z=vY (the latter repeats the highest Y monomial).
For EVERY certificate G, G restricted to S_k is O_k(A) in a fixed
unscaled basis. To see uniformity over G, choose k+1 fixed distinct
positive v-values. Their moment vectors form a basis of S_k. Their
quadratic values are known multiples of A; PSD Cauchy--Schwarz bounds
all cross terms, giving a constant depending only on that fixed basis.
At A=0, S_k lies in ker G exactly. For k=6 this proves seven forced
soft directions, without a numerical solver. It does not determine
the quotient margin after contact removal and source-dependent
normalization, nor all additional limit-kernel directions.

The independent exact product-state identity is
 F(W,vW,uW,uvW)=2W^2 Du Dv[2Au^2+2lambda Dv
                         +v^2(d+2n+2beta Du)].
It reduces on C2 to 2W^2 Du Dv[2Au^2+(d+2n)v^2].
This confirms nearly tight classical directions for bounded u,W and
v=O(a), but is not a uniform full-state or all-scale completion.

Recommended message: any proposed native-C2 construction should
respect the two forced corner matrices, the exact u-face curve and
its seven-dimensional O(A) Gram restriction, and the product identity.
It must handle the other state directions and contact-kernel projection
before claiming a strict uniform reduced Gram. These necessary
constraints neither refute nor prove finite-a Z6 admission or (ND).

4. Evidence that remains evidence.

The corner checker verifies algebraic identities and three exact
one-parameter asymptotics. It does not supply the transformed limit
form, a reproducible 57+4=61 kernel calculation, a rational feasible
Gram after those 61 reductions, or a six-grade finite-a Schur bound.
The reported margins 0.16,0.18,0.09 and graded feasibility are numerical
evidence, not certified exact endpoint admission. The last assertion
“every tested a>0 satisfies (ND)” also overstates what the document's
earlier unresolved/noise-limited solver cases establish; at most it
reports positive numerical margins in the cases actually resolved.
Strict reduced-Gram persistence, uniform native admission and the
candidate's global exactness remain unproved.
