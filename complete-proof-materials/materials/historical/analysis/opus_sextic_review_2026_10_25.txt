5. RELEVANCE OF THE USER'S NPA/EASY-REGION PROPOSAL.

The hybrid is mathematically viable in principle, but needs a fixed
finite exact statement on each easy region. If these are source regions,
one must prove that a single finite NPA level gives exact support
certificates throughout those regions and then show that those cones
together with the hard-region cones generate the whole polar. If these
are testing-angle regions, ordinary NPA constraints do not by themselves
encode the angle restriction; an exact restricted model/localizing
certificate and its projection must be supplied. Per-source convergence,
sampled tightness, or a level that varies without a uniform bound does
not give one finite lift. Simply intersecting two outer approximations
does not prove exactness without a reverse-inclusion argument.

Thus NPA can take over a genuinely uniform exact easy-region theorem,
and could reduce the amount of hard explicit construction. It does not
currently prove the corrected ND statement or remove the two closed
faces from the remaining source coverage obligation.

Strongest validated improvement: a concrete fixed196-function global
candidate with a soundness and closedness proof, plus supplied exact
individual boundary witnesses in fixed support-face reductions. The
unproved step is universal adequate admission in that particular fixed
family, with the continuation reduction needing the repairs above.

ADDENDUM: A BOUNDED STRUCTURAL REPAIR OF THE RANK OBLIGATION.

The parent proposed reducing the rank calculation to one normalized
contact. This is valid whenever all contact state coordinates are
nonzero. Put s=u^(1/6),t=v^(1/6). Positive contact-angle scaling and
nonzero diagonal state scaling send every basis monomial to itself
times its contact value. Thus Z(contact-scaled variables)=D_zc Z.
The invertible Gram congruence G'=D_zc G D_zc sends G zc=0 to G'1=0;
the target coefficient-space scaling is also invertible. Consequently
the reduced coefficient-map rank is constant along these scalings.
Negative nonzero state coordinates cause no difficulty: diagonal signs
are invertible real congruences.

At the normalized contact all reduced roots are z_i-z_ref within their
parity block. Products of two such roots are annihilated by the six
functionals ds,dt,dW,dX,dY,dZ at(1,1,1,1,1,1). Value is redundant by
state homogeneity. All four supplied pruned bases have412 coefficient
monomials and contain the six columns

       Y^2, s^2Y^2, t^2Y^2, t^12W^2, X^2, Z^2.

The six-gradient evaluation matrix on these columns has determinant
plus or minus64, so these annihilators are independent. Therefore the
reduced rank is at most406. An exact406 rank lower bound at the all-ones
contact, for example a nonzero406 minor modulo a prime, would prove
rank406 at every nonzero contact in that fixed support-face basis.
No heavy rank calculation was performed in this review.

The native formulas inspected have no state-scaling zero: C2
verify_boundary.py68 has W>0,X<0,Y=c*h/a>0,Z=1. For C1 line83,
