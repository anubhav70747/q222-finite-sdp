GENERIC ANSWER

Put
 A=a(d+2n)/2, b=A+n, P=C+d/2, Q=alpha+d/2,
 Gamma=a^2(b+P+Q)-b.
For a>0,a!=1, the full space of homogeneous quadratic identities has
dimension3, with the compact basis

              mP-DQ=0,
              mGamma-bQ=0,
              DGamma-bP=0.                           (F1)

These are exactly the2-by-2 minors of the source-linear matrix
                    [[m,D,b],[Q,P,Gamma]].
The first identity is the previously known D(alpha+d/2)=m(C+d/2);
the other two are genuinely additional once a is treated as a fixed
coefficient, rather than as another variable with constant coefficients.
The script checks all three against the complete polynomial coefficient
map and verifies their independence over Q(a).

After clearing the same positive source denominator as in the prior note,
the21 products of six source coordinates have75 distinct(r,z) coefficient

the quadratic identity space has dimension8. Write A=d/2+n and
                  L=m+D-d/2-2n.
A full basis consists of the six products L times each of
                  d,n,m,C,D,alpha
and the two equal-angle Schur identities

All eight are checked, independent, and exhaust the specialized nullspace.

LIMIT OF THE RESULT

The factored identities supply a useful rectangular rank-one structure,
not a symmetric PSD representation or a description of the convex hull.
Indeed, all three quadrics are unchanged along the source direction
 (delta d,delta n,delta m,delta C,delta D,delta alpha)
                =(1,-a/[2(a+1)],0,-1/2,0,-1/2),
which fixes b,P,Q,m,D. Additional information is needed to recover the
actual stationary surface or its closed conic hull along that direction.
No higher-degree elimination, moment construction, or full cone claim is
made in this bounded calculation.
