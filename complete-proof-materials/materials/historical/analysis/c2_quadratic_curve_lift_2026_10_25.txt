EXACT ORDER-THREE CONE FOR A CONTINUOUS C2 FAMILY
25 October 2026

Result and scope
----------------
The closed conic hull of the C2 source curve below has an exact rational
order-three PSD representation. Its dual has an exact rational order-three
representation with one scalar auxiliary variable. These statements concern
the cone generated by this entire continuous family, not the full canonical
validity cone or Q222. Adding its dual block to the fixed 1+AB relaxation
strictly sharpens that relaxation, using the separately established fact that
the interior curve sources have no nine-word SOS certificate.

1. The physical source curve and its endpoints
---------------------------------------------
Put b=2/5 and use source order (d,lambda,n,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta). For 0<z<=b set

 c(z)=(1,0,(2-5z)/(8(1+2z)),(2+z)/(8(1+2z)),
       (2+z)/(2(1+2z)),3(2+z)/(8(1+2z)),
       3(2-z)/(8(1+2z)),3z^2/(2(2-z)(1+2z)),0).

This is the native C2 specialization a=b_source=1, r=t_source=1/2,
c_source=sqrt(z). In the native source formulas,

 Ac=1+z/2, h=1/2, g=j_c=(1+z)/2,
 Delta=2(1+z)(1+2z), N=2(1+z).

All strict source-chart gaps are positive for 0<z<=2/5; in particular
1-zh=b_source-a^2 z r=1-z/2>0. The only additional coefficient face at z=b
is n=0. The contact has independent testing angles u=v=sqrt(z) and homogeneous
state (W,X,Y,Z)=(1,-sqrt(z)/2,sqrt(z)/2,1). It is not unit normalized.
The separate source parameter z is held fixed when taking testing/state
derivatives. The accompanying independent checker verifies the native
specialization, full parent value, and all six first derivatives exactly.
Stationarity alone is not a proof of validity.

Here is the global-validity implication of the separately derived determinant
identity in c2_curve_physical_validity_2026_10_25.py/.json. Let P_c(u,v) be the
matrix of the complete canonical homogeneous parent F_c in the order W,X,Y,Z,
and put D_u=1+u^2,D_v=1+v^2. For U=u^2,V=v^2>0,

 det(P_c)=16 D_u^2 D_v^2 u^2 v^2 L_z(U,V),

where, with X=U/z,Y=V/z,

 L_z(zX,zY)=a(X+1/X-2)+e(Y+1/Y-2)
            +f(X/Y+Y/X-2)+g(Y/X+X+1/Y-3),

 a=27z(z+2)^3/[16(2-z)(1+2z)^4],
 e=27z(z+2)^3/[16(1+2z)^4],
 f=81(z+2)^4(2-5z)/[256(2-z)(1+2z)^4],
 g=27z(z+1)(z+2)^3/[16(2-z)(1+2z)^4].

All four weights are nonnegative on 0<z<=b, and all brackets are
nonnegative by the two-term or three-term arithmetic-geometric mean
inequality. This proves det(P_c)>=0. To pass from determinant to the FULL
state form, its Z=0 principal block is positive definite when uv!=0:
the d part alone restricts to

 2[(uW+uvX-Y)^2+D_u v^2 Y^2+D_v X^2],

whose three linear forms are independent. All event additions are PSD.
Consequently Schur complementation of this 3x3 block makes det(P_c)>=0
equivalent to P_c>=0. Literal zero testing angles follow by continuity;
projective testing endpoints follow after division by D_u D_v. Thus the
identity certifies every state and every independent real/projective test,
not just the displayed contact. The z=0 source is the coefficient limit of
valid sources; z=b is covered literally or by the same continuity argument.
This paragraph uses the displayed determinant identity, whose exact expansion
is checked by the separate physical-validity script; the present checker does
not duplicate that determinant computation or the NPA exclusion computation.

Multiply by s(z)=8(2-z)(1+2z)>0. The exact polynomial curve is

 ctilde(z)=(16+24z-16z^2,0,4-12z+5z^2,4-z^2,
            16-4z^2,12-3z^2,12-12z+3z^2,12z^2,0).

Every coefficient is nonnegative on [0,b]. The scaled and unscaled curves
give the same nonzero source rays. Source validity is preserved under their
conic sums and limits.

2. Exact moment representation and closedness
--------------------------------------------
Let h=(h0,h1,h2) and define

 M(h)=[[h0,h1],[h1,h2]],    ell(h)=b*h1-h2.

The source cone is exactly

 K={T h : diag(M(h),ell(h))>=0},

where the nine rows of T are

 (16,24,-16), (0,0,0), (4,-12,5), (4,0,-1), (16,0,-4),
 (12,0,-3), (12,-12,3), (0,0,12), (0,0,0).

One direction is immediate from h=(1,z,z^2): M(h) is PSD and
ell(h)=z(b-z)>=0. Conversely the two constraints imply h1>=0.
If h2=0, PSD forces h1=0 and h=h0(1,0,0), h0>=0.
If h2>0 then h1>0; put t=h2/h1 in (0,b], w=h1^2/h2 and w0=h0-w>=0.
Exactly,

 h=w0(1,0,0)+w(1,t,t^2).

This proves admission by at most two actual atoms, with no limiting
approximation. In particular no extra source-sign blocks are needed.

T has rank three. Its inverse on its image is explicit:

 h2=alpha/12,
 h0=m/4+alpha/48,
 h1=(d-4m+alpha)/24.

Thus T maps this closed moment cone homeomorphically onto a closed cone.
It is exactly the closed conic hull of ctilde(z), 0<z<=b: z=0 is the only
additional curve endpoint, and is a limit from that interval. Equivalently,
the positive d coordinate on the compact curve uniformly controls atomic
mass. This is closedness of this particular moment image, not a general
claim that projected PSD cones are closed.

3. The exact dual block, with a direct quadratic proof
----------------------------------------------------
For a dual source-coordinate vector y in R^9 write (a0,a1,a2)=T^T y.
Then y is in K* precisely when p(z)=a0+a1*z+a2*z^2>=0 on [0,b]. This is
equivalent to the existence of a scalar tau>=0 such that

 H(a,tau)=[[a0,(a1-b*tau)/2],[(a1-b*tau)/2,a2+tau]]>=0.       (*)

Indeed (*) gives the identity

 p(z)=[1,z] H(a,tau) [1,z]^T + tau*z*(b-z).

For the converse let p>=0 on [0,b], and put tauB=(2*a0+b*a1)/b^2.
If tauB>=0, choose tau=tauB. Directly,

 H=a0*(1,-1/b)(1,-1/b)^T+p(b)*(0,1/b)(0,1/b)^T>=0.

If tauB<0 then a1<-2*a0/b<=0. Since p(b)>=0,
a2>=-(a0+b*a1)/b^2>-a1/(2b)>0. Hence the quadratic's vertex lies in
(0,b). Nonnegativity at the vertex gives 4*a0*a2-a1^2>=0, so tau=0
works. This proves all degenerate cases without invoking a degree theorem.

The order-three source moment cone has the strict point
h=(1,b/2,b^2/3)=(1,1/5,4/75): det(M)=1/75 and ell=2/75.
Therefore the elementary conic dualization also has the usual exact
attainment guarantee. Strictness is relative to this three-dimensional
source span; lambda=beta=0 throughout. The dual block is total PSD order
three (one 2x2 block and one scalar), not a statement that the full Q222 set
has a lift of that order. To constrain behavior coordinates, compose y with
the fixed source-to-affine-Bell-slack pairing already used for canonical
sources. No contact-dependent testing functions enter this construction.

4. Why the new block strictly sharpens fixed 1+AB
-----------------------------------------------
This implication uses the independently established nonmembership of each
source c(z), 0<z<=b, in the nine-word SOS image. It does not follow merely
from failed stationarity equations in some smaller subspace.

Use W=(I,A0,A1,B0,B1,A0B0,A0B1,A1B0,A1B1), with A_i^2=B_j^2=I and
Alice's operators commuting with Bob's. In the FULL span of products
w_i^*w_j, the coefficient of I in W^*QW is tr(Q): distinct listed words
cannot have product I in this universal algebra. For a convergent sequence
of SOS coefficient vectors their traces are bounded. PSD then bounds all
Gram entries, so a convergent PSD Gram subsequence proves the image closed.

A source outside this closed cone has a strict separating linear functional
L, with moment matrix [L(w_i^*w_j)]>=0 and L(f_c)<0. All its diagonal entries
are L(I). If L(I)=0, PSD forces the whole moment matrix to vanish; L would
then vanish on the product span containing f_c, a contradiction. Normalize
L(I)=1. Its nine Bell expectations define a feasible 1+AB behavior, including
event positivity because every event projector belongs to span(W) and is
idempotent. That behavior violates f_c, whereas the order-three K* block
enforces f_c>=0 for every curve source. All physical behaviors satisfy the
new block by Section 1. Hence its intersection with 1+AB is a strict, sound
improvement, without needing a numerical separating behavior.

This does not prove the intersection equals Q222, or cover every C2 source.
The construction is an exact continuous-family block that can be joined
with other independently sound blocks.
