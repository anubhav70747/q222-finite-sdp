2. A short exact lower-level consequence

Suppose the root space is restricted to linear observables (the five-word
NPA1 level). In C1 the available roots are h1,h4,h5. The affine equations
force
 alpha=A, C=beta+d/2, m=D+d/2,
 Q_lin=[[A,-tau,tau],[-tau,C,0],[tau,0,m]], tau=(2A+d)/4.
It is PSD exactly when A>=tau^2(1/C+1/m), since C,m>0. This supplies an
exact source subclass and a complete admission criterion in this restricted
root space. For example d=1,A=alpha=1/2,C=m=1,D=beta=1/2 admits it.

However no member has a nonzero interior two-qubit contact with distinct
Bob observables. Completing the square forces at any zero
 B0 psi=A1 psi+(tau/C)(A0-I)psi,
 B1 psi=A1 psi-(tau/m)(A0-I)psi.
Thus A1 psi is a strict convex combination of the unit vectors B0 psi
and B1 psi. Equality in the triangle inequality forces B0 psi=B1 psi.
For finite positive native v, B0-B1 is invertible on Bob's two-dimensional
block, a contradiction. A positive Schur residual only adds another square.

In C2 the linear root space is h1,h2,h5. The affine identity q17=
(2C+d)/8-q47 forces C=-d/2 when all bilinear-root rows vanish, impossible
for d,C>0. Hence NPA1 cannot admit any actual interior native C1/C2 contact.
This is a lower-level statement, not an exclusion from the nine-word cone.

3. A continuous ACTUAL C2 family excluded from the full nine-word cone

Let c>0, z=c^2<=2/5, d=1, lambda=beta=0, and
 n=(2-5z)/(8(1+2z)), m=(2+z)/(8(1+2z)),
 C=(2+z)/(2(1+2z)), A=3(2+z)/(8(1+2z)),
 D=3(2-z)/(8(1+2z)), alpha=3z^2/[2(2-z)(1+2z)].
All required native event weights are positive, and n>=0, including n=0
at z=2/5. In the SG2 Pauli convention
 A0=B0=Z, A1=((1-u^2)Z+2uX)/(1+u^2),
 B1=((1-v^2)Z+2vX)/(1+v^2),
the contact is u=v=c, psi=(1,-c/2,c/2,1).
Direct rational substitution gives f_c(A,B)psi=0 and both angular first
derivatives zero. The checker verifies these six identities. Consequently
SG7 ('Stationary zeros and physicality', 04_geometry.tex) proves global
validity from these equations and the nonnegative source weights. This is
an actual valid native source family, not merely a positive parameter box.

We now prove that none of these sources has a 1+AB certificate.
Put
 a=3(2-z)/[2(1+z)], b=-(z+4)/[2(1+z)],
 d0=-b, e=-(z+2)/[2(1+z)]. Then ae-b*d0=1.
For the seven C2 roots h, their actions on psi have rank4: columns
(h1,h2,h3,h5) have determinant -8c^4/(1+c^2). Their kernel has basis
 N1=(0,0,-1,1,0,0,0)^T,
 N2=(a,b,-a,0,0,1,0)^T,
 N3=(d0,e,-e,0,-1,0,1)^T.
At a zero, positivity forces Q T^T=0, where T contains the seven root
actions. Hence Q=N S N^T for a symmetric3-by-3 S, N=(N1,N2,N3).

Four homogeneous relations in the complete affine coefficient solution are
 q34=q67=0, q13=q57+q77, q23=-q56.
In S coordinates these imply
 S23=0, S11=-a*S12-e*S13,
 a(S12+a*S22)+d0(S13+e*S33)=0,
 b(S12+a*S22)+ e(S13+e*S33)=0.
Since ae-b*d0=1, it follows that
 S12=-a*S22, S13=-e*S33, S11=a^2*S22+e^2*S33.
Writing k=N^T h, the entire candidate polynomial is therefore
 S22(k2-a*k1)^2+S33(k3-e*k1)^2.
The two roots factor exactly as
 k2-a*k1=(I-B1)(a*A1-A0-b*I),
 k3-e*k1=(A1-I)(B0-e*B1+d0*I).
Their A0A1 and B0B1 universal-word coefficients are respectively
 -2a*S22 and -2e*S33; the other square cannot contribute to those words.
These words do not occur in a Bell functional, so both coefficients must
vanish. Since a>0 and e<0 throughout 0<z<=2/5, S22=S33=0. Hence Q=0,
contradicting d=1. In fact, there is no symmetric Gram satisfying both
the coefficient identities and this contact kernel; PSD is needed only
to infer that the Gram annihilates the contact.

A particularly small rational example is c=1/2:
 (d,lambda,n,m,C,A,D,alpha,beta)
   =(1,0,1/16,3/16,3/4,9/16,7/16,1/28,0).
It is globally quantum-valid by the six checked contact equations and SG7,
but is outside the fixed nine-word 1+AB certificate cone.

The short checker npa_seven_c2_family_2026_10_25.py verifies the source
equations, nonzero rank minor, null basis, determinant1, four homogeneous
