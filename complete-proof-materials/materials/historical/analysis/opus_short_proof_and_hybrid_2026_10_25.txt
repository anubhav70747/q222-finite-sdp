1. What PEN_AND_PAPER.md does and does not prove.

Part A is a clear modular implication [I1]--[I5] => finite lift. The
source/test mixture identity preserves the constant coefficient because
sum_i w_i=1, and the pure-testing factor DuDv||psi||^2 can legally be
cancelled. The final Tarski statement concerns existence of algebraic
entries at some fixed finite size; it does not evaluate that size or entries.

The indispensable import is [I5], exactly Proof.tex proposition
prop:insight-canonical (lines364ff). It quantifies over EVERY separately
labelled real closed source/test pair, every C1/C2 contact source and
EVERY independent projective test/state, including singular faces. Its
proof explicitly imports bounded-coefficient Chapter A, C1 Chapter B,
C2 Chapter C, Theorem6.2, and the complementary charts. Chapter07 then
assembles those same inputs. PEN_AND_PAPER.md itself acknowledges that
this is the approximately31,000-line companion theorem.

Thus this is not a new short, self-contained proof of the hard result.
It is a readable proof conditional on the imported theorem, with an
independent-review claim that the import has been audited. That claim
and the scripts are evidence, not a replacement proof inside the note.
The reconstructed R6 remote matrices/HI Gram mentioned in the review
README should be explicit, citable proof material when reproducibility
of [I5] is claimed. I have not re-certified those cases here.

The useful concise identity in Part B is
 det(Mplus)F=(P psi)^T adj(Mplus)(P psi)+det(P)s^2.
Its separated SOS right side certifies the MULTIPLIED parent. Cancelling
the mixed det(Mplus) is precisely the hard content still imported; it
does not follow from its pointwise positivity. This is the correct
place to state the remaining mathematical obligation in the narrative.

Important distinction: TF12/Corollary3.19 needs no separately supplied

instructions do not evaluate one fixed global testing library. Calling
the short note nonconstructive is accurate; calling this an evaluated
lift, or a new proof removing the long cases, would not be accurate.

2. A precise constructive hybrid criterion.

Let V=R^9 be affine Bell coefficients, z(q)=(1,q), and
 P={f: f.z(q)>=0 for every q in Q222}.
Fix ONE finite NPA level k, with compact projected outer set Q_k
containing Q222, and define N_k={f:f>=0 on Q_k}. Let H_1,...,H_s be
finitely many explicitly represented conic SDP certificate cones of
Bell coefficients. REQUIRE GLOBAL SOUNDNESS H_i subset P.

The exact coverage condition is

           P=closure(N_k+H_1+...+H_s).                (H)

Then the finite hybrid primal is
 Q_hyb=Q_k intersect {q:z(q) in H_i^* for all i},
and Q_hyb=Q222 by polarity. The closure is allowed in(H): taking a
dual already takes it. A stronger sufficient coverage proof is to put
every extreme source ray from the canonical classification in that sum,
including all finite relabelling orbits, permitted zero faces and limit
rays. Covering each ray by one component is sufficient but not necessary;
a decomposition into several components also suffices.

Uniformity requirements are finite k, finitely many H_i of fixed matrix
size, and exact admission over their entire claimed source domains.
Their free Gram entries may depend on the source. A fixed family can
include algebraic testing roots and fixed chart gates; an adaptive gate
threshold inside a testing root has not been counted as one fixed function.
If finitely many NPA levels are used, replace them by their maximum.
Neither hierarchy convergence nor a collection of individual sources
with finite certificates proves(H) at one level. A useful sufficient
easy-region condition would be a compact normalized source set with
uniform positivity margin gamma>0, together with a justified uniform
NPA approximation bound smaller than gamma divided by its coefficient
norm bound. No such evaluated k/coverage split is supplied here.

DUALITY REQUIREMENT FOR THE LITERAL PRIMAL PENCIL. If
 H_i={f:exists y, L_i(f,y)=sum f_a A_ia+sum y_b B_ib>=0},
then, with attained conic duality, its dual condition is
 exists Y_i>=0: <B_ib,Y_i>=0, <A_ia,Y_i>=z_a.
Full strict feasibility in the appropriate reduced coordinate/face
space is a sufficient regularity condition. A strict matrix>=epsilon I
bounds trace(Y_i) by f_*.z/epsilon and makes the adjoint image closed;
separation then gives the exact dual representation. A numerical dual
solve or unproved no-gap assertion is insufficient. Embedded closed
faces use their seven free source coordinates, not impossible strict
positivity of their two fixed-zero coordinates. For canonical-to-full
transport M_g, impose M_g^T z in the corresponding source-cone dual;
the existing eight-copy assembly supplies these exact maps.

TEST-CHART WARNING. A cone sound only on a cap is not an H_i subset P.
For fixed test charts covering ALL physical atoms, first intersect their
source-certificate cones using the same source and separate Gram variables.
Only that global source cone may be added in(H). The order293 R1 cone
currently certifies |u|<=9/256 OR |v|>=1/16; the remaining strip is open.
Adding its dual as an unrestricted inequality family could exclude real
quantum points. Intersecting that partial cone with N_k makes it sound
but adds nothing beyond N_k, since N_k+(partial intersect N_k)=N_k.

3. Closed faces can be added directly; L6 membership is unnecessary.

SG11--SG14, latex/chapters/04_geometry.tex (lines439--541), give
EXACT full-validity source cones for two representatives:
 F_a:lambda=m=0, one6x6 Gram with three skew auxiliaries(SG12);
 F_o:lambda=alpha=0, two3x3 blocks with one auxiliary xi(SG13).
Each has seven remaining nonnegative source coordinates. The printed
strict points make the whole reduced lift strictly feasible; SG14 proves
attained dual representation and separated trace certificates, including
singular coefficients. They may simply be added as known sound H_i.
The finite CHSH symmetry orbits give the twelve exclusive-pair faces:
eight distance-three pairs and four opposite pairs in the SG8 cycle.

Do SG12/SG13 already prove F_a,F_o subset L1 subset L6? NOT directly
from the displayed Gram identities. SG12 is a Gram for the limiting
univariate K(v); SG13 is a Gram for the rescaled limiting form G_s.
Their equivalence with full-parent validity is then proved by determinant
remainders and SG4, not by printing a full F SOS in Z1. SG14 duality
gives a finite testing certificate, but not necessarily that specified
polynomial feature space. No L1 inclusion was established in this pass.
It is unnecessary for the hybrid.

In particular let H=L6, or another globally sound closed candidate, and
add the finite source orbits of H and F_a,F_o. Events are already in L1.
If every C1/C2 contact representative is admitted to H, the classified
extreme rays are all covered by this sum, whether or not the closed
faces lie in H. No invariance of H's testing basis should be assumed:
finite source-permuted cones can instead be included explicitly. In
canonical nonnegative coordinates such a finite sum of closed cones is
closed: each summand is coordinatewise bounded by the total along any
convergent sequence. Hence here the classification proves exact equality,
not just dense coverage. This removes the closed-face-in-L6 obligation.

4. What this snapshot actually establishes.

Known exact inputs: events; the two closed-face SDP cones and their finite
symmetries; canonical/full coefficient maps; and fixed regional R1 matrices.
L6 has soundness, closedness, finite affine equations, events and four
exact contact certificates. Its nondegeneracy continuation hypothesis(ND)
is still a conjecture, not supplied by seeds, numerical sweeps or ordinary
closedness of L6. At c435bd1 the candidate note also leaves full strict
feasibility to be checked; a later exact witness can resolve regularity,
but cannot establish source coverage by itself.

The repo supplies no chosen finite NPA level and no proof of(H), either
as a complete easy/hard source partition or as conic generation. The
long theorem[I5], if valid, already gives qualitative existence through
Corollary3.19, but cannot be substituted for fixed hard cones when the
output requested is actual matrix entries.

Recommendation. Keep PEN_AND_PAPER as the modular qualitative proof,
explicitly dependent on the companion. For constructive work, remove
the separate closed-face L6 goal, retain those existing exact cones,
and target only the still-unproved contact-ray coverage in(H). State
the finite NPA level, hard source domains, global testing coverage and
exact dual regularity as separate mathematical claims. This makes a
successful hybrid sufficient without claiming L6 alone is canonical-exact.
