1. Full Bell operator at a real planar maximally entangled zero
-------------------------------------------------------------
Choose fixed local real bases taking the zero state to
Phi+=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2). All C2 contacts under discussion are real planar;
write Alice and Bob Bloch directions a_x,b_y in the X,Z plane in this fixed
gauge. The complete Bell slack is

 B=kappa I+u.(sigma tensor I)+v.(I tensor sigma)
                +sum_{i,j in {X,Z}} J_ij sigma_i tensor sigma_j,

where u=sum_x h_x a_x, v=sum_y j_y b_y, J=sum_xy T_xy a_x b_y^T.
The ordinary source coefficients h_x,j_y,T_xy remain the fixed Bell
coefficients; u,v,J depend on the testing directions. Direct action on Phi+
gives

 B Phi+=(kappa+tr J)Phi+ +(u_X+v_X)Psi+
                       +(u_Z+v_Z)Phi- +(J_ZX-J_XZ)Psi-.

Consequently B Phi+=0 is exactly

 kappa=-tr J,   v=-u,   J=J^T.                         (1)

In the orthonormal Bell basis (Phi+,-Phi-,Psi+,Psi-), the FULL operator is
zero on the first row/column, and its remaining block is

 K=2*[[-J_XX,-J_XZ,u_X],
      [-J_XZ,-J_ZZ,u_Z],
      [u_X,u_Z,-tr J]].                                (2)

This proves the proposed 3x3 identity, including its signs. At this fixed
measurement pair positivity for every state is exactly K>=0. Equivalently
-J>=0, u lies in range(-J), and u^T(-J)^+u<=-tr J. Equation (2) retains
the marginal/state coupling; keeping only the correlator block -J loses it.

2. One explicit necessary 5x5 matrix for state and angle perturbations
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Hold this local gauge fixed. Vary only the two independent measurement
angles theta=(alpha_1,beta_1), holding a_0,b_0 fixed. Other smooth local
angle coordinates give congruent conditions. For every globally valid
slack tight at this strategy, scalar angle stationarity is

 tr(M_1)=tr(M_2)=0,
 M_1=sum_y T_1y a'_1 b_y^T,
 M_2=sum_x T_x1 a_x (b'_1)^T,

where a'_1,b'_1 are the actual unit-circle tangents. Define q_1=h_1 a'_1,
q_2=j_1 b'_1 and the three-component real columns

 w_j=(-(q_j)_Z,(q_j)_X,(M_j)_ZX-(M_j)_XZ),  j=1,2.

These are exactly the components of Pi_perp (partial_j B)Phi+ in the basis
(-Phi-,Psi+,Psi-). Put W=[w_1,w_2] and

 C_11=-(1/2)sum_y T_1y a_1.b_y,
 C_22=-(1/2)sum_x T_x1 a_x.b_1,
 C_12=C_21=(1/2)T_11 a'_1.b'_1.

Then the precise joint second-order condition is

                    [[K,W],[W^T,C]]>=0.                (3)

Proof: use the normalized state path Phi+ +epsilon*z+O(epsilon^2), with
z orthogonal to Phi+, and theta=epsilon*t. Because B Phi+=0 and the scalar
first-angle derivatives vanish, the expectation through order epsilon^2 is

       z^T K z +2 z^T W t+t^T C t.

Nonnegativity for both signs of epsilon and all z,t gives (3). Imaginary
state tangents add their independent K quadratic form, since the planar
operator and the angle-action columns are real. They do not change (3).
This uses the complete state form, not a selected positive summand.

Equivalently (3) says K>=0, range(W) subset range(K), and
C-W^T K^+ W>=0. The range condition is essential on degenerate state faces.
All entries of (3) are SOURCE AFFINE when the contact directions and the
Bell basis are fixed. The inverse expression is only an equivalent ordinary
finite-dimensional Schur condition; it is not division by a mixed tensor
element or a source-independent SOS construction.

The angle parametrization by the circle also covers a half-angle chart's
projective endpoint locally. It gives local necessity only: no assertion
that the 5x5 condition proves positivity at distant measurement angles.

3. Native quadratic identity in ordinary Bell coefficients
---------------------------------------------------------
For lambda=beta=0 the canonical coefficient map is

 kappa=d+n+m+C+A+D+alpha,
 h_0=n-A-alpha, h_1=m-C-D,
 j_0=-n+C+A, j_1=-m+D-alpha,
 T_00=-d/2-n-A, T_01=d/2+alpha,
 T_10=-d/2-C,   T_11=-d/2-m-D.

Therefore the native maximally entangled identity

                  D(alpha+d/2)=m(C+d/2)                (4)

is precisely

       T_11(T_01+T_10)+T_01 h_1+T_10 j_1=0.           (5)

Indeed D*T_01+m*T_10 equals minus one half of the left-hand side of (5).
This is an exact algebraic translation, not an additional theorem that
(1), (3) or (5) alone suffices for global validity. It shows explicitly
that the source quadric balances correlator and marginal coefficients.

4. Exact scope and the remaining inference
-----------------------------------------
The current canonical positive native chart already has a separate generic
physicality proof: positive three-state block, nonnegative Laurent outer
determinant coefficients, and stationarity followed by balance/AM-GM. Thus
(2)-(3) provide a concise structural state/angle condition; they are not a
new independent universal admission proof for those native sources.

A fixed-contact affine matrix can constrain sources tight at THAT contact.
As the contact moves, its Bloch directions, Bell basis, and the coefficients
in W,C move. Treating that family as one fixed finite source cone would
still require elimination or a proved finite convex-hull representation.
The local Schur inverse does not provide that elimination. No such global
inference is made here, and the ordinary coefficient identity does not
remove the already established rank-four quadric obstruction to a single
source-linear rank-one 2x2 determinant representation.
