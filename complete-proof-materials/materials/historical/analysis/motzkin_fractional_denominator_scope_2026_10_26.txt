Motzkin scaling does not force a factor-three denominator
2026-10-26. Scope: the scalar limiting polynomial only; no new parent admission.

Let M(u,v)=u^4 v^2+u^2 v^4-3u^2 v^2+1. On u,v>=0 the exact identity is
 M=(u^2 v-u v^2)^2+2[(uv)^(3/2)-(uv)^(1/2)]^2+(uv-1)^2.
Indeed the three terms expand respectively to
 u^4v^2-2u^3v^3+u^2v^4,
 2u^3v^3-4u^2v^2+2uv,
 u^2v^2-2uv+1.
For M+a, a>=0, add the constant square (sqrt(a))^2. Thus the entire limiting interval a in [0,2] in NATIVE_MULTIPLIER_OBSTRUCTION.md, equation (5), already has a half-power SOS. A denominator divisible by three is NOT necessary for this scalar limiting problem.

In formal square-root coordinates u=x^2,v=y^2 this is the polynomial identity
 M(x^2,y^2)=(x^4y^2-x^2y^4)^2+2(x^3y^3-xy)^2+(x^2y^2-1)^2,
valid for all real x,y. Consequently k=2 works for this scalar problem, and every even grid, including k=4 and k=6, contains the same roots.

The k=3 grid also works, independently. Set A=u^(2/3)v^(1/3), B=u^(1/3)v^(2/3), C=1, with real cube roots. Then
 M=A^6+B^6+C^6-3A^2B^2C^2
  =1/2 sum_{q in {A,B,C}} sum_{(a,b) in {(A^2,B^2),(B^2,C^2),(C^2,A^2)}} [q(a-b)]^2.
The last formula follows by multiplying
 (a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca)=a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc.
Every root has exponents in (1/3)Z^2, each coordinate between zero and two. It is valid even for all real u,v, because real cube roots exist there. Equivalently, M(x^3,y^3) is a polynomial SOS for all real x,y. Hence both the half-power and third-power constructions fit the sixth-power grid; neither shows its necessity.

Domains must not be conflated. The displayed half-power roots are real on the positive quadrant (and its boundary), whereas they do not define real functions when uv<0. Their polynomial pullback is an identity on the full real (x,y)-plane after u=x^2,v=y^2. The cube-root identity defines real functions on the full real (u,v)-plane. None of these is an ordinary polynomial SOS of M(u,v): that remains impossible, even after adding a>=0, by the axis/degree argument in the cited note.

Relation to the actual state-linear feature space Z_k:
The scalar roots above require angular exponents up to TWO. The original parent feature space is Z_k={u^(i/k)v^(j/k) psi_l:0<=i,j<=k}; its unpulled-back exponents are at most ONE in each angle and each root is linear in the independent state. The Motzkin obstruction instead first substitutes the polynomial state psi_t(x,y) printed in NATIVE_MULTIPLIER_OBSTRUCTION.md and rescales u=-tx,v=ty. After the sign congruence, a pulled-back feature is a source scalar times x^(i/k)y^(j/k) times one fixed component of psi_t. Its possible leading terms and cancellations retain that common state-origin restriction. An arbitrary scalar SOS for the limiting M+a need not lift to this restricted pulled-back space, much less to an identity for all independent states.

Therefore this calculation defeats only a scalar argument demanding 3|k. It neither proves Z_2 admission nor contradicts the independently verified native C2 half-power exclusion. The latter concerns the full matrix/state quadratic and its restricted support/contact Gram, and is strictly stronger evidence than this scalar limit. The no-finite-NPA polynomial argument also remains intact: its pulled-back roots are ordinary polynomials, while these new scalar roots use fractional powers in the original angles.

Exact verification: direct expansion checked the square-root identity and the nine-square cube-root identity in SymPy; no optimization, parameter search, or numerical approximation was used.
