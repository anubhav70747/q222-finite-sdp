GLOBAL TENSOR CRITERION: CONDITIONAL IMPLICATION AUDIT
26 October 2026. Local native certificates are hypotheses throughout.

Verdict. The implication from the FULL universal tensor-SOS assertion to
some finite affine SDP lift is correct. It supplies neither local coverage
nor evaluated matrices nor a finite NPA level.

Primary source checked directly: Claus Scheiderer, arXiv:2004.04196v2,
https://arxiv.org/html/2004.04196v2, Corollary3.19 (HTML lines518--523),
Theorem3.10 (416--445), definition3.7 (392--405), Theorem2.10 (299--306).
For a closed convex semialgebraic Q, Corollary3.19 uses exactly
 forall real-closed K containing R, forall f in(P_Q)_K, forall q in Q_K:
 f0 tensor1+sum f_i tensor q_i belongs to Sigma(K tensor_R K)^2.
The paper writes point first; the factor flip is an algebra isomorphism
preserving squares. No uniform square count, testing library, matrix
order, or tensor-rank bound is required here. Theorem3.10 separately
characterizes a SPECIFIED extension-degree bound d through bounded
rank of each square tensor. The corollary's proof obtains a finite
variety/function space from universal SOS, using the preceding
algebraic/real-spectrum argument and Theorem2.10.

Quantifiers and field labels. Two separately labelled copies of the SAME
arbitrary K suffice. Proving the claim for arbitrary different source and
testing fields is stronger. Keep the tensor product over the actual real
base field. Do not multiply its two factors into K: ordinary f(q)>=0,
including all real evaluations, is not the required SOS assertion.
The finite nine-atom polynomial model for Q makes Q compact, convex and
semialgebraic over R. Its fixed first-order formula transfers to Q_K;
transferring the polar formula forall q in Q, f(q)>=0 identifies(P_Q)_K
with all affine K-functionals nonnegative on Q_K. Real compactness is
not being asserted in a non-Archimedean field.

Conditional atom assembly. Assume exhaustive finite source decomposition
and a tensor identity for EVERY required independent atom pair, including
literal boundaries and nonstandard relative rates. A homogeneous parent
identity F=(1 tensor h_t)[f,Phi] with h_t>0 permits division by its PURE
testing square root. Source normalization d=1 is restored by sqrt(d)
in the source factor. No mixed tensor denominator may be canceled.
For f=sum_j a_j f_j and q=sum_i w_i Phi_i, with coefficients in their
own factors, a_j,w_i>=0 and sum_i w_i=1, atom SOS h_ijk gives exactly
 [f,q]=sum_i,j,k[(sqrt(a_j) tensor sqrt(w_i))h_ijk]^2.
The testing weight sum is essential for the affine constant. Zero
weights are never inverted. The finite extreme-source decomposition
can transfer through its first-order midpoint/extremality formula;
that observation does not verify any local native lemma.

Both lift inclusions. The corollary supplies X,phi and finite-dimensional
U with Q contained in phi(X(R)) and phi*(P_Q) contained in Sigma U^2.
Enlarge U by1 and let V span1,phi*x_i and all u_i u_j for a fixed basis
of U. Impose on a linear functional L on V
 L(1)=1, L(phi*x_i)=q_i, [L(u_i u_j)]>=0.
All coordinate-ring relations inside V are fixed finite linear
relations, so this is one finite affine pencil, with equality constraints
eliminated or represented by scalar PSD blocks. If q belongs to Q,
evaluation at a preimage in X(R) is feasible. Conversely, any feasible L
is nonnegative on every phi*f=sum squares from U, hence f(q)>=0 for
every supporting affine f. Separation for closed convex Q gives q in Q.
No representing measure, moment completeness, or Slater assumption is
needed for this direct argument.

The X,U and pencil dimensions/entries remain existential. Rational
semialgebraic defining data for Q permit algebraic entries in principle
by first-order transfer after finite sizes are fixed; this evaluates
nothing and implies no finite NPA characterization.

Actionable scope. The recovered texts' global criterion and both
inclusions are sound conditional mathematics. Their claims that exactly
two named gates remain, or that routing is exhaustive, are not established
by this audit and must not become unconditional premises. The required
hypothesis is complete separated atom SOS over every real-closed K,
not an inventory, real sampling, determinant positivity, or a bounded
compact-native certificate alone. No public file was changed.
