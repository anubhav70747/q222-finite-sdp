\begin{abstract}
This release reconstructs the analytical extraction refinement from retained
sources and newly generated exact data. It delivers an evaluated local
complete-parent regular-C2 certificate, an exact affine pencil for the
tilted--CHSH two-coordinate projection, the displayed $B_0$ coefficient
module, restricted contact data, and the general local constructions used
to form fixed features. It also proves a precise limitation of literal
Schur-remainder extraction and specifies a conditional exact algebraic
search for a full lift. The global feature list, full eight-coordinate
affine pencil, and both global inclusions remain open. The accompanying
archive contains the complete sources and replayable coefficient checks,
as well as the earlier continuation archive unchanged.
\end{abstract}

\section{Outcome, scope, and source custody}
\label{sec:release-scope}

Write the original quantum coordinates as
\[
 q=(a_0,a_1,b_0,b_1,E_{00},E_{01},E_{10},E_{11})\in\R^8.
\]
The purpose of exact finite-lift extraction is to evaluate finite integers
$N,m$ and all real symmetric matrices in
\begin{equation}
 \Q=\left\{q\in\R^8:\exists y\in\R^m,
 A_0+\sum_{\nu=1}^{8}q_\nu A_\nu+\sum_{j=1}^{m}y_jB_j\succeq0\right\},
 \label{eq:global-target}
\end{equation}
and to prove both inclusions for those same coefficients. Equation
\eqref{eq:global-target} is the target of the open global extraction items,
not a newly evaluated representation delivered by this report.

The regenerated results have the following precise domains.

The regular-C2 certificate keeps the complete polynomial parent and
separates source and testing variables. Its local feature list and product
map are fully evaluated. The tilted construction additionally supplies an
explicit matrix pencil and a proof of both inclusions for its projection.
A rational nonquantum behavior shows why this family, even after
relabelings and probability constraints, does not describe the full set.
The global finite-cover theorem states exactly what further data would
turn sufficiently many restored local certificates into
\eqref{eq:global-target}.

has been overwritten.
