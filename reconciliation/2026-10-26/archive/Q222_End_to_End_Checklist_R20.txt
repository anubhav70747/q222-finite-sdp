  f^tensor(q) = f0 tensor 1 + sum_i f_i tensor q_i.
It must be a finite sum of squares in R tensor_Rreal R, with the two factors retained separately. The source/test formulation with independent K_c,K_t must imply this same-extension tensor statement; ordinary multiplication f(q)>=0 is insufficient.

[OPEN, U1] Establish this statement for EVERY such field, functional and point, using the source/test reductions and all terminal certificates below. This universal statement, not a finite list of numerical tests, is the input to the existence bridge.

Bridge source: Claus Scheiderer, Second-order cone representation for convex sets in the plane, arXiv:2004.04196v2, Corollary 3.19, printed page 15.
