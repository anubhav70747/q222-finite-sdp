## 2. A small exact obstruction to affine overlap linearization

Ramanathan, *No-Disturbance-without-Uncertainty generates the quantum set in
the simplest Bell scenario* (arXiv:2610.08749), gives
`Q222 = conv(A_B)`, where `A_B` is the set of no-signalling behaviors
sharing one Bob overlap `c` for all Alice conditional ensembles. For fixed
`c` this is a collection of second-order cones, equivalently four 3-by-3
positive semidefinite matrices.

Write the Bell behavior in expectation coordinates and let

```
t_{a|x} = 1 + a A_x
p_{a|x} = B_0 + a E_{x0}
q_{a|x} = B_1 + a E_{x1}
```

for `a=+1,-1`, `x=0,1`. The common-overlap matrices are

```
                   [ t   p   q  ]
G_{a|x}(c)   =     [ p   t   ct ]  >= 0.
                   [ q   ct  t  ]
```

The seemingly natural affine relaxation introduces four free entries
`z_{a|x}` in place of `c t_{a|x}` and retains only

```
z_{+|0} + z_{-|0} = z_{+|1} + z_{-|1} = 2c.
```

**Proposition (exact rational separating point).** The preceding affine
relaxation strictly contains `Q222`.

**Proof.** Consider

```
(A_0,A_1)                     = (3/5, 0)
(B_0,B_1)                     = (1/5, -1/5)
(E_00,E_01,E_10,E_11)        = (3/5, -3/5, 7/10, 7/10)
c                              = 1/5
(z_{+|0},z_{-|0},z_{+|1},z_{-|1}) = (4/5,-2/5,1/5,1/5).
```

The four affine matrices, ordered by `(x,a)=(0,+),(0,-),(1,+),(1,-)`,
are

```
G_0+ = (1/5) [ 8   4  -4 ]    G_0- = (2/5) [ 1 -1  1 ]
             [ 4   8   4 ]                   [-1  1 -1 ]
             [-4   4   8 ]                   [ 1 -1  1 ]

G_1+ =       [ 1  9/10 1/2 ]    G_1- =       [ 1 -1/2 -9/10 ]
              [9/10 1   1/5 ]                   [-1/2 1  1/5 ]
              [ 1/2 1/5 1   ]                   [-9/10 1/5 1  ].
```

All diagonals are positive. Their triples of order-two principal
minors, followed by their determinants, are exactly

```
G_0+: (48/25, 48/25, 48/25; 0)
G_0-: (0, 0, 0; 0)
G_1+: (19/100, 3/4, 24/25; 2/25)
G_1-: (3/4, 19/100, 24/25; 2/25).
```

Thus all four matrices are PSD, and their overlap-sum constraint
holds. But the tilted-CHSH expression (with Bob's setting-1 outcome
relabeled from the standard convention) is

```
I_1 = A_0 + E_00 - E_01 + E_10 + E_11 = 16/5,
I_1^2 - 10 = 6/25 > 0.
```

The exact quantum maximum at tilt one is `sqrt(10)` (the
tilted-CHSH formula `sqrt(8+2 alpha^2)`, `0<=alpha<=2`). Hence this
relaxed point is nonquantum. QED.

**Physical interpretation.** The normalized effective Bob overlaps
`z_{a|x}/t_{a|x}` are `1/2,-1,1/5,1/5`. They have the same average
over Alice outcomes at both settings, but they do not arise from the
simple common-`c` construction. Preserving the average anticommutator
is strictly weaker than preserving the hidden joint overlap structure
under Alice's steering. The counterexample rules out precisely this
four-block affine relaxation, *not* all polynomial or matrix-moment
extensions of the common-overlap formulation.

## 3. A one-page geometric reduction for a human proof

Fix one of the eight PR vertices. The eight CHSH-losing events are
ordered as the cycle

```
(lambda, D, C, A, alpha, m, beta, n).
```

Two such event assignments can vanish simultaneously at a deterministic
behavior precisely when their cyclic distance is one or two. Thus their
compatibility graph is the **square of the 8-cycle**, `C_8^2`.
Its cliques are exactly

```
empty:                1
single event:         8
compatible pairs:    16
compatible triples:   8 (three consecutive cycle vertices).
total:               33.
```

SG5–SG6 show that these, and only these, omission patterns leave
the angular Laurent support coercive. In a coercive extreme source,
physicality forces a stationary contact. At the contact there are
four state-kernel and two angular stationarity equations, all
homogeneous and linear in the Bell coefficients. Thus seven or more
positive event coefficients would leave a nontrivial normalized
two-sided perturbation: **at most six positive events**. The graph
gives **at least five**. Consequently there are two or three
omissions; up to the dihedral CHSH symmetries the two-omission
possibilities have cyclic distance one or two, yielding precisely
the C1 and C2 classes, and the three-omission cases are boundary
specializations.

This is the physical core of the source classification: CHSH frustration
and deterministic compatibility, followed by contact rank. It does not
replace the required uniform state-normal certificates.

At a noncommuting contact, the determinant is a balanced eight-term
Laurent polynomial: `sum_e w_e e = 0`, `w_e >=0`. Its positive
dependencies are generated by twelve elementary circuits (four
opposite pairs, four 1-1-1 triples, four 1-1-2 triples). The
weighted AM–GM identities prove global scalar angular positivity.
The state polynomial still has three transverse positive directions,
and its two complete state normals are the genuine difficulty:
division by a source-and-test-dependent Schur determinant is not
legal in `K_c tensor_R K_t`.

## 4. Explicit-lift frontier

- The tilted projection has an evaluated exact order-4 representation.
- The order-133 expression is a tensor-SOS for **`(det H_3) F`** with
  quartic source features; the mixed determinant cannot be canceled
  in the separated tensor ring to obtain a certificate for `F`.
- The evaluated order-293 regional R1 lift covers the stationary source
  box `0<B,jB,T<=2^-10`, `0<p<min(j,1)`, `0<H<1`, `n>=0`, with no
  additional constraint on `j` or `jT`, on the testing union
  `|u|<=9/256` **or** `|v|>=1/16`. It leaves
  `|u|>9/256`, `|v|<1/16`, and other canonical source families.
- Global outer pencils of orders 8,368 and 10,736 are strictly larger
  than `Q222`; the repository supplies exact separators. Larger
  outer pencils have unproved reverse inclusions.
- The exact total positive-semidefinite order lower bound is nine.
  This bounds the **sum** of PSD block orders, not each block separately.

   `H->0/1`, literal `v=0` and Alice infinity.
4. **Other canonical source families.** Obtain fixed source-independent
   finite testing lists for C1, remaining C2 and the closed faces;
   assemble them with the eight PR-sector relabelings.
5. **Reverse inclusion.** For each proposed global pencil, prove
   `projection(pencil) subset Q222`, not merely inclusion of quantum
   behaviors. Rational separation is an effective falsification test,
   not a replacement for a universal reverse-inclusion proof.
6. **Alternate overlap route.** For fixed `c`, use four matrix cones
   `G_{a|x}(c)>=0`. Search for a *uniform-degree*, univariate
   polynomial-matrix dual certificate valid across `c in [-1,1]`,
   including the singular endpoints and Bell-contact degeneracies.
   Matrix Fejer–Riesz/interval SOS could then yield a finite LMI;
   no uniform degree or exact dual selector is proved here. The
   preceding witness rules out the degree-zero marginal-coupling
   relaxation.

**Overall conclusion.** The archived algebraic checks support several
